Defense Mechanics

Parry

Skill: Weapon or Shield skill

Effect: Weapon/shield absorbs damage up to its AP. Excess hits character minus armor.

Penalty: -20% per additional parry vs same attacker per round

Dodge

Skill: Dodge + DEX bonus + INT bonus - ENC penalty

Effect: Full evade on success. Full damage on failure, minus armor.

Aimed Attack

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Choose specific hit location before rolling

Disarm

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Damage targets opponent's weapon HP instead of body

Strike Rank Calculation

Base Formula: SIZ Modifier + DEX Modifier (from Strike Rank Modifier Table)

Resolution: Lower SR acts first in combat. Encumbrance penalty adds to strike rank.

Encumbrance Calculation

Total Weight: Sum of all equipment, weapons, and shields

Max Capacity: STR stat

Penalties: For each point over capacity:

  • Strike Rank increases by +1
  • Movement Rate decreases by 1 (minimum 0)
  • Dodge skill penalty: -5% per point over capacity

Example: STR 10, Total ENC 12 = 2 over. SR +2, Movement -2, Dodge -10%.

Healing Rate

Formula: CON / 6 (rounded up, minimum 1)

Recovery: 1 hit point per week per healing rate
