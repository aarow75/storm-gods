Defense Mechanics

Parry

Skill: Weapon or Shield skill

Effect: Weapon/shield absorbs damage up to its AP. Excess hits character minus armor.

Penalty: -20% per additional parry vs same attacker per round

Dodge

Skill: Dodge + DEX bonus + INT bonus - ENC penalty

Effect: Full evade on success. Full damage on failure, minus armor.

Aimed Attack

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Choose specific hit location before rolling

Disarm

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Damage targets opponent's weapon HP instead of body

Strike Rank Calculation

Base Formula: SIZ Modifier + DEX Modifier (from Strike Rank Modifier Table)

Modifiers: Weapon modifier, Movement cost, Surprise penalty, Encumbrance penalty

Resolution: Lower SR acts first in combat. Cumulative modifiers add to strike rank.

Phases of the Melee Round

A melee round consists of the following phases:

Phase 1: Declare Actions & Modifiers

All combatants declare what they intend to do: attack, defend, move, cast spell, etc. Declare any special actions (aimed attack, disarm, etc.) and modifiers.

Phase 2: Calculate Strike Ranks

Each combatant calculates their Strike Rank for this round based on their declared action. Consider: base SR, weapon modifier, movement cost, surprise penalties, encumbrance penalties, and any other situational modifiers.

Phase 3: Act by Strike Rank

Starting with the lowest Strike Rank number, combatants act in order. Each participant performs their declared action: attack, dodge, parry, cast spell, etc. Upon acting, recalculate remaining participants' Strike Ranks if needed.

Phase 4: Resolve Attacks & Defenses

For each attack, determine if it hits (roll vs. weapon skill), determine hit location, roll damage, and apply defenses (armor, parry, dodge). Record damage to the appropriate hit location.

Phase 5: Apply Conditions

Apply any status changes from the round: injuries, incapacitation, death, spell effects, etc. Check for unconsciousness, limb loss, or other consequences of damage.

Phase 6: Round End & Reset

Clear surprise status if this is round 1. Reset for next round. All combatants recover one action if they acted. Determine if anyone is still combat-capable; end combat if only one side remains.

Key Points: Combat is simultaneous at the same SR. Multiple actions at the same SR are resolved at the same time. After everyone has acted, a new round begins.

Movement in Combat

Movement Allowance: Before attacking, a participant may move up to their movement rate in meters

Cost: Every 3 meters of movement costs +1 Strike Rank

Effect: Movement delays a participant's action in the round, possibly preventing them from acting if their modified SR exceeds remaining combatants.

Distance Tracking: Combat tracker tracks distance to opponent in meters for missile/ranged combat.

Surprise Mechanics

When Surprised: A participant who is caught off-guard takes a distance-based Strike Rank penalty

Penalty by Distance: +3 SR if opponent within 3m, +1 SR if opponent beyond 3m

Duration: Surprise effect applies only to the first round of combat. When New Round is clicked, surprise clears.

Surprise at Close Range

Warrior A: Base SR 5, moves 3m (+1) = Final SR 6

Warrior B: Base SR 4, surprised at 2m (+3) = Final SR 7

Result: Warrior A acts first (SR 6 before 7)

Surprise at Range

Warrior C: Base SR 5, surprised at 6m (+1), moves 3m (+1) = Final SR 7

Warrior D: Base SR 4 = Final SR 4

Result: Warrior D acts before C due to lower SR

Encumbrance Calculation

Total Weight: Sum of all equipment, weapons, and shields

Max Capacity: STR stat

Penalties: For each point over capacity:

  • Strike Rank increases by +1
  • Movement Rate decreases by 1 (minimum 0)
  • Dodge skill penalty: -5% per point over capacity

Example: STR 10, Total ENC 12 = 2 over. SR +2, Movement -2, Dodge -10%.

Healing Rate

Formula: CON / 6 (rounded up, minimum 1)

Recovery: 1 hit point per week per healing rate
