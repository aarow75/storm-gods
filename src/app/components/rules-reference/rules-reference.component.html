Defense Mechanics

Parry

Skill: Weapon or Shield skill

Effect: Weapon/shield absorbs damage up to its AP. Excess hits character minus armor.

Penalty: -20% per additional parry vs same attacker per round

Dodge

Skill: Dodge + DEX bonus + INT bonus - ENC penalty

Effect: Full evade on success. Full damage on failure, minus armor.

Aimed Attack

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Choose specific hit location before rolling

Disarm

Penalty: -20% to hit

Effect: Damage targets opponent's weapon HP instead of body

Strike Rank Calculation

Base Formula: SIZ Modifier + DEX Modifier (from Strike Rank Modifier Table)

Modifiers: Weapon modifier, Movement cost, Surprise penalty, Encumbrance penalty

Resolution: Lower SR acts first in combat. Cumulative modifiers add to strike rank.

Movement in Combat

Movement Allowance: Before attacking, a participant may move up to their movement rate in meters

Cost: Every 3 meters of movement costs +1 Strike Rank

Distance Moved SR Cost Distance Moved SR Cost
1-3m+1 SR7-9m+3 SR

Effect: Movement delays a participant's action in the round, possibly preventing them from acting if their modified SR exceeds remaining combatants.

Distance Tracking: Combat tracker tracks distance to opponent in meters for missile/ranged combat.

Surprise Mechanics

When Surprised: A participant who is caught off-guard takes a +12 Strike Rank penalty

Duration: Surprise effect applies only to the first round of combat

Effect: +12 SR penalty typically prevents surprised participant from acting in round 1. When New Round is clicked, surprise clears.

Surprise Example

Warrior A: Base SR 5, moves 3m (+1) = Final SR 6

Warrior B: Base SR 4, surprised (+12) = Final SR 16

Result: Warrior A acts first (SR 6 before 16)

Surprise + Movement

Warrior C: Base SR 5, surprised (+12), moves 6m (+2) = Final SR 19

Result: Heavily delayed; very unlikely to act in round 1

Encumbrance Calculation

Total Weight: Sum of all equipment, weapons, and shields

Max Capacity: STR stat

Penalties: For each point over capacity:

  • Strike Rank increases by +1
  • Movement Rate decreases by 1 (minimum 0)
  • Dodge skill penalty: -5% per point over capacity

Example: STR 10, Total ENC 12 = 2 over. SR +2, Movement -2, Dodge -10%.

Healing Rate

Formula: CON / 6 (rounded up, minimum 1)

Recovery: 1 hit point per week per healing rate
