Combat Flow

Initiative & Turn Structure

  • Each participant draws an initiative card (1–10). Lower acts first.
  • On surprise: choose any initiative card value.
  • Each turn: 1 Action + 1 Fast Action (or 2 Fast Actions)

Actions

  • Attack (melee or ranged)
  • Cast a spell
  • Use a skill
  • Retreat (EVADE to avoid free attack)
  • Grapple
  • Help an ally (+boon to their next roll)
  • Dash (move an extra full movement)

Fast Actions

  • Move (up to movement value in meters)
  • Draw or sheathe weapon
  • Open a door
  • Pick up item
  • Stand up from prone
  • Drink a potion
  • Reload a crossbow

Reactions (outside your turn)

  • Parry: Roll weapon skill to negate damage. Dragon = free counterattack; Demon = weapon takes Durability hit.
  • Dodge (EVADE): Roll EVADE to avoid hit entirely. Demon = fall prone.
  • Parry or dodge — not both — against a single attack.

Terrain & Visibility

  • Cramped space: Bane on two-handed/long weapon attacks
  • Rough ground: Bane on AGL rolls while moving
  • Dim light: Bane on attacks and AWARENESS
  • Darkness: No ranged attacks; bane on all sight rolls
  • Prone: Melee attackers get boon; ranged attackers get bane
  • Mounted: Boon on attacks vs. unmounted; bane on attack without RIDING skill
  • Sneak attack: Boon on attack; target cannot parry or dodge

Special Attacks

Grapple

Opposed STR vs. target STR or AGL (target's choice). Success: both grappled — neither moves or attacks normally; only knives usable. Each round: opposed STR to maintain or escape.

Disarm

Opposed weapon skill roll. Success: target drops weapon (lands D3m away, random direction).

Topple

Opposed STR roll instead of damage. Success: target falls prone and loses next fast action to stand.

Shove

Opposed STR roll. Success: push target back D3m. If they hit a wall: D3 damage + prone.

Find Weak Spot

Fast action. AWARENESS roll. Success: next attack this round ignores target's AR.

To-Hit Roll

  • Roll d20 + modifiers
  • Hit if result ≥ THAC0 − target AC
  • Lower AC is better (AC 10 = unarmored; AC 2 = plate)
  • DEX AC modifier subtracts from AC (high DEX = lower AC)

  1. Surprise — d6 each side; 1–2 = surprised (that many segments)
  2. Declare Actions — spells must be named before initiative
  3. Initiative — d6 each side; lower roll acts first
  4. Winner's side acts
  5. Loser's side acts
  6. Repeat; damage from tied initiative is simultaneous

Spellcasting

  • Spell casting time in segments (6 seconds each)
  • Caster's segment + casting time = when spell fires
  • Any damage taken during casting loses the spell
  • Caster loses DEX AC bonus while casting
  • 0 HP → unconscious; loses 1 HP/round; dead at −10 HP
