## STATS

Stats are determined by putting 4 points into the 5 stats:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | **STR** | Use for melee and other physical might rolls |
| Toughness | **TOU** | Use for starting HP, death chart rolls, and feats of endurance and resistance |
| Agility | **AGI** | Use for missile attacks, dodging, initiative and general dexterity moves |
| Intelligence | **INT** | Use for spells, perception, and other "smart" stuff |
| Presence | **PRE** | Use for reaction checks, charisma, lying, intimidation, and other social checks |

None may be higher than +2 at the start of gameplay.

Additionally, one stat may optionally be put at a -1 to gain an extra point to spend elsewhere.

## CHARACTER IMPROVEMENT

Leveling Up is based on XP. Each level requires that many XP to attain it, and characters are given 1XP per "meaningful" session — one during which the character faced challenges, explored, or otherwise interacted with the gameworld.

Bonus XP can be awarded for significant events: completing a quest, surviving especially difficult combat, exploring a new area or surviving a dungeon, attaining a major story landmark — no more than 2 bonus XP per session, with the exception of major multi-session accomplishments.

Leveling up provides the following benefits:

*Players must select* only one *of these two advancements:*

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Roll 6d6 vs. current HP | Rolling higher grants d6 HP. Rolling equal or lower grants 1 HP. |
| Put 1 point into any stat | To a maximum of +5 |

Additionally:

- At 1st level, players choose one skill automatically.
- At 3rd, 5th, and 9th level (max attainable), they may select from the skill list again, forfeiting their other advancement.
- Skills stack if taken more than once. Skills marked with an \* may only be taken once.

## RESOLUTION

All **Resolution** is 2d6+Stat. 8+ is a standard success. When doing very difficult things, roll at disadvantage. Very easy things shouldn't require a roll, unless under pressure, in which case you can roll at advantage.

**Two 6's is always a critical success, and two 1's is always a critical failure.**

| Stat Bonus | Success Chance |
|-----------|---------------|
| 0 | 41% |
| +1 | ~60% |
| +2 | 72% |
| +3 | 83% |
| +5 | 97% (only fails on natural 2) |

All characters receive one **Fate Point**, which can be used *once per session* to re-roll any one of their own dice rolls.

**HP** are determined at character generation by rolling a d6 and adding TOU. This is the only time TOU is added to a HP roll.

*OPTIONAL: take max HP at character generation for higher survival rate.*

## COMBAT

Players start a combat by rolling d6+AGI for **Initiative**. On a 4+ they go first, otherwise enemies do. Natural 1 always loses initiative. In group combat, roll one initiative per side (all members of a side act together), or activate one unit per side at a time — your choice.

In a round, a character can move and attack, drink something, throw something, or anything reasonably done in a few seconds.

**Attacking and Defending** is done in a "player facing" method:
- 2d6+STR vs 8 to succeed with a **melee** or **brawling/grappling** attack
- Use AGI for **missile** attacks
- Use INT for **magical** attacks/spellcasting

Success means rolling weapon **damage**, subtracting any modifiers for **armor** or other equipment, and dealing that much damage to the opponent's HP.

- Critical success doubles damage dice.
- Critical failure means some setback — a broken bowstring, a lost weapon, a fall to prone, etc.

Brawling attacks deal d6/d (roll 2 take the worst) damage.

Grappling means the opponent cannot attack with anything other than Light weapons or brawling attacks. If grappled or grappling, the player must roll STR check to break free or maintain the hold each round.

On the opponent's turn, the player rolls 2d6+AGI to **dodge**. Failure means taking damage the same way. Critical failure means the enemy doubles their damage dice. Critical success with some weapons can result in a bonus action — see Weapons.

If 2 players are fighting each other, use opposed Dodge/Attack rolls.

*OPTIONAL: Modify rolls by one for every level difference between opponents. Ex: A L4 and L2 character fight — the L4 adds +2 to their attack and Dodge rolls.*

**Damage dice explode.** 6's are rerolled *only once*, and the additional number added to the total. If a second 6 is rolled, it is counted "as is." (Max single d6 damage: 12. Critical doubles dice before exploding.)

Reaching 0 HP prompts a roll on the **Death and Wounding Table**. Rolls are made at a cumulative -2 for each time an individual at 0 HP takes damage. Anything below an adjusted roll of 7–8 means the character is out of the fight but will still recover HP at battle's end.

### DEATH AND WOUNDING

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 2 | Instant Death |
| 3 | Fatal Wound. Die in d6 rounds. |
| 4 | Severed or destroyed limb. Die in 2d6 rounds without immediate healing. -2 to AGI permanently. D6 weeks to heal past 50% HP unless magically healed. |
| 5 | Shattered. Roll d6: **1** — Face -1 PRE **2** — Leg -1 AGI **3** — Arm -1 STR **4–5** — Core -1 TOU **6** — Head -1 INT. D6 days to heal past ½ HP unless magically healed. |
| 6 | Broken, Slashed, or Pierced. -1 HP permanently. D6 days to heal unless magically healed. |
| 7–8 | Unconscious 2d6 rounds. If wearing a helm, stunned 1 round. |
| 9 | Stunned 1 round. With helm, knocked down. |
| 10 | Knocked down. Standing takes a round's action. Attack and dodge at disadvantage while prone. |
| 11 | Reeling but still in it. |
| 12 | Adrenaline surge. Immediately gain d6 HP. At end of combat, black out for d6 rounds. |

All injuries stack. If permanent HP loss would reduce a character below 1 HP, the character is dead.

**Healing** occurs at the end of battle — characters immediately gain back 1+TOU (min 1) HP. Characters also recover d6+TOU with a full rest. Night-time encounters and interruptions cancel this healing, as does a lack of rations/supplies.

HP represents not just physical damage, but stress, injury, blood loss, and decreasing luck as survival chances drop toward zero.

### MORALE

Any time an enemy is:
- Reduced in total number by 50%
- Takes damage equal to 50% or more of its HP in one hit
- Loses their leader

Roll 2d6 — higher than their morale rating flees or surrenders; equal or lower continues fighting.

### OPTIONAL COMBAT RULES (Book 3)

These rules add tactical depth. Recommended for pit fights and important duels; use standard rules for normal combat.

**Optional Weapons Rule:** Special effects that normally trigger only on a critical now trigger on a critical OR a damage roll of 6. Choose to either roll the extra damage die OR use the weapon effect.

#### MODIFIED ATTACK/DODGE DIFFICULTY

When fighting statted opponents, modify difficulty by their stats: Attack difficulty = 8 + opponent's AGI. Dodge difficulty = 8 + opponent's STR.

#### MOVEMENT ZONES

| Zone | Definition |
|---|---|
| Close | Melee distance |
| Near | Outside melee, within a short move |
| Far | Within a long move |

All zones are within missile range. Beyond Far, missile attacks suffer -2 to hit.

**Short Move:** Move one zone and still attack and dodge normally.

**Long Move:** Cover two zones in one round; cannot attack (may still dodge).

**Withdrawing:** Must use a long move (Close → Near) to safely disengage; otherwise provokes an immediate free attack from all opponents in melee.

**Charge:** Straight line from one zone away only. +2 to damage roll, -1 to Dodge this round.

#### FATIGUE

Place a d6 face-up at 6 as a Fatigue counter at combat start.

- Each round a combatant attacks, reduce the Fatigue counter by 1 at round's end.
- At the start of each round, make a TOU check — the remaining pips on the counter count as a bonus to the roll.
- **Failure → Winded:** -1 to all combat rolls (including initiative and morale). Counter resets to 6 but now decrements by 2 per attacking round.
- **Failure while Winded → Exhausted:** All rolls at disadvantage.
- **Recovery:** Any round with no attack action, remove one Fatigue level and reset the counter to 6. An Exhausted combatant can recover to Winded in one such round; Winded to normal in another.

#### KNOCKDOWN

Triggered by: a critical hit, an exploding damage roll of natural 6, or taking 50%+ of total HP in a single attack.

Make an AGI check — failure means knocked prone. Prone combatants attack and dodge at disadvantage. Rising takes a short move.

#### BLEEDING

A wound of 3+ HP from an edged or piercing weapon places a Bleeding counter on the sheet. Bleeding counters are removed at the end of the following round — when removed, the combatant takes 1 HP damage. Dropping to 0 HP from bleeding = passed out (no Death Table roll).

#### FIGHTING MODES

Chosen at the start of each round before initiative is rolled.

| Mode | Attack | Dodge |
|---|---|---|
| All Out | +2 | -2 |
| Aggressive | +1 | -1 |
| Measured | — | — |
| Cautious | -1 | +1 |
| Defensive | -2 | +2 |

A combatant may choose any mode and then opt not to attack, still receiving the dodge bonus.

#### EXPANDED MORALE TRIGGERS

Roll morale whenever an opponent:
- Is hit by a critical
- Takes damage equal to 75%+ of total HP in one hit
- Is reduced to 25% or less total HP
- Has 3 or more Bleeding counters simultaneously

#### ENEMY BEHAVIORS (SOLO PLAY)

Each round, in order:

1. **Morale Check** (if applicable)
2. **Fatigue Check** — Enemies check the same way as PCs. Exhausted enemies roll d6: on 1–4, use Defensive mode and withdraw; on 5–6, roll mode normally.
3. **Roll Enemy Mode:** 1 = Defensive, 2 = Cautious, 3–4 = Measured, 5 = Aggressive, 6 = All Out
4. **Roll Initiative** (Fatigue affects this)
5. **Determine Movement** — Unengaged opponents use ranged combat if able, or close distance. If more than one zone away, short move to close, then another short move to Charge.
6. **Resolve Attacks and Defense** using applicable skills
7. **Add Bleeding counters** if applicable
8. **Adjust Fatigue counter**
9. **Remove Bleeding counters** placed the previous round

## MAGIC

**Magic** is handled by rolling an INT check vs. **Spell Difficulty**. Success indicates normal casting and results. Critical success doubles the effect of the spell.

Magic-using characters must choose "Make a Demonic Pact" either at creation or later through gameplay. Magic is the result of **Pacts** with demons who represent certain ideals or archetypes — these powers must be paid in return for their knowledge.

### DEMONIC PACTS (CHOOSE OR ROLL D6)

| d6 | Pact |
|----|------|
| 1 | Blood |
| 2 | Destruction |
| 3 | Corruption |
| 4 | Illumination |
| 5 | Shadow |
| 6 | Domination |

There are 5 spells in each pact (see Spells).

### DOOMS

To maintain a pact requires taking on various taboos and observances, known as **Dooms**. Each affects the character in a major way and limits roleplaying choices.

| d6 | Doom |
|----|------|
| 1 | The caster must not use metal weapons or armor. Violation breaks their pact and they lose all spells and powers. |
| 2 | Each new moon, the caster must sacrifice something of great value (monetarily or otherwise, up to and including human sacrifices). Failure to do so may result in a curse or the loss of powers. |
| 3 | The caster must speak the names and prayers of his demon master out loud when casting. He is unable to cast spells if silenced through mundane or magical means, and his casting is loud, attracting attention of any in earshot. |
| 4 | At the behest of his demon, the caster must travel to a desolate place of powerful significance and spend d6 days and nights there in prayer and meditation. |
| 5 | The caster must create all their spells on scrolls written in blood that cost 1 HP permanently to keep that spell "active" and ready to cast. Destroying a scroll gives the lost HP back, but the spell cannot be used until a new scroll is made (1 full night of work). |
| 6 | The caster may not speak for a number of hours equal to the tier of the last spell cast. Breaking the silence before time is up could mean the loss of that spell's use, or some other wrath from their patron. |

### SPELL TIERS & DIFFICULTY

All five spells in a pact are available to the caster immediately depending on their INT — **but casting requires a base difficulty of 8, which increases by 1 for each tier of the spell.**

**A caster may only attempt a spell of a Tier as high as their current unaltered INT score.**

| Tier | Difficulty | INT Required |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | 8 | +1 |
| 2 | 9 | +2 |
| 3 | 10 | +3 |
| 4 | 11 | +4 |
| 5 | 12 | +5 |

Natural 12's always indicate success, and the spell's effect is considered "doubled" or "extreme."

**Casting Failure** results in 1 damage to the caster and the inability to cast spells again until a rest.

### ARCANE DISASTER

Critical Failure results in Arcane Disaster (roll 2d6):

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 2 | A lesser demon takes hold of the caster's body. The caster's soul is trapped and enslaved to the demon. Adventure is at an end. |
| 3 | The caster's blood vessels burst. Reduced to 0 HP and -2 TOU permanently unless healed by powerful magic. Roll on Death Table. |
| 4 | Permanent psychic damage. -2 INT and suffer from vivid, traumatic hallucinations. |
| 5 | A part of the caster's soul is torn away. -1 INT and -1 PRE; suffer from horror, despair, and emptiness. |
| 6 | Cursed by a demon. Lose ability to use Fate Point until the curse is ended. |
| 7 | Body changes in a disturbing way. -1 PRESENCE, but +1 PRE to intimidation. |
| 8 | Spell sigils burn into the caster's flesh. -1 PRESENCE; chronic pain makes STR checks at disadvantage. |
| 9 | Magic lashes back at the caster. -1 permanent HP. |
| 10 | Psychic echo of the failed spell. -1 INTELLIGENCE for d6 sessions. |
| 11 | Spell drains the caster's vitality. -1 STRENGTH from fatigue for the remainder of the session. |
| 12 | The spell fails with only minor psychic backlash. |

### PACT OF FLESH (Book 3)

Servants and dedicants of Kae'lith have mastered the body's reactions to pleasure and pain. This pact is in opposition to the cults of Akkai.

#### Tier 1
**Serene Vision of Supreme Ecstasy** — Caster chooses d3 targets who are totally immobilized with intense pleasure or pain for d3 rounds. Attack rolls against affected targets automatically succeed, but will end the effect.

#### Tier 2
**Bondage Is Complete Freedom** — 2 barbed chains spring from the caster's body for d6 rounds +1 per caster level. They attack independently each round using the caster's PRE for attacks, dealing d6/d damage. On a damage roll of 5–6, the chain hooks into the target and automatically deals d6/d per round until target succeeds at a STR check to break free — doing so causes another d6/d damage.

#### Tier 3
**Ever-Changing Temple of Flesh** — For 10 minutes + 10 minutes per level, the caster describes desired physical changes and manipulates any stat by up to +2. Another stat is lessened by 2 for the duration.

#### Tier 4
**Fearsome Icon of Kae'lith's Chosen** — Permanently rewrites the biology of a living creature within 30'. The target's physical form is completely altered, with 5 stat points to distribute as the caster chooses. The target obeys all caster commands, but each day roll 2d6 for each servitor — on a 2–3, the servitor immediately attempts to find and kill the caster.

#### Tier 5
**Many Forms, Many Pleasures** — Caster places their life essence into a sculpture no smaller than their physical form. Their body becomes dormant. The caster may possess any human within 20' of the sculpture with a successful casting check — the target's mind is trapped in the sculpture. The caster may reverse the effect at any time. If the possessed body is killed, the caster's consciousness returns to the sculpture immediately. While in the sculpture or another body, the caster's physical form does not age.

### PACT OF FLESH DOOMS (Book 3)

Roll when first making a Pact with Kae'lith. Roll again on any critical casting failure (also roll on Arcane Disaster table).

1. Caster becomes addicted to either pleasure or pain (d6: even = pleasure, odd = pain). Must indulge each day or suffer cumulative -1 to all rolls per day abstained. No experience can be repeated more than 3 times without meaningful escalation.
2. Caster can survive only on raw meat.
3. Caster develops instability of form — all stats are rerolled every day (roll d6 then subtract a second d6). Extreme results indicate extreme physical and mental changes.
4. Caster becomes obsessed with extreme body modification. At least one major modification (amputation, scarification, piercing, extensive tattooing/branding) must be done each month or they lose access to their Pact.
5. Caster experiences intense bouts of pleasure or pain at random moments — blessings from Kae'lith. Any roll resulting in doubles forces a PRE check or the caster cannot act for 1 round.
6. Caster suffers -2 to all social PRE checks with anyone outside the Cult of Kae'lith; +2 with all worshipers of Kae'lith.

Upon discovery of a dungeon or ruin, roll on the following tables to generate it.

### NATURE AND SIZE OF THE LOCATION

#### Nature (d6)

| d6 | Nature |
|----|--------|
| 1 | City Ruins |
| 2 | Dungeon |
| 3 | Temple |
| 4 | Caverns |
| 5 | Fort |
| 6 | Special (vertical structure with bridges, underwater caves, labyrinth, ruined vessel, etc.) |

#### Size (d6)

| d6 | Size | Areas |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Small | 3 |
| 2–3 | Medium | 5 |
| 4 | Large | 7 |
| 5 | Vast | 9 |
| 6 | Colossal | 11 |

*Adjust sizes as needed for desired challenge and pacing.*

### DETERMINING DUNGEON AREAS

#### Dice Pool Creation

Create a pool of d6's equal to the number of areas — e.g., a Large dungeon uses 7d6. Areas can represent a floor, a room, a large cavern, a section of corridors, or anything similar. Each die's face value determines its contents from the Area Type table.

#### Rolling and Placement

Roll all dice at once on a flat surface. The dice positions represent the physical layout of areas:

- **Closer dice** are directly connected.
- **Farther dice** require passages, stairs, or additional connectors — roll on the Passages table for those gaps or simply roleplay them.
- The **number on each die** indicates area type (see below).

*Solo play: Draw around the dice on the initial throw to establish shape and connections. When entering each area, reroll that die to reveal its contents — this preserves discovery rather than mapping everything in advance.*

### BOSS ROOMS

If a 6 is rolled, that die is a Boss area. If multiple 6's appear, keep only the one furthest into the dungeon and reroll the others until a non-6 result appears.

### DUNGEON FOE

Roll 2d6 for encounter type. Any entry can be replaced with a creature that better fits the generated dungeon.

**Converting OSR monsters:**
- Convert HD to d6 HP dice.
- d6–d8 damage → d6. d4 and under → d6/d. d10–d12 → d6/a.
- Heavier damage → appropriate number of d6's (2d6 is heavy; 5d6 is near-lethal).
- Descending AC 7–9 = Light (1); 4–6 = Medium (2); 0–3 or better = Heavy (3).
- Ascending AC +1–+3 = Light; +4–+6 = Medium; +7–+10 = Heavy.

All monsters need: HP, Attack/Damage, Armor, Morale, and any special abilities.

## VALLEY OF THE BLACK ZIGGURAT (Book 2)

### Setup

Roll 22d6, or scatter that many tokens on a sheet and roll contents individually.

- The die closest to you is rerolled if it shows a 6.
- The die furthest from you is always a 6 (Boss area).
- Any other 6's are rerolled.

**Solo play:** Drop all 22d6 to determine shape and connections. Reroll each die as you enter that area to preserve discovery.

On rolls of 1–3 when traversing a passage or pathway, roll another d6: 1–3 = Random Foe, 4–6 = Trap/Obstacle.

Reaction rolls are never rolled in the Valley — all Foes serve Ictezoma'a and the Ziggurat and are always hostile.

When PCs leave the Valley, re-roll all previously visited areas.

### Negotiation Roll (2d6 + PRE)

Roll at the beginning of each deal.

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| <8 | Buying is 10% more expensive; selling is 10% less profitable. |
| 8+ | Even trade at standard rate. |
| 10+ | 10% in PCs' favor. |

## GLADIATOR CAMPAIGN (Book 3)

### New Stat: FAME

Fame tracks a Pit Fighter's reputation. It increases on wins and decreases on losses, tracked separately from XP. Fame affects Location, Earnings, and certain other game aspects. Fame begins at 1 and cannot drop below 1.

### Faces and Heels

**PRE** controls crowd interaction. When doubles are rolled in a pit fight, crowd momentum is activated:

- **Successful PRE check:** Crowd is with you — +1 to all die rolls this match. Winning a match with momentum = gain a **Face** marker.
- **Face marker:** Make all future crowd momentum checks at advantage.
- **Failed PRE check:** Crowd turns against you — -1 to all rolls this match. Winning while crowd is against you = gain a **Heel** marker.
- **Heel marker:** Roll PRE at disadvantage on crowd momentum checks. A failed check grants +1 (feeding on their hatred). Winning a match with momentum, or making a successful check, removes Heel status.

A current Face or Heel marker allows an immediate PRE check for crowd momentum at the start of each match (instead of waiting for doubles).

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Two Faces | Highest PRE check wins momentum as the crowd favorite |
| Two Heels | Lower PRE check wins momentum as the most hated |
| Face vs. Heel | Both enjoy their respective bonus simultaneously |

### Optional Combat Rule: Modified Initiative

In the Gladiator Campaign, INT bonus — not AGI — affects the initiative roll and determines who acts first.

### Match Types (All Locations)

**One v One** — PC faces a single opponent.

**One v Many** — PC faces multiple opponents. Opponents gain pack tactics: -2 to PC's Dodge when outnumbered (multiple opponents in melee).

**Team Match** — Generally 3 v 3. Initiative rolled individually; combatants act in order high to low. Ties are simultaneous. Pack tactics apply to outnumbered individuals.

**Beast Match** — PC fights one or more beasts. Only beasts with pack tactics listed receive its benefit.

**Death Match** — A fight to the death. If reduced to unconsciousness, the PC is considered dead. Winners earn double Fame and earnings.

**Championship Match** — Double Fame and coin. A Championship Death Match is worth 5× total Fame and coin.

### Opponent Ranking System

When rolling for an opponent at any Location, mark their position. Your next opponent is automatically the next higher number. Losing drops you d6 spots in the rankings. If a fighter is killed and permanently removed, select the next higher one.

To determine opponent's weapon (if not listed), roll d6: 1 Sword, 2 Dagger, 3 Spear, 4 Club, 5 Axe, 6 Flail.

All fighters own a shield and receive +1 armor in any match where shields are allowed. Fighters not wielding two weapons, a two-handed weapon, or who benefit from being unarmored will use a shield.
