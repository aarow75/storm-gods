# OSRIC Spells

Arcane casters (Magic Users, Illusionists) learn spells into spellbooks and must memorize them each day; INT determines the chance to learn each new spell and the maximum knowable per level. Divine casters (Clerics, Druids) pray for spells; WIS grants bonus spells and high-level access. **Paladins** cast from the Cleric list starting at level 9; **Rangers** gain Druid spells at level 8 and Magic User spells at level 9. A spell is expended when cast and must be re-memorized or re-prayed. Casting requires speech and gestures — a bound and gagged caster is helpless — and taking damage before the spell's casting time completes spoils it.
