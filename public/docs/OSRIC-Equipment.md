# OSRIC Equipment

Costs are in **gold pieces (gp)**: 10 sp = 1 gp, 100 cp = 1 gp. Encumbrance is in pounds; a character can carry up to 150 lb adjusted for STR bonus before slowing down. Starting gold depends on class (Fighters 5d4×10 gp, Clerics 3d6×10, Magic Users 2d4×10, Thieves 2d6×10).
