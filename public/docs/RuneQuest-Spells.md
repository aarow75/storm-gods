# RuneQuest Spells

## Spirit Magic

Spirit magic (called *battle magic* in RuneQuest Classic) is powered by the caster's POW. Unless noted otherwise, spells have a range of 80 meters and *temporal* spells last 2 minutes (10 melee rounds). Casting an offensive spell requires overcoming the target's POW on the resistance table. *Variable* spells can be learned and cast at 1 to 4 points; *focused* spells require a focus to cast.

### Bladesharp
*Variable · 80m · passive, temporal.* Cast on any thrusting, stabbing, or hacking weapon: +5% chance to hit and +1 damage per point of the spell, to a maximum of +20%/+4. The points may be split among several weapons. Incompatible with other weapon-improving spells (Fireblade, Dullblade).

### Countermagic
*Variable · 80m · passive, temporal.* A defensive shell that attempts to stop incoming spells. An incoming spell two or more points weaker is eliminated and the Countermagic remains; equal or within one point, both are eliminated; two or more points stronger, it blows through. Operates at half effectiveness against Rune magic; cannot dispel spirits or elementals. Incompatible with Protection, Shimmer, and Spirit Screen.

### Detect
*1–2 points · 40m · focused, instant.* The Detect family locates things by direction and distance: **Detect Magic** (enchanted items glow), **Detect Life** (human-sized and larger life), **Detect Enemies** (beings intending harm to a chosen person), **Detect Gold/Silver/Gems**, **Detect Spirit**, **Detect Undead**, and **Detect Traps** (reveals the intent to trap in a glowing 3m circle). All are stopped by more than 3 meters of solid rock or metal.

### Disruption
*1 point · 80m · focused, instant.* Speeds up random molecular motion in the target's body. If the caster's POW overcomes the target's POW, the target takes 1D3 damage in a random hit location, **not absorbed by armor**.

### Extinguish
*2 points · 40m · focused, instant.* Puts out one fire up to the size of a large bonfire, or all small fires within 10 meters. Extinguished fires are cold and must be relit from scratch. (A Light spell is not vulnerable to Extinguish.)

### Fanaticism
*1 point per target · 80m · temporal.* Increases the fanatic's hit probability by half again (a 30% swordsman becomes 45%), but the fanatic cannot parry or cast protective spells, and any Defense bonus is halved. One person is affected per point invested. Demoralize cancels Fanaticism, and vice versa.

### Firearrow
*2 points · 80m · focused, passive, instant.* Cast on an ordinary arrow (or sling stone or thrown weapon), which bursts into flame as it is loosed and does **3D6 damage** if it hits — an impaling Firearrow does the weapon's full normal damage *plus* 3D6. The damage is heat, not magic, so it cannot be resisted, but armor absorbs it. The missile is consumed. Excellent for igniting houses. Incompatible with Multimissile and Speedart.

### Fireblade
*4 points · 80m · focused, active, temporal.* Cast on the metal part of an edged weapon, which flames and does **3D6 damage** on a hit — this replaces the weapon's normal damage (damage bonus still applies; impales use the Fireblade damage). Armor absorbs the damage. If the caster takes physical or magical damage, the Fireblade goes out. Incompatible with Bladesharp and Dullblade.

### Glamour
*2 points · 80m · passive, temporal.* Adds half again to the target's CHA for the duration. Too brief to impress a guild, but very useful in a crisis of leadership — or for convincing your captors that you can pay a good ransom.

### Heal
*Variable · touch · permanent.* Heals 1 point of damage per point of spell; the wounded location must be touched, and effect is immediate. Two points cauterize a severed limb; **six points reattach a severed limb** if both parts are available. A hit location can only be healed once every five melee rounds, and overlapping heals don't stack — the larger takes effect. Cannot raise the dead.

### Ignite
*1 point · 40m · focused, instant.* Sets fire to anything normally burnable, with the effect of a butane lighter — a torch, a house, a pile of hay. Living skin and hair cannot be ignited without overcoming the victim's magic resistance.

### Light
*1 point · 80m · focused, passive, temporal.* Cast on an object — a stick, a fingertip — which then lights a 12-meter radius, bright enough to read by. Not vulnerable to Extinguish.

### Lightwall
*4 points · 80m · focused, passive, temporal.* Creates a wall of light 10m wide × 3m high × 6cm thick. The caster's side can see through it; the glare blocks all vision from the other side. It illuminates 15 meters on each side, can be shaped, and can be moved 3 meters per round if the caster does nothing else.

### Mobility
*1 point · 80m · focused, passive, temporal.* Doubles the movement class of the recipient. Carried by hunters for themselves and their animals, and by elite cavalry in wartime for shock value.

### Protection
*Variable · 80m · passive, temporal.* Adds one point of armor per point of spell (up to four) to **all** parts of the body. Critical hits ignore it, as they do armor. Incompatible with Countermagic, Shimmer, and Spirit Screen.

### Shimmer
*Variable · 80m · passive, temporal.* Adds 5% per point (max 20%) to the target's Defense against **all** attackers. Incompatible with Countermagic, Protection, and Spirit Screen.

### Speedart
*1 point · 80m · focused, passive, temporal.* Gives missiles +15% to hit and +3 damage. Works on any missile — arrows, thrown axes, knives, rocks, sling bullets. Incompatible with Firearrow and Multimissile.

### Spirit Screen
*Variable · 80m · passive, temporal.* (Also called Spirit Shield.) Protection for spirit combat: each point temporarily destroys two points of an attacking spirit's POW. Must be cast **before** engaging in spirit combat. Incompatible with Countermagic, Protection, and Shimmer.

### Strength
*2 points · 80m · passive, temporal.* Adds half again to the target's effective STR (round up), never above species maximum (21 for humans). Incompatible with Vigor.

### Vigor
*4 points · 80m · passive, temporal.* Adds half again to the target's CON for the duration — you can take damage that would normally kill you and be healed before the spell wears off. Never above species maximum. Incompatible with Strength.

## Rune Magic

Rune magic comes from the gods. Initiates and priests sacrifice POW for **rune points**; casting a rune spell succeeds on the caster's holy strength and takes effect at strike rank 1. *Reusable* spells return with worship at the caster's temple; *one-use* spells are gone once cast. Rune magic is twice as strong as spirit magic against Countermagic and Dispel Magic.

### Orlanth

#### Wind Words
*1 rune point · 160m · 15 minutes · reusable.* If downwind, the caster can have the wind carry conversations within range to his ears as if he were standing next to the speakers.

#### Lightning
*2 rune points · 160m · instant · reusable.* The caster hurls a bolt of Orlanth's lightning at one target he can see. If the caster's POW overcomes the target's, the bolt does 3D6 damage directly to a random hit location; armor does not protect against the storm's fire.

#### Thunderbolt
*3 rune points · 160m · instant · one-use.* Orlanth's own weapon: thunder and lightning strike a single foe. If the caster overcomes the target's POW, the target takes 3D6 damage to total hit points **and** must be healed magically before natural healing can begin. The crash is heard for kilometers.

### Ernalda

#### Bless Crops
*1 rune point · ritual · one season · reusable.* Cast on a field at planting, this spell wards one hectare against blight, pest, and drought and increases the harvest by half again. The heart of Ernalda's pact with her worshippers — temples cast it for the community every Sea Season.

#### Heal Body
*3 rune points · touch · instant · reusable.* Cures the **total damage** taken by a body, regardless of hit location. It will also regrow or heal a maimed limb: roll 1D100 for the percent of the limb lost; it regrows at one game week per 10% if the spell is applied within ten minutes, one game month per 10% if cast within seven days. After seven days the limb will not regrow.

### Humakt

#### Truesword
*1 rune point · handheld · 15 minutes · reusable.* **Doubles the damage** done by any sword in the wielder's hand, up to the maximum the sword can roll (a broadsword does up to 9 points, a greatsword up to 16). Does not double damage bonuses or Bladesharp; no effect on a Fireblade.

#### Shield
*2 rune points · caster or touch · 15 minutes · reusable.* Each point of Shield provides 2 points of armor protection on all locations **and** 2 points of Countermagic, stacking with armor but not with Protection or Countermagic spells cast separately. Incompatible with Absorption, Reflection, and Spirit Block.

#### Sever Spirit
*3 rune points · 160m · instant · one-use (reusable for Humakt's own priests).* Acts as a sword to cut the bond between body and spirit. The priest makes a POW vs. POW roll: if successful, **the target is dead**. If unsuccessful, the victim still takes 1D6 damage to CON, with effects similar to poison damage.

### Seven Mothers

#### Reflection
*2 rune points · 160m · 15 minutes · reusable.* Reflects incoming spells back at their casters. Any spell of no more POW points than the Reflection is turned back on its caster (battle magic counts at half strength against it — a 2-point Reflection turns back battle magic of up to 4 points). Incompatible with Absorption, Shield, and Spirit Block.

#### Axis Mundi
*3 rune points · ritual · while maintained · reusable.* Establishes a Center of Power — a gateway between the mundane world and the spirit world, as taught by the Red Goddess's missionaries. Within the warded circle the cult's worshippers add the priest's rune magic to their defense, spirits of the cult may pass freely to aid the faithful, and hostile spirits must overcome the priest's POW to enter.

## Sorcery

> The character creator uses the sorcery spell list of the modern RuneQuest rules; these spells do not appear in RuneQuest Classic, so the summaries below are adapted to match. Sorcery is manipulated by **intensity** (effect), **range**, and **duration**, limited by the sorcerer's mastered techniques and Free INT. Casting costs 1 magic point per point of manipulation.

### Animate (Substance)
Causes a mass of the named substance (clay, stone, water, rope…) to move at the sorcerer's mental command. Each point of intensity animates roughly 1 SIZ of material; the substance can grip, carry, or strike as the caster directs while concentration lasts.

### Banish
Drives a spirit, elemental, or summoned entity back to its home plane. The caster's intensity must overcome the entity's POW; on success the entity is expelled immediately and cannot return by the same summoning.

### Beast Form
Transforms a willing target (or the caster) into an ordinary animal — wolf, hawk, fish — of roughly the same SIZ per intensity. The subject keeps INT and POW but gains the beast's physical characteristics, movement, and natural weapons.

### Blessing
Lays a minor benediction on a person, tool, or endeavor: +5% per intensity to one named skill used under the blessing's purpose, while the duration lasts.

### Castback
A ward that hurls hostile magic back at its source. Any spell failing to exceed the Castback's intensity strikes its own caster with full effect. The ward collapses after reflecting a spell.

### Curse
Afflicts the target with misfortune: −5% per intensity to all skill rolls while the curse endures. The target's POW must be overcome, and the curse can be lifted by Dispel Magic of equal intensity.

### Damage Boosting
Cast on a weapon: adds 1 point of damage per intensity to each successful blow. The sorcerous keen is visible as a faint violet edge. Incompatible with other damage-enhancing magic.

### Damage Resistance
The target's flesh turns aside harm: incoming damage from each blow is reduced by the spell's intensity before armor. One of the sorcerer's chief defenses, since sorcery grants no armor of faith.

### Diminish (Characteristic)
Reduces the named characteristic (STR, CON, DEX…) of the target by 1 per 2 intensity, if the target's POW is overcome. A fighter with diminished STR loses damage bonus; diminished DEX slows strike rank.

### Dispel Magic
Unweaves active magic: eliminates one spell of equal or lower total magnitude. Works on spirit magic at full strength and on rune magic at half strength.

### Dominate (Species)
Seizes the will of one creature of the named species whose POW is overcome. The victim obeys the sorcerer's mental commands for the duration, though it will not directly cause its own death.

### Enhance (Characteristic)
Raises the named characteristic of the target by 1 per 2 intensity, up to half again its natural value. Enhanced STR increases damage bonus; enhanced DEX quickens strike rank.

### Flight
Lifts the target into the air and moves them at the caster's will — roughly movement class 3 per intensity. An unwilling target's POW must be overcome; a flier dropped when the spell ends falls normally.

### Forge (Substance)
Shapes and joins the named substance as a master craftsman would, without tools or heat: mends a sword, welds a chain, seals a stone wall. One SIZ of material per intensity is worked per casting.

### Glow
Conjures sorcerous light on an object or in the air: a soft radiance illuminating 3 meters per intensity. Unlike spirit magic Light, its color and brightness are at the caster's whim.

### Haste
Accelerates the target: +1 movement class and −1 strike rank per 2 intensity. The world seems to slow around the hastened, but the strain leaves them fatigued when the spell ends.

### Illusion
Weaves a phantom of sight and sound up to 1 SIZ per intensity. It cannot cause direct harm; a viewer who tests it or has cause to doubt may disbelieve by overcoming the spell's intensity with INT.

### Neutralize Magic
Suppresses rather than destroys: magic of equal or lower magnitude within the target area ceases to function for the spell's duration, resuming when it lapses.

### Palsy
Locks one hit location of the target rigid, if their POW is overcome — an arm drops its sword, a palsied leg collapses. Vital locations (head, chest, abdomen) render the victim helpless but not dead.

### Phantom (Sense)
Creates a false impression of the named sense — a sound, a scent, a touch, a taste — perceived by all within range. Useful for the whispered voice in a guard's ear or the smell of smoke in a crowded hall.

### Regenerate
The slow sorcerous knitting of flesh: the target heals 1 hit point per intensity in each hit location over the spell's duration, and severed members regrow if the spell is maintained over weeks.

### Sculpt (Substance)
Reshapes the named substance plastically under the sorcerer's hands — stone flows, iron bends, glass stretches — 1 SIZ per intensity. The material keeps its new shape when the spell ends.

### Sense (Substance)
The sorcerer perceives the named substance within range: direction, distance, and rough quantity, through up to 3 meters of intervening rock or metal per intensity.

### Slow
Retards the target: −1 movement class and +1 strike rank per 2 intensity, if their POW is overcome. A fully slowed foe seems to wade through honey.

### Spirit Screen
As the spirit magic spell: shields the target in spirit combat, blunting an attacking spirit's POW by 2 per intensity. The sorcerous version can be extended by duration manipulation to last through a night's vigil.

### Summon (Entity)
A ritual that calls an entity of the named type — elemental, spirit, or stranger thing — from its home plane. The summoned entity's size and power are governed by the intensity of the ritual; a summoning without a binding is an invitation, not a command.

### Teleport
Instantly moves the caster (or a touched target) to a spot within range that the caster can see. Each intensity carries roughly SIZ 7; an unwilling target's POW must be overcome.

### Venom
Turns the target's own blood venomous, or envenoms a blade: creates a poison of potency 6 per intensity that attacks the victim's CON on the resistance table, doing its potency in damage on success and half on failure.

### Ward
Protects a place or person against intrusion: a warded area of 1 meter radius per intensity alerts the caster when crossed, and intruders suffer 1D3 damage per 2 intensity unless they overcome the ward's intensity with POW.
