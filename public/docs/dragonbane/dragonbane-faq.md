## Your Player Character

### What's the point of Key Attribute in Professions?

Nothing from a rules standpoint. It's just a little help when you make the character. It is good to be high in that basic trait if you are going to become that profession. It has no rule aspects.

## Skills

### Can I push a failed push and so on until I succeed?

No. You can only push your failed skill or attribute roll once.

> p32 Pushing your roll: "The new result applies, whatever it is."

### Do multiple Boons and/or Banes stack? How do they work together?

> p32: If you get multiple boons/banes to your roll, roll an additional D20 for each boon/bane and count only the lowest/highest result. However, please note that unless you have a very high skill level, rolls with multiple banes have a very low chance of success.

> p32 Boon and Bane: Sometimes you might get both boons and banes to your roll. Each boon negates one bane, and vice versa. If you have one boon and one bane, make a normal roll (one D20). If you have two boons and one bane, it counts as a boon.

### I want to Hide?

If a player wants to hide, does the "Sneaking" skill apply?

Yes, it's in the description (p34).

### Weapon or Ride skill to fight while riding?

> p55: On horseback you fight as usual, but all attacks and parries use the lowest of your RIDING skill and the weapon skill. Also, RIDING replaces EVADE when horseback.

When you are on horseback and going to attack or parry with a sword, you also need to take the Riding skill into account. So, for example, if you have 10 in Riding and 15 in Sword, 10 is what you use.

**Suggestion**: Mounting/Dismounting is a RIDE check or an Action, player choice in the moment.

### What skill to use for listening?

Awareness (p33 English / p34 Swedish).

### Can I lock a door with Sleight of Hand (and lockpicks)?

Yes. You can do this in reality so why not in an RPG?

### Ability Check roll question

When instructed to roll an ability check (not a skill check) are you rolling under the attribute value?

Yes, you roll with your attribute value. So if you have STR of 15, you need to roll 15 or lower.

### Does Brawling count as a Weapon skill?

Yes. p35: Brawling is under Weapon Skills.

### Question about shields and skill advancement marks

Can someone with 10 in Axes and 18 in Swords use their Axe skill for the Shield in order to potentially get advancement marks when parrying?

Yes you can use Axe skill for shields and get marks in it.

> p46 "There is no skill for shields – instead you can use any STR-based melee skill (i.e. any of them except KNIVES and STAVES) to parry with a shield."

### Which skill would you use to follow a trail in a dungeon?

It depends on how the player describes their approach:
- Tracking like a hunter → Hunting & Fishing
- Looking for minuscule clues → Spot Hidden
- Animal lair / signs → Beast Lore
- Remembering a legendary monster → Myths & Legends
- Tracking an intelligent creature trying to not be followed → Sneaking and Awareness

### Can you push a Bushcraft roll to make camp/sleep?

Yes, you can push Bushcraft — but you only get one shot. And if your rest is interrupted, you could be in deep trouble.

> Shift Rest: A shift rest lasts one full shift of time and can only take place in a safe location where there are no enemies nearby. During a shift rest you recover all your lost HP and WP and heal all conditions. If a shift rest is interrupted by combat or hard work it has no effect.

**Suggestion**: The pushed condition should not be cleared by sleep — live with it the day after.

### Why would a player ever not Barter?

Really it's up to the GM to balance. The risk is rolling a Demon. GMs can increase the price on a failed roll.

### When are Opposed rolls used in the rules?

Opposed rolls are called out where used. There are only two open opposed rolls (Poison and Disease). The full list:

- Awareness
- Persuasion
- Sneaking
- Topple
- Disarm
- Grapple
- Poison (Open Opposed)
- Disease (Open Opposed)
- Dominate spell
- Chess set
- Playing cards

## Heroic Abilities

### Does a Sneak Attack and Backstab damage stack?

No. A Backstab is already a sneak attack, so they don't stack. — *Tomas (Free League)*

Anyone can attempt an ordinary Sneak Attack (p43). The Backstab Heroic Ability is exactly the same thing but with no Bane. That's why they don't stack — you can't Double Sneak Attack.

**Does Backstab cancel out the bane on the Sneak Attack?**

This is a misunderstanding. The Backstab boon is to the attack roll, not the SNEAKING. In combat where you can use BACKSTAB, you don't do a SNEAKING roll.

### Do Heroic Abilities use an action?

Yes — unless stated otherwise, they count as an action. Some of them are probably in need of clearing up, but in some cases it might be an obvious missed entry.

### Does any Weapon skill work for HA Requirements?

Yes. If you have any weapon skill at 12, the requirement is met. Example: Brawling 14 and Knife 5 — the Brawling 14 satisfies a "weapon skill 12" requirement.

### Does HA have no Requirements when creating characters?

Yes, no requirements at character creation.

> p26 "Note that some heroic abilities require a minimum skill level. Such requirements don't apply to your starting heroic ability."

### Does Weasel use an Action/Reaction?

Correct, Weasel does not use an action. — *Tomas (Free League)*

### Can you use HA Defensive or Fast Footwork even if you had your turn and used your action?

Yes.

> p36-37: "You may attempt to dodge/parry an attack without consuming your action for the round. The bonus dodge/parry can be performed at any time during the round." "This ability can be used multiple times per round, as long as you have enough WP."

### Can Musician maximize the length of effect by waiting to the end of the next turn?

Yes. The idea is that your turn doesn't happen until you're using it. If you 'wait', your turn is moved in the round. All effects that trigger on your turn are also moved if you swap cards (voluntarily or not). — *Tomas (Free League)*

### Musician ally range effect?

Friends should be within 10 meters when you activate the ability. What they do after you activate matters less — you were just inspired when the bard sang.

### What rolls does Musician boons affect?

"All rolls" means any action using Skills or Attribute rolls. Moving rocks (STR roll), figuring out a puzzle (INT), Bushcraft rolls for the night — all get the boon.

Note: players can Help each other (p32) outside of combat to give a boon also by default, and boons stack.

### Musician against Monsters?

Monsters are a type of enemy, so you can choose to use Musician to give monsters disadvantage on skill rolls. However, monsters don't use skills for attacks (p84 top), so it's probably better to choose to give friends advantage instead. Monster dodge and parry rolls against skill 15 could be a useful target for the bane.

### Can a PC being targeted by the redirected Weasel attack defend?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Yes, the PC can defend.
> "The new target may try to dodge or parry normally."

### Is it possible to use Heroic Abilities together?

Generally yes, unless stated otherwise. Combining Guardian (force enemy to attack you) + Defensive (parry without spending action) makes sense. Chaining Weasel to multiple PCs is also possible but makes sense only if everyone is within base contact.

### Can you try using Defensive, Fast Footwork and Weasel against the same attack?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No.

- Defensive: "...you cannot try to dodge and parry the same attack."
- Fast Footwork: "...you cannot try to dodge and parry the same attack."
- Weasel: "...you must activate this ability before attempting any dodge or parry."

### If a skill decreases from 18, what happens to the HA you got?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**:
> p29 "If the skill level is reduced below 18 for any reason, you keep the heroic ability and you will even gain a new ability if the skill level is increased to 18 again."

### HA Iron Fist and the Mentalist spell Power Fist seem to clash?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No longer a problem.

> p38 Iron Fist: "The damage of an unarmed attack increases **by** one D6." (previously "increases to 2D6", which caused the conflict)

### Does the Berserker heroic ability cost an action?

Activating Berserk and the first attack are one single action (based on "immediately attack the nearest opponent"). Subsequent attacks after that are normal turns.

## Combat

### When does a Turn start?

That your turn only actually starts when you move/take your action is reasonable. No official ruling yet, but "Before beginning your turn you can choose to wait" is better wording.

### Can enemy NPCs swap initiative cards?

Yes they can. It's monsters that don't swap.

> p41 Waiting: "Monsters themselves never choose to wait."

### Does Actions replenish at the start of each Round or at the start of your Turn?

All Actions replenish at the start of each Round. This is implied from how the other rules work but not spelled out directly.

### Running away from combat?

1. Apply the free attack rule (p43) when the fugitive moves away.
2. If they survive the free attack, they're out of the battle and removed from the playing field.
3. Combatants with ranged weapons may shoot at the fleeing character on their turn until out of range.
4. Those still in the fight can choose to pursue on their next turn.
5. If the pursuer gets out of the fight, roll opposed Movement to see if they catch up.

### What happens when I roll a Demon on a Parry/Dodge?

Nothing in the rules, but as a GM feel free to embellish. Note it applies to NPCs too.

**Suggestion for Parry Demon**:
1. Take an extra D6 damage
2. No armor protection
3. Weapon/Shield is damaged
4. Break something else important you carry
5. Take a new condition
6. Enemy gets extra weapon damage die

**Suggestion for Dodge Demon**:
1. You end up Prone
2. Take an extra D6 damage
3. Drop something important (gone on a 1 on D6)
4. Damage armor
5. Take a new condition
6. Enemy gets an extra weapon damage die

### Initiative: Needing More Than 10 Cards

> p41 INITIATIVE FOR NPCS: "To make things simple, particularly in large battles, the GM can draw a single initiative card for a group of NPCs. All NPCs in that group act on the same turn in the round."

**Suggestion**: Use a suit from a normal deck of cards for 13 initiative cards.

### In an Ambush how do you handle the initiative cards?

Those who fail the Awareness roll get the bottom cards (counting from #10 and down). Sort out as many high-numbered cards (10, 9, 8, etc.) as the number of failed characters, shuffle those, and deal from that group.

### Is diagonal movement allowed on the combat board?

Yes (p44).

### Weapon size restrictions for Mallards and Halflings?

No size restrictions are mentioned in the rules.

### Weapons with STR range used in melee have 2 meter range?

Yes, throwable weapons that have "Range: STR" automatically have 2m range when used as a melee weapon.

### Can you use a round rest outside combat?

Yes, you can. The rules are written with combat in mind but a round rest can be used in non-combat times as well (once per shift). — *Fenhorn (FL Moderator)*

### Does the free action "Draw weapon" allow you to sheathe a weapon for free?

> p43 Draw Weapon: "Draw, exchange, or put away a weapon kept at hand."

Yes, "put away" means you can sheathe any weapon as a free action. It's not about stuffing things into a backpack — it's about freeing your hands.

### What's the point of using Shields?

From Tomas (Free League):
- Shields have much higher durability than weapons (don't break as easily)
- Shields are the **only** way to parry ranged attacks (without HA Deflect Arrow)
- Some monster attacks can **only** be parried with a shield
- Shield is a weapon in its own right (D8 damage) — not helpless if disarmed
- Works with the Dual Wield heroic ability

"We don't want to make shields essential — this is a game of fantasy mirth & mayhem, not a medieval combat simulator. If you really want to take full advantage of a shield, learn Shield Block."

### Are Parry and Dodge opposed rolls?

No. Both attacker and defender spend an action each. In opposed rolls only the active party spends an action.

> p42 Reactions: "Some actions are not performed on your turn, but on the opponent's."
> p46 Parry: "When parrying, you roll against your skill level for the weapon."
> p47 Dodge: "Roll for EVADE – on success you evade the attack and take no damage."

### Can you push Parry and Dodge skill rolls?

Yes. The push rules only consider if it's a Skill or Attribute roll — not when or how it's used.

### Attacking weapon (Knife) breaking on armor?

> p50: "If the damage from a melee attack is completely negated, the attacking weapon itself suffers the damage instead."

Yes, since the Knife only has 6 durability it breaks. But it's a special case — all other melee weapons have 9 or higher durability.

**Hint**: Use the optional Find Weak Spot rule if you're wielding a knife.

### Non-lethal attacks?

No rules for it. Common suggestions:
- If the player declares they're pulling the blow and the attack brings the NPC to 0 HP, they're unconscious (not dead).
- Unarmed attacks are likely non-lethal.

> p50: "When an NPC reaches zero HP, no death rolls are made – the GM decides whether the person lives or dies."

### Do you need HA Throwing Arm to throw weapons?

No. The appropriate weapon skill covers throwing for weapons designed to be thrown. HA Throwing Arm is for tossing melee weapons that are **not** normally ranged.

### Can a prone character perform attacks or free attacks?

Yes. Nothing in the book prevents it.

### Does a prone character count as in cover with bane on ranged attacks?

Nothing in the rules. **Suggestion**: It would make sense to impose a bane on ranged attacks against a prone character at distance.

### Can I spend a round to aim with a ranged weapon to get a boon?

Nothing in the rules. **Suggestion**: Spending your round to aim allows you to gain a boon on the ranged attack.

### Using ranged weapons while in cover?

RAW: Either you have a bane, or the enemy can't target you. Cover is simple but functional.

A free "switch of position" to lean out and fire (once per turn) is a reasonable interpretation; you are partially obscured when doing so.

### Can a Parry Movement push an opponent into a danger zone?

> p46 Parry Movement: "When successfully parrying an attack, you can move both yourself and the enemy 2 meters (one square) in any direction. These movements follow the usual rules, but do not count toward anyone's movement in the round and trigger no free attacks from anyone."

By RAW, yes — you have complete control and can move the NPC into flames, acid, over cliff edges, etc.

**Suggestion**: Require the player to state intent before the Parry roll, with a Bane to succeed. A normal Parry would not force an opponent into danger.

### What happens if a PC dodges the first attack and moves 2m away from an attacker with attacks left?

Yes, if you dodge out of range, the remaining attacks from that turn fail.

### Ranged critical hits and shields?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Yes, you can use a shield to try to block ranged critical hits.

> p49 "(or parried with a shield)"

## Free Attacks

### Roll or auto-hit when an NPC does a free attack?

You have to roll for the attack as usual. — *Tomas (Free League)*

### Unlimited free attacks?

No ruling, but it makes sense to limit free attacks to one per triggering event.

### Involuntary movement triggering free attacks?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No.

> p43: "Also note that involuntary movement never triggers a free attack."

### If a NPC is within 2 meters of two separate PCs and tries to flee, does it invoke Free Attacks from each?

Two separate Evade rolls (one per PC), stating which free attack they're evading before each roll.

### How many Free attack Evades can a player do in a turn?

No limit.

### Triggering Free Attacks at 4 meters with Long weapons?

**RAW**: No — free attacks are about 2m control zone, not weapon length.

**Suggestion**: Yes — it's about the space you control with your weapon.

### If I move on my turn but take no action, can I later in the round parry or dodge?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No.

> p42: "You must perform your action and move on your turn – you cannot save any of them for later."
> p42: "This uses up your own turn in the round, which means that you cannot perform a reaction if you have already had your 'turn.'"
> p42: Parry and Dodge "...is a reaction that takes place outside your turn and replaces your normal turn in the round."

### If I do nothing on my turn, can I later in the round parry or dodge?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No. Same ruling as above — your turn has passed.

### Using a reaction loses Move also?

Yes, you lose your movement in the round as well (p43). If you dodge or parry, you do get some movement (2m).

### Parrying and Dodge movement — do they create free attacks?

No.

> p46 and p47: "These movements do not trigger free attacks from anyone."

## Sneak Attacks

### Can I Sneak Attack with ranged weapons?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Yes.

> "It can be done with both ranged and melee weapons, but never during active combat (except with the Backstabbing heroic ability)."
> Note: You do not get the extra damage die since that requires a Subtle weapon, and there are no Subtle ranged weapons.

### Can I sneak attack with a Long weapon from 4m?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Yes.

> p43 now says "...within 2 meters (for most weapons)"

### Can you use a Sneak Attack during active combat?

No. Sneak Attacks are pre-combat surprise only (before initiative has been drawn). The "draw initiative" text in the rule is the key. In ongoing combat, use the Backstab heroic ability.

### Does the Sneak attack extra damage die increase the damage bonus?

No. The sneak attack increases the total damage by one weapon die — not the damage bonus.

## Grapple

### Why is it "a free action for the enemy" to try escaping from Grapple?

The English text is a mistranslation. The Swedish version says: "The roll is **an action** for the enemy but not for you." (Not a free action — it costs the grappled character an action to try to break free.)

### What's Break?

Break is an unarmed attack with a boon that cannot be dodged or parried, performed while maintaining a grapple. It's an action.

### I'm Grappled — can I cast a spell with only Word as the requisite?

> p48: "As long as you maintain your grip the enemy can neither move nor perform any action that requires body movement, except trying to break free."

No official ruling yet. Being grappled and held may be too restrictive for concentration.

### Grapple when no action left — do they go free?

No. Keeping the hold is a free action. The grappled person must use an action to try to break free; the grappler uses a free action to maintain.

**Grapple Example**:
- Round 1, Init 1: A grapples B (action for A, free action for B). If A wins, A has grip.
- Round 1, Init 2: B tries to break free (action for B, free action for A). If B loses, A keeps grip.
- Round 2, Init 2: A uses Break (special unarmed attack with boon, cannot dodge/parry).

### Can Find Weak Spot be used for ranged weapons?

No, melee only.

### Helping others in combat

When a character helps, both parties are acting on the higher initiative (the helper goes first, or the helped character delays). If the helper dies before the helped character acts, the boon is lost.

Two approaches:
1. Character B goes first, declares assist, flips card — Character A gets boon when attacking.
2. Character A goes first, Character B declares assist before A rolls, B flips card — A rolls with boon.

## Damage, Poison and Death {#damage-poison-and-death}

### Order of applying Resistance & Armor Rating?

Armor first, then Resistance. As a rule of thumb, always apply resistance last — it's the creature's inherent toughness, not the armor's job.

### Armor Rating and HP of inanimate objects?

From the official adventures (examples):

| Object | AR / HP |
|--------|---------|
| Chain | 9 / 20 |
| Barrel | 3 / 10 |
| Tool Box | 6 / 25 |
| Chest (light) | 5 / 25 |
| Chest (heavy) | 10 / 20 |
| Trapdoor | 10 / 20 |
| Padlock | 5 / 20 |
| Weak door | 5–6 / 10 |
| Rusty iron cell doors | 12 / 20 |
| Rusted iron door | 6 / 30 |
| Sturdy barricade | 12 / 100 |
| Red Oak Gate | 12 / 40 |
| Old Bronze Gate | 18 / 80 |
| Sturdy Iron Framed Gate | 25 / 100 |
| Portcullis | 10 / 30 |

### Armor damage to weapons — unarmed?

> p50: If the damage from a melee attack is completely negated, the attacking weapon itself suffers the damage instead.

For unarmed attacks: **Suggestion** — the unarmed attacker takes D4 damage to themselves if the attack is completely negated.

### Area of effect attacks on prone characters?

The extra D6 on prone characters is only for melee attacks (under the melee combat section). It does not apply to AoE attacks.

### Does Armor protect against fire damage and fire spells?

Yes. Individual spells or monster attacks state if armor has no effect. If not in the description, armor protects normally. Dragon fire is the only exception in the core rulebook.

### Lethal Poison doesn't kill?

No, that's a misunderstanding. Lethal poison gets your HP to 0. You make death rolls when you reach 0 HP.

### Can Monsters be poisoned?

No. p52: "Poison has no effect on monsters."

**Suggestion**: Make a house-rule with poison specific to a monster. Treat their CON as 15 for the opposed roll.

### Are you cured from Poison after successful death rolls?

Yes. The poison stops damaging when you reach 0 HP and start doing death rolls.

### Can a character potentially rally multiple times in a single combat?

Yes. Death saves reset every time they get back above 0 HP. Additional damage counts as failed death saves; at 0 HP they drop to the ground and need to rally again.

### Can a character already doing death rolls suffer instant death?

Yes. If at 0 HP and you take damage equal to or greater than your maximum HP, you die immediately (no further death rolls).

### Does new damage cause automatic failed death rolls?

Yes. Characters at 0 HP automatically fail one death roll for each instance of damage they take.

### Death rolls with bane when Sickly?

Yes.

### Continue death rolls after combat is over?

RAW (p50): Once combat is over, keep counting rounds making death saves until resolved. Other PCs can intervene to try to save a character through magic or a Healing roll (no bane outside of combat).

## Magic

### How to become a Mage after character creation?

> p61: "You can learn additional schools of magic by first acquiring the heroic ability Magic Talent (p38), and then studying under a teacher for at least a week. At the end of the week, make a roll against INT. On success, get your base chance (INT) as the skill level. On failure, try again after another week. Even non-mages can learn magic this way."

### No mage skill base chance?

Mages start with a single magic school treated as "trained" (p19). All secondary skills (including magic schools) have a 0 base chance unless trained. A trained school skill is doubled from your INT-based chance.

### How to learn a new spell beyond your base chance limit?

You can hold a number of prepared spells equal to your base chance of INT. Spells are stored in your grimoire. If you lose your grimoire, all currently prepared spells stay prepared, but you can't swap/change spells until you get a new one.

### Upper limit to powering up a spell?

Spells are cast at Power Level 1, 2 or 3, with WP cost of 2, 4, or 6. Some spells don't use Power Levels and always cost 2. Magic Tricks cost 1 WP.

### Power from body when 1 WP left?

Yes. Errata in v1.3, p58: WP can be drawn from the body even with one WP left.

### Is Power Level = Rank?

No. Power Level is chosen at casting time and affects how powerful the spell is. Rank indicates spell complexity and the order in which spells must be learned (Rank 1 → Rank 2, etc.).

### Can Power from the Body kill you?

Yes. The damage is not based on your HP — it's based on the die you rolled. Rolling a D20 and getting 18 deals 18 damage regardless of your HP. This can be outright lethal.

> p58: "Roll a die of your choice (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, or D20) before casting the spell – the result indicates how many WP you gain and must use straight away, but you also take the same amount of damage."

### How to count Spell range on a 2×2 grid?

Each square = 2×2 meters (per p44 sidebar). Your own square is not counted.

| Distance | Squares |
|----------|---------|
| 2m | 1 square |
| 4m | 2 squares |
| 6m | 3 squares |
| 10m | 5 squares |
| 20m | 10 squares |
| 30m | 15 squares |

### Cast Protector spell on caster?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Yes.

> p62: "You can cast the spell on yourself."

### Protector power level 3 = immune to magic?

No. At power level 3, a monster's magical attack only hits if it uses 4 or more hit dice.

### Advancement marks, Dragon rolls and Enchant Weapon spell?

Yes, you get advancement marks from rolling a 2 on an Enchanted Weapon (since 2 counts as a Dragon roll). This is the literal RAW interpretation. — *Tomas (Free League)*

### Casting spells when in Melee — gets Bane?

No. — *Fenhorn (FL Moderator)*

(p49 rule about ranged weapons near enemies does **not** apply to spells.)

### Does metal affect spells already cast (like Flight)?

No. If the spell has already been cast, it's not affected by metal.

### Enchant Weapon spell and touching a Metal weapon?

As long as the mage doesn't hold the weapon **during** spellcasting and only touches it at the end, it's okay. — *Fenhorn (FL Moderator)*

### What are the requirements of magic tricks?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: A Gesture.

> p58: "All magic tricks require a gesture to be cast." (It cannot be done secretly.)

### Magic Trick "Flick" killing people and monsters — abuse?

By RAW, Flick deals 1 damage to creatures and costs 1 WP. At minimum Gesture requirement (Errata), people can make Awareness rolls to spot the caster.

**Suggestions**:
1. Flick doesn't do damage to creatures (objects only)
2. Only damage a creature once per day/hour
3. Keep as-is; casting requires a clear gesture or strong word (detectable)

### Can you whisper a Spell with Word as requisite?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: No.

> p58: "It cannot be a whisper."

## Gear

### How does the Gear Boons and Banes table work?

| Situation | Roll Type |
|-----------|-----------|
| No required OR boon item | Bane |
| Required item only | Normal |
| Boon item only (no required) | Normal (boon and implied bane cancel) |
| Both required AND boon item | Boon |

**Examples**:

| Activity | Bane (no gear) | Normal | Boon |
|----------|---------------|--------|------|
| Making camp (BUSHCRAFT) | None | Sleeping furs OR Tent | Sleeping furs AND Tent |
| Travelling (BUSHCRAFT) | None | Map OR Spyglass | Map AND Spyglass |
| Saving lives (HEALING) | None | Bandages OR Surgical Instruments | Both |
| Lockpicking (SLEIGHT OF HAND) | None | Simple Lockpicks | Advanced Lockpicks |

### What's the Durability of Armor?

Not in the core rules. **Suggestion**:

| Armor | Durability |
|-------|-----------|
| Leather | 3 |
| Studded Leather | 6 |
| Brigandine | 8 |
| Chainmail | 10 |
| Scale mail | 12 |
| Plate mail | 15 |
| Open helmet | 10 |
| Great helm | 12 |

### What's the weight of clothes?

No ruling. **Suggestion**: No weight while worn (like armor). Weight of 1 in a backpack.

### Can a Book give Boon in any skill, even weapons?

Books grant boons to a specific subject within a skill — not necessarily the entire skill. Examples:
- "Ancient Heroes and Villains" → boon on MYTHS & LEGENDS related to heroes/villains only
- "Beast of the Cave — An Encyclopedia" → boon on BEAST LORE for cave creatures only

Books are meant to be deliberately situational. Let the title and narrative likelihood guide pricing and applicability.

## Monsters

### Monster skills — Awareness and Sneak?

> p84: "Monsters can have skills, but they are mainly used outside of combat or for opposed rolls – not for attacks. They only attack using their monster attacks."
> p84: "Roll against a default skill level of 15 for all dodges and parries by monsters."

Awareness: ~10–14 for animals, higher for peak predators. Sneaking: ~12–16 (lower for larger monsters).

### Do monsters trigger free attacks when they move away?

Yes. All movement away triggers a free attack. Monsters have Evade 15 unless specified.

### Do monsters get Free Attacks?

Yes. Clarified in Errata v1.3 p43: A monster that gets a free attack performs a **monster attack** (p83), which may also affect other characters.

### Do Monster Abilities trigger free attacks?

Only if the ability involves moving **away** from a PC (beyond 2m). Moving **around** a PC (staying within 2m) does not trigger free attacks. A monster that flies or runs by you and then leaves the 2m area triggers a free attack.

### How does monster size work on a battle map?

> p83: "Large: A large monster can block a 4×4 meter area…"

The monster **dominates** these squares (the area it controls) — not necessarily its exact physical footprint.

### Ferocity clarification

Ferocity = number of initiative cards drawn per round (essentially multiple turns per round). On each turn, the monster can move and do one attack. If it tries to dodge or parry, it loses one of its turns.

### Limits to Ferocity?

No limit. A monster with Ferocity equal to (number of PCs – 1) would have Ferocity 4 with 5 PCs.

### Monsters with no Ferocity?

Those monsters function as NPCs mechanically (draw 1 initiative card). Goblins and Orcs are examples.

### Do all Monsters do Fear attacks?

No. Fear attacks are one type of attack most monsters have. If a monster is so scary that just seeing it would trigger a Fear attack, it would be written as a passive ability.

### Can Monsters move every turn?

Yes. Note that Monster movement is per **turn**, not per round.

> p83: "Note that this score refers to movement per turn for monsters, not per round."

### When does a Monster roll for their monster attack?

At the **beginning** of each Monster turn. The GM rolls or selects the attack, then moves the Monster before and/or after the attack as appropriate.

### Monster abilities that restrain

A monster maintaining a grip/restraint can still act on subsequent turns (the grip itself doesn't cost an action to maintain for the monster). Monsters don't follow the same action economy as players. Example: a Troll biting a PC still has arms free to strike.

## NPCs

### Stats and Damage Bonus meaning in Bestiary?

**Rulebook Errata 2.0**: Changed notation for clarity.
- OLD: Damage Bonus: AGL+D4
- NEW: Damage Bonus AGL: +D4

It means a +D4 Damage Bonus based on Agility — not AGL score + D4.

NPCs rarely make attribute rolls. The primary ones needed are CON & WIL to resist spell effects. If a specific attribute is needed, there's a box on p106 explaining how to calculate it.

### Low NPC skill values?

NPCs have ~5 in most skills (untrained). Skills from their profession are doubled (~10–14). GMs can often simply decide whether an NPC succeeds or fails based on narrative fit — you don't need to roll for everything.

### Poison against NPC with HA Robust?

> p52: "For poison effects on NPCs, roll against their maximum HP, reduced by 2 for each level of the Robust heroic ability."

The HP is reduced (not the poison potency) to give you the equivalent CON number for the opposed roll.

## General Discussion

### Camping and Bushcraft

Step-by-step camp procedure:
1. Find a "safe" place (no nearby enemies). Boon for especially safe locations like caves.
2. (Optional) Divide the watch: Some sleep, some keep watch, some hunt or cook.
3. Each sleeping character rolls BUSHCRAFT. No gear = automatic Bane. Sleeping fur = ignore the Bane. Tent = one character rolls with Boon; one small tent covers 1 other, large tent covers 5 others.
4. Success = good night's sleep. Failure = try again next shift (but event tables may trigger as time passes).

6 hours of sleep (in any combination, uninterrupted) is sufficient to avoid exhaustion.

### Opposed Roll Mechanic

RAW favors the active party — the opposing party's skill level only matters when it's lower than the active party's roll.

This is a deliberate design decision: "low roll good, lowest roll best." The math still heavily favors higher skill (a Skill 15 character wins most of the time against Skill 5), but a character with a 5 always has a slim chance if they roll very low.

**Alternative methods**:
- **Pendragon style**: Highest roll **under** skill level wins.
- **Counting difference**: The one with the largest difference between roll and skill level wins (e.g., Skill 10 rolls 4 = diff 6; Skill 18 rolls 11 = diff 7 → Skill 18 wins).

### Magic and Metal

> v1.3 p58: "Metal has an anti-magical effect, which means that you cannot use magic if you are wearing metal armor or have a metal weapon **at hand**. This includes partially metal items such as axes, spears, studded leather, and arrows (but not staves, clubs, or slings). Items in your inventory do not count."

**At hand** means worn on belt or readily available (up to 3 weapons).

Key rulings (from historical DoD editions + Fenhorn):
- A mage **can** wear leather armor
- A mage **can** use a mace or staff without issue
- A mage **can** carry iron in a backpack (not at hand)
- Tiny iron objects are exempt (a key, a few coins)
- A sheathed sword on the belt **counts** as at hand
- An arrowhead embedded in the mage — ruled as tiny (no effect)
- Metal floor/walls/cage — blocks spellcasting (no stone or wood nearby to stand on = no magic)
- Already-cast spells are not affected by metal contact

### How to Deal with Skill Roll Spam

Suggested approaches:
- One player rolls; others **Help** (grants a boon) rather than rolling independently.
- Require a **time cost** for additional attempts (each try takes a stretch).
- Roll event tables when time passes — repeated attempts have narrative consequences.
- Demon rolls on skill spam have consequences: wrong information, wasted time, damaged equipment.
- For Persuasion: Each additional party member trying after a failure imposes a Bane (the NPC already said no).

### Balancing Encounters

The game is not concerned with strict balance — the power curve is generally flat, with WP as a limiting factor.

Rough guidelines (number of enemies vs. PCs):
- **Fewer enemies**: PCs have the upper hand
- **Equal**: Depends on equipment and tactics; PCs may have the edge
- **Slightly more**: Enemies have the edge, but well-equipped/smart PCs can cope
- **Twice as many**: Enemies have a strong advantage
- **Outnumbering**: Likely lethal without something to even the odds

Use **Ferocity as a balance metric**. Keep total enemy actions per round (summed Ferocity) around 1–1.5× the number of PCs for a challenging but fair fight.

### Damage Types: Slashing, Piercing, Bludgeoning

These rules are **optional**. Summary:

| Damage Type | Armor Interaction | Critical Effect | Special |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|---------|
| Bludgeoning | Leather/studded leather +2 AR | — | Potential knockdown |
| Slashing | Chainmail +2 AR | — | Can hit multiple adjacent (with HA) |
| Piercing | — | Ignores armor AND natural armor | Find Weak Spot: bane on attack but enemy counts as armorless |

Piercing is strongest mechanically (armor bypass, Find Weak Spot). Slashing is considered underpowered by many in the community — house-rule buffs such as a bleeding condition are common.

### Selling Gear from Encounters

No official sell-back rules. Common house-rules:
- Enemy gear is **broken** from the fight: sell for half price (unless repaired via Crafting)
- Use the Bartering skill for negotiations
- Suggested base value: **1/4 of original cost** (Bartering success → 1/2; Dragon roll → full price; Demon roll → no buyers)
