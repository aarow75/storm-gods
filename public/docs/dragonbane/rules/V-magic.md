# Chapter 5: Magic

## Schools of Magic

Magic in Dragonbane is organized into distinct schools, each with its own philosophy and approach.

### General Magic

Common spells accessible to all mages, covering utility, healing, and basic effects.

### Animism

Magic focused on spirits, animals, and natural forces.

### Elementalism

Magic that controls and manipulates the elements: fire, water, air, and earth.

### Mentalism

Magic that affects the mind, including telepathy, mind control, and illusions.

## Spells

Spells are magical effects that a caster can produce by expending magical energy.

### What is a Spell?

A spell is a discrete magical effect that can be cast as an action in combat or outside of combat. Each spell has specific mechanics and effects.

### Learning Spells

Characters can learn spells through training, discovery, or by finding spell scrolls.

## Casting Spells

The process of casting a spell involves specific steps and requirements.

### Spell Casting

1. Declare that you are casting a spell and identify which spell
2. Expend the required magical resources (spell slots or mana)
3. Roll for spell accuracy or the target makes a save
4. Apply the spell's effects

### Spell Slots

Spellcasters have spell slots that determine how many spells they can cast before needing rest.

### Spell Save DC

Some spells require targets to make saving throws. The spell's save difficulty is determined by the caster's abilities and the spell's power.

### Concentration

Some spells require concentration, meaning the caster cannot cast other concentration spells or lose the effect if they take damage.

## Learning Magic

How characters can improve their magical abilities and learn new spells.

### Spell Training

A character can spend time and resources training with a mentor to learn new spells.

### Spell Scrolls

Spells can be found written on magical scrolls, which can be learned or used directly.

### Experimentation

Advanced spellcasters may discover new spells through research and experimentation.

## Spell List

### General Magic

Spells accessible to all schools of magic:

- **Arcane Missile** — Launch magical projectiles at a target
- **Detect Magic** — Sense the presence of magical auras
- **Light** — Create or enhance light in an area
- **Mage Hand** — Create an invisible magical hand to manipulate objects
- **Magic Missile** — Hurl magical force at enemies
- **Prestidigitation** — Minor magical effects and tricks
- **Read Magic** — Understand magical writing and glyphs
- **Shield** — Protect yourself with magical barriers

### Animism

Spells focused on spirits, animals, and nature:

- **Animal Friendship** — Gain the trust and aid of animals
- **Animal Messenger** — Send messages via animals
- **Awaken** — Grant sentience to creatures
- **Bark Skin** — Harden skin like tree bark
- **Beast Sense** — Perceive through an animal's senses
- **Call Animals** — Summon wild creatures to aid you
- **Commune with Nature** — Speak with natural features
- **Speak with Animals** — Understand and communicate with animals
- **Summon Beast** — Summon a magical creature to fight for you

### Elementalism

Spells that control the elements:

- **Burning Hands** — Launch flames at enemies
- **Chain Lightning** — Conduct lightning between multiple targets
- **Chill Touch** — Drain life force with cold
- **Cone of Cold** — Unleash a blast of frozen air
- **Control Water** — Manipulate water in various ways
- **Fireball** — Explode with massive flames
- **Frost Armor** — Protect yourself with ice
- **Ice Storm** — Create a storm of ice shards
- **Lightning Bolt** — Hurl a bolt of electricity
- **Wall of Fire** — Create a barrier of flames
- **Wall of Ice** — Create a barrier of ice

### Mentalism

Spells that affect the mind:

- **Charm Person** — Make a creature regard you favorably
- **Command** — Issue a magical command
- **Detect Thoughts** — Perceive surface thoughts
- **Dominate Person** — Control a creature's actions
- **Fear** — Instill terror in creatures
- **Hold Person** — Paralyze a creature
- **Hypnotic Pattern** — Create a mesmerizing pattern
- **Invisibility** — Make yourself unseen
- **Mind Blank** — Protect your mind from intrusion
- **Modify Memory** — Alter a creature's memories
- **Phantasmal Killer** — Create deadly illusions
- **Suggestion** — Plant an idea in a creature's mind
