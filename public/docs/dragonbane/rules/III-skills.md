# Chapter 3: Skills

## Roll the Dice

The core mechanic of Dragonbane involves rolling a d20 and comparing the result to a target number. Understanding how and when to roll dice is fundamental to playing the game.

### When to Roll

Your Game Master will tell you when a skill check is required. You roll dice when:
- Your success is uncertain
- The outcome matters to the story
- Failure creates interesting consequences

### How to Roll

1. Roll a d20
2. Add your skill bonus (derived from your profession and attributes)
3. Compare the result to the target number set by your Game Master
4. If your result meets or exceeds the target, you succeed

## Boons & Banes

Circumstances may provide advantages (boons) or disadvantages (banes) to your rolls.

### Boons

A boon grants you additional dice or a bonus to your roll, representing favorable circumstances, help from allies, or specific training.

### Banes

A bane imposes a penalty or disadvantage to your roll, representing unfavorable circumstances, injuries, or poor conditions.

### Combining Boons and Banes

If you have both boons and banes, they cancel each other out one-for-one until only one type remains.

## Opposed Rolls

Some situations require two characters to oppose each other. In an opposed roll:
1. Both characters roll relevant skill checks
2. The character with the higher result wins
3. The degree of success matters for determining consequences

## The Core Skills

Core skills are the most frequently used abilities in the game. All characters have access to these skills, though some professions excel at them.

### Combat Skills
Skills related to fighting with weapons and tactics.

### Survival Skills
Skills related to wilderness, tracking, and outdoor survival.

### Social Skills
Skills related to persuasion, deception, and interaction with others.

### Knowledge Skills
Skills related to understanding lore, history, and specialized knowledge.

### Craft Skills
Skills related to creating, repairing, and crafting items.

## Heroic Abilities

Heroic abilities are special powers unique to your character that represent exceptional training or innate talents. Unlike regular skills, heroic abilities often have dramatic effects and can turn the tide of battle.

### Using Heroic Abilities

Heroic abilities typically have specific triggers or conditions that allow them to be used. Some can be used multiple times per day, while others are more limited.

### Learning New Heroic Abilities

As your character gains experience, you can acquire new heroic abilities that reflect your growth and development.
