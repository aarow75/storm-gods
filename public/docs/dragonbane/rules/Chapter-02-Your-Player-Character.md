# CHAPTER 2 – YOUR PLAYER CHARACTER

The adventurer you play in *Dragonbane* is called your *player character*. They are your avatar, your eyes and ears in the game world. Over the course of the game your player character will change and grow. Their skills can be improved through experience, but you may also see their personality evolve in ways that cannot be measured in numbers.

> **CREATING YOUR CHARACTER**
>
> 1. Choose or roll your **kin**
> 2. Note your **innate ability**
> 3. Choose or roll your **profession**
> 4. Choose or roll your **age**
> 5. Choose or roll your **name**
> 6. Roll your **attributes**
> 7. Calculate your **derived ratings**
> 8. Choose your **trained skills**
> 9. Note your **heroic ability**
> 10. Choose or roll your **weakness** (optional)
> 11. Choose or roll your **gear**
> 12. Choose or roll your **memento** (optional)
> 13. Choose or roll your **appearance**

## LANGUAGE

All player characters can speak, read, and write a common tongue. Non-human kin often have their own languages. To understand other languages, the LANGUAGES skill is used.

### Human

Humans are the last-born. The other kin have songs and legends about times before the dawn of humanity. Today the humans have spread across much of the known world. Many are peasants; others are warriors or adventurers: fearless humans who venture into the unknown in search of glory, gold, and knowledge.

### Halfling

Halflings are short humanoids often found in hilly farmlands where they live in earthen dwellings with well-stocked larders. They are modest and amiable by nature, with a natural affinity for hearth and home. Because of their vulnerability and fear of "the tall ones," they are good at staying hidden and quickly getting out of critical situations.

Dwarves are the oldest of the elder folk, at least according to their own chroniclers. ## AGE

Age is divided into three categories: young, adult, and old. Older age means lower attributes but higher skill ratings.

| D6 | AGE | TRAINED SKILLS* | ATTRIBUTES† |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Young | 6+2 | AGL and CON +1 |
| 4–5 | Adult | 6+4 | — |

*Six trained skills must be selected from your profession, the rest chosen freely. † Up to a maximum of 18.

## NAME

Each kin has a list of six names. Choose or roll one, or make up your own. **Nicknames** are a common custom for adventurers; a number of typical nicknames are listed by your profession.

## ATTRIBUTES

Your adventurer has six base attributes on a scale from 3 to 18. The higher the score, the better.

**Starting Scores:** Roll 4D6 and remove the worst die. Assign that score to an attribute of your choice. Repeat five more times. Once done you may swap two scores. Then adjust attributes based on your age (maximum 18 in any attribute).

- **Strength (STR):** Raw muscle power.
- **Constitution (CON):** Physical fitness and resilience.
- **Agility (AGL):** Body control, speed, and fine motor skills.
- **Intelligence (INT):** Mental acuity, intellect, and reasoning skills.
- **Willpower (WIL):** Self-discipline and focus.
- **Charisma (CHA):** Force of personality and empathy.

> **OTHER METHODS**
>
> There are many other ways to determine attributes, such as rolling all of them and distributing the results freely. As long as you all agree in the group, you can use any method you prefer.

## ENCUMBRANCE

You can carry a number of items equal to half your STR (rounded up) in your Inventory without difficulty.

**Weapons at Hand:** Up to three weapons at hand do not count toward encumbrance. Shields count as weapons.

**Helmet & Armor:** Worn helmet and armor do not count toward encumbrance.

**Food:** Up to four rations count as one item.

**Arrows & Slingstones:** Each quiver counts as one item. Slingstones need not be noted.

**Backpack:** Increases carrying capacity by 2 (only one at a time).

**Heavy Items:** Items with weight 2, 3, 4+ take up that many inventory rows.

**Tiny Items:** Do not affect encumbrance. Coins under 100 are tiny; 100–199 = 1 item, 200–299 = 2, etc.

**Over-Encumbered:** Make a STR roll to move in combat or walk a shift. Fail = drop items or stay put. Carrying another person = automatically over-encumbered.

## EXPERIENCE

**Advancement Marks:** When you roll a dragon or demon when using a skill, tick the check box next to that skill. At the end of the game session, the GM asks:

✦ Did you participate in the game session?
✦ Did you explore a new location?
✦ Did you defeat one or more dangerous adversaries?
✦ Did you overcome an obstacle without using force?
✦ Did you give in to your weakness (optional rule)?

For each "yes" you can justify, place an advancement mark next to an unmarked skill of your choice.

**Advancement Rolls:** Roll D20 for each mark. If the result *exceeds* your current skill level, it increases by one (max 18). Erase marks after rolling.

**Teacher:** A shift of training with a teacher (skill 15+ and higher than yours) grants an extra advancement roll immediately. A teacher can only raise a skill by one per period of training.

**Heroic Abilities:** You earn a new heroic ability when:

✦ A skill reaches 18.
✦ After a grand heroic deed (rare; GM or adventure awards it).

> **OVERCOME WEAKNESS:** If you acted clearly against your weakness during a session, you get two advancement marks instead of one, and you overcome and must remove your weakness. After one full session without a weakness, choose a new one.
