# Chapter 2: Your Player Character

## Kin

Your character's kin represents their ancestry and cultural background. Choose one of the available kin to define your character's heritage and inherent abilities.

Available Kin:
- Dragonborn
- Dwarf
- Elf
- Halfling
- Human
- Orc
- Tiefling

## Profession

Your profession determines your character's training, skills, and abilities. Select a profession that resonates with your character concept.

### Artisan

Skilled craftspeople and makers, artisans excel at creating and repairing items.

### Bard

Charismatic performers who combine music, magic, and charm to influence the world around them.

### Fighter

Warriors trained in combat, fighters are masters of weapons and armor.

### Hunter

Skilled trackers and ranged combatants who are comfortable in wilderness environments.

### Knight

Noble warriors bound by codes of honor and chivalry, knights are protectors and champions.

### Mage

Scholarly spellcasters who study and master the arcane arts.

### Mariner

Sailors and seafarers experienced in naval combat and maritime navigation.

### Merchant

Traders and businesspeople skilled in commerce, negotiation, and acquisition of goods.

### Scholar

Intellectuals and researchers dedicated to knowledge and learning.

### Thief

Cunning rogues specializing in stealth, theft, and subterfuge.

## Age

Determine your character's age, which may influence their experience and capabilities.

## Name

Choose a name for your character that fits their kin and background.

## Attributes

Your character has six core attributes that define their capabilities:

### Strength (STR)
Raw physical power and muscle.

### Dexterity (DEX)
Agility, balance, and hand-eye coordination.

### Constitution (CON)
Endurance, health, and resistance to hardship.

### Intelligence (INT)
Reasoning, memory, and knowledge.

### Wisdom (WIS)
Perception, insight, and awareness.

### Charisma (CHA)
Force of personality, leadership, and charm.

## Derived Ratings

These statistics are calculated from your attributes and represent important character capabilities.

### Hit Points
Your character's health and ability to withstand damage.

### Armor Class
Your character's resistance to being hit in combat.

### Movement
How far your character can move in a round.

### Initiative
Your character's readiness for combat.

## Skills

Skills represent specific areas of training and expertise. Your profession and attributes determine your available skills and bonuses.

## Heroic Abilities

Special abilities that represent your character's unique talents and training. These abilities can turn the tide of battle or solve otherwise impossible challenges.

## Gear

Your starting equipment and possessions. This typically includes armor, weapons, and tools relevant to your profession.

## Encumbrance

The weight and bulk of items your character carries. Excessive encumbrance can affect your movement and actions.

## Appearance

Describe your character's physical appearance, including hair, eye color, notable features, scars, or distinctive marks.

## Experience

Your character begins with a certain amount of experience points that can be spent to improve abilities and learn new skills.
