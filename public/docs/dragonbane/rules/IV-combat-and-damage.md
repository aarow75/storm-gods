# Chapter 4: Combat & Damage

## Rounds & Initiative

Combat is organized into rounds, with each round representing approximately 6 seconds of action.

### Initiative

At the start of combat, all participants roll for initiative to determine turn order.

1. Roll a d20 and add your Initiative modifier
2. The Game Master announces the turn order from highest to lowest
3. Each character takes their turn in order

### Round Structure

Each round consists of all combatants taking their turns in initiative order.

## Actions & Movement

During your turn in combat, you can take actions, move, and perform other activities.

### Action

You can take one action per turn. Actions include attacking with a weapon, casting a spell, or using an ability.

### Movement

You can move up to your character's movement speed during your turn.

### Bonus Action

Some abilities or spells grant you a bonus action, allowing you to do something additional on your turn.

### Reaction

Reactions can be taken at any time during anyone's turn, allowing you to respond to events or opportunities.

## Melee Combat

Combat with nearby enemies using weapons and abilities.

### Making an Attack

1. Roll a d20 and add your attack bonus
2. Compare the result to the target's Armor Class
3. If you hit, roll damage and apply it to the target
4. If you miss, the attack has no effect

### Hit Locations

In Dragonbane, attacks can target specific locations on an opponent's body, potentially affecting their abilities.

### Armor and Protection

Armor increases your Armor Class, making you harder to hit. Different armor types offer varying levels of protection.

## Ranged Combat

Combat with distant enemies using bows, crossbows, spells, or other ranged weapons.

### Range

Ranged attacks have a normal range and a long range. Attacks beyond long range automatically fail.

### Cover

Cover provides bonuses to your Armor Class against ranged attacks.

### Line of Sight

You cannot attack targets you cannot see.

## Damage

When an attack hits, the attacker rolls damage based on their weapon or ability.

### Damage Types

Different damage types have different effects:
- Slashing
- Piercing
- Bludgeoning
- Fire
- Cold
- Lightning
- Poison

### Applying Damage

Damage reduces the target's Hit Points. When Hit Points reach 0, the character falls unconscious.

### Critical Hits

Rolling a natural 20 on an attack roll results in a critical hit, dealing double damage.

## Conditions

Temporary effects that modify a character's capabilities.

### Unconscious

When a character's Hit Points reach 0, they become unconscious and cannot take actions.

### Poisoned

A poisoned character has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

### Frightened

A frightened character has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks while the source of fear is visible.

### Paralyzed

A paralyzed character cannot move or speak and automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saves.

## Healing & Resting

Recovery between battles and long-term health management.

### Short Rest

A short rest lasts 1 hour. During a short rest, a character can recover some Hit Points.

### Long Rest

A long rest lasts 8 hours. During a long rest, a character fully recovers Hit Points and can prepare new spells.

### Healing Magic

Spells and abilities can restore Hit Points instantly.

## Other Hazards

Environmental dangers and challenges beyond combat.

### Falling

Falling damage is determined by the distance fallen.

### Fire

Exposure to flames causes burn damage.

### Poison

Ingesting or being exposed to poison can cause damage and conditions.

### Extreme Weather

Harsh environmental conditions can cause exhaustion and damage.

## Riding Animals

Travel and combat while mounted on animals.

### Mounts

Some characters may have trained mounts to ride into battle or across long distances.

### Mounted Combat

Special rules apply when attacking while mounted.

## Improvised Weapons

Using items not normally intended as weapons in combat.

### Effectiveness

Improvised weapons deal reduced damage compared to manufactured weapons.

### Creativity

The Game Master may allow creative use of improvised weapons with variable results.
