# Chapter 6: Gear

## Armor & Helmets

Protective equipment that increases your defense in combat.

### Light Armor
- **Leather Armor** — AC +1, minimal encumbrance
- **Studded Leather** — AC +2, slightly heavier leather
- **Hide Armor** — AC +2, made from animal hides
- **Chain Shirt** — AC +3, flexible chain

### Medium Armor
- **Scale Armor** — AC +4, overlapping scales
- **Breastplate** — AC +4, full chest protection
- **Half-Plate** — AC +5, combines plate and mail
- **Chain Mail** — AC +5, interlocked rings

### Heavy Armor
- **Plate Armor** — AC +6, full body metal coverage
- **Field Plate** — AC +7, optimized for mounted knights

### Helmets
- **Leather Helm** — AC +1
- **Metal Helm** — AC +1, better protection
- **Full Face Helm** — AC +2, complete head protection

## Melee Weapons

Weapons designed for close combat.

### Unarmed
- **Fist** — 1d4 damage, always available

### Light Weapons
- **Dagger** — 1d4 damage, can throw
- **Hand Axe** — 1d6 damage, can throw
- **Warhammer** — 1d6 damage, versatile

### Medium Weapons
- **Axe** — 1d8 damage, requires two hands
- **Flail** — 1d8 damage, ignores some armor
- **Longsword** — 1d8 damage, versatile
- **Mace** — 1d8 damage, effective against armor
- **Quarterstaff** — 1d8 damage, requires two hands
- **Spear** — 1d8 damage, can throw

### Heavy Weapons
- **Greatsword** — 2d6 damage, requires two hands
- **Halberd** — 2d6 damage, reach weapon
- **Pike** — 2d6 damage, reach weapon
- **War Hammer** — 2d6 damage, requires two hands
