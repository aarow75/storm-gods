# Kal-Arath Weapons & Armor

All weapons in a size category deal the same damage, but their special abilities vary, allowing for customization and strategy beyond a simple "attack and dodge" round.

- **Dual wielding**: two weapons can be used simultaneously — roll both weapons' damage and take the best. Dual-wielding is only possible with a Medium and Light weapon, or two Light weapons.
- **Heavy weapons** are always two-handed.
- **Masterwork weapons** give +1 to attack or damage.
- **Magical weapons** are extremely rare and often come with one or more powerful abilities: roll attack or damage with advantage, damage explodes on a 5 or 6, or some other granted ability.

## Light Weapons

*Damage: d6/d — roll 2d6, take the worst.*

### Dagger
Can be thrown (AGI). Damage is d6/a (roll 2d6, take best) when grappling or when the enemy is prone. Cost 5s.

### Knife
As Dagger: can be thrown (AGI, range 10/20), damage at advantage when grappling or against prone enemies. Cost 3s.

### Hatchet
Can be thrown (range 10/20). On a critical, can forgo the extra damage die to automatically grapple the opponent. Cost 8s.

### Staff/Stick
On a critical, forgo extra damage to knock the enemy prone. Cost 2s.

### Brawling
Unarmed strikes deal d6/d. (The Way of the Sundering Fist pact spell rolls unarmed damage at advantage.)

## Medium Weapons

*Damage: d6.*

### Sword
On a critical Dodge success, immediately make a counterattack. Cost 30s.

### Battle Axe
On a critical, forgo extra damage to tear the enemy's weapon away. Cost 20s.

### Flail
Ignores shields. Cost 15s.

### Spear
Can interrupt anyone moving into attack range and attack them, regardless of initiative roll. Cost 10s.

### Mace
On a critical, forgo extra damage to stun the target for one round. Cost 25s.

## Heavy Weapons

*Damage: d6/a — roll 2d6, take the best. All Heavy weapons are two-handed.*

### Greataxe
On a critical, the enemy is immediately knocked prone. Cost 50s.

### Two-Handed Sword
On a killing blow, immediately attack again. Cost 70s.

### Maul
On a critical, forgo extra damage to stun the opponent for d6 rounds and knock them prone. Cost 40s.

## Missile Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Range | Special |
|--------|--------|-------|---------|
| Sling | d6/d | 30/60 | On 6 damage, instead of exploding, enemy is stunned 1 round |
| Javelin | d6 | 20/40 | On a hit, may forgo damage to destroy the enemy's shield |
| Shortbow | d6 | 30/60 | May move, attack, and move again |
| Longbow | d6 | 60/120 | May spend up to three rounds aiming, for +1 attack and damage per round spent |

## Shields

A shield gives **−1 damage reduction** and can be **sacrificed to reduce all damage from a single attack to 0**. Cost 15s. (The Flail ignores shields; a Javelin hit may destroy a shield.)

## Armor

Armor reduces incoming damage. Heavy armor should be nearly as rare as magical items — reserved for the most powerful champions and warlords.

| Type | Reduction | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Light Armor | −1 | Often piecemeal leather, studs, spikes, bronze plates, and so on |
| Medium Armor | −2 | Often lamellar, metal ring on leather, full suits, and so on |
| Heavy Armor | −3 | Almost always customized for the individual, comprised of foreign parts like chain and plate or scale |
| Shield | −1 | Can be sacrificed to negate all damage from one attack |
