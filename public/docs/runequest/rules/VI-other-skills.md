# VI. OTHER SKILLS

## Overview

Beyond combat and magic, adventurers pursue various non-combat professions and trades. These "other skills" represent specialized knowledge and training. This section covers skill mechanics and common professional guilds.

---

## 1. Introduction to Specialized Skills

### Non-Combat Skill Categories

The eight skill categories include three major non-combat categories:

1. **Knowledge Skills** — Lore, Navigation, Repair
2. **Communication Skills** — Languages, Bargaining, Persuasion, Oratory
3. **Perception Skills** — Tracking, Searching, Scanning, Listening

All other skills (Agility, Stealth, Manipulation) support both combat and non-combat activities.

### Skill Improvement

Non-combat skills improve the same way as combat skills:

1. **After an adventure**, if the skill was used, make an **experience roll**
2. **Roll D100** vs. current skill rating
3. **If roll is above current**: Increase by 1D6%
4. **If roll is below or equal**: No improvement

Some skills cannot improve through experience and must be **trained** by a mentor instead.

### Untrained Skill Use

If an adventurer lacks a specific skill, they may attempt it using the associated characteristic:

| Skill Category | Associated Characteristic | Untrained Roll |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge (Lore) | INT | INT × 5 |
| Communication | CHA | CHA × 5 |
| Perception | INT + POW | Average of both × 5 |
| Manipulation | STR or DEX | STR/DEX × 5 |

## 2. Knowledge Skills

### Lore Skills

Lore represents detailed knowledge about a specific subject:

| Lore Type | Subject | Base % |
|---|---|---|
| **Lore (World)** | Geography, history, cultures, famous people | 15% |
| **Lore (Animal)** | Fauna, animal behavior, hunting knowledge | 10% |
| **Lore (Plant)** | Flora, agriculture, herbalism | 10% |
| **Lore (Religion)** | Cult practices, mythology, religious history | 10% |
| **Lore (Craft)** | Specific crafts (blacksmithing, tailoring, etc.) | 5% |

**Advancement**: Lore skills do NOT improve through experience rolls. They must be:
- Learned from a teacher/mentor (takes weeks of study)
- Advanced through research and experimentation (GM discretion)

### Other Knowledge Skills

| Skill | Base % | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Navigation** | 15% | Finding direction, reading maps, wayfinding |
| **Repair** | 10% | Fixing broken items, maintaining equipment |
| **Appraisal** | 10% | Estimating value of goods and items |

## 3. Communication Skills

### Language Skills

All adventurers speak their native language at 50% base (often higher with modifiers).

| Skill | Base % | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Speak (Native)** | 50% (often 60-70% with bonuses) | Common tongue; always learned |
| **Speak (Other)** | 0–20% | Additional languages; each costs learning points |
| **Read/Write** | 0–20% | Literacy; not all cultures emphasize written language |

**Learning a new language**: Takes 1D6 weeks of intensive study; requires a teacher or immersion in the culture.

### Persuasion and Social Skills

| Skill | Base % | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Bargain** | 15% | Negotiate prices, trade value |
| **Intimidate** | 10% | Coerce through threat or fear |
| **Oratory** | 10% | Public speaking, leadership, group persuasion |
| **Persuade** | 15% | Convince someone through reason or charm |

**Social Skill Mechanics**:
- Roll D100 vs. skill rating
- Opposed by target's Insight (INT×5) or Willpower (POW×5)
- Success means target is swayed (extent depends on how much you won by)

## 4. General Notes on Languages

### Language Families

Languages in Glorantha belong to families with shared roots:

| Language Family | Examples | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Languages** | Sartar, Esrolian, Prax Common | Standard (1 week each) |
| **Old Languages** | Aurelion (ancient), Kalanthani (old empire) | Advanced (2 weeks each) |
| **Monster Languages** | Troll speech, dwarf runes, dragon languages | Very difficult (4+ weeks each) |

### Literacy

Reading and writing are separate from speaking:
- **Illiterate** (0% Read/Write) — Cannot read common text
- **Semi-literate** (1–30%) — Can read simple texts, names, basic writing
- **Literate** (31–70%) — Can read most texts; write clearly
- **Educated** (71%+) — Can read complex texts; write eloquently

Not all cultures emphasize written language. Some cultures are primarily oral traditions.

## 5. Thieves Association

### Thief Skills

Thieves specialize in stealth and sleight-of-hand:

| Skill | Base % | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Hide** | 10% | Conceal self in shadows or cover |
| **Move Quietly** | 10% | Walk silently; avoid detection by sound |
| **Climb** | 40% | Scale vertical surfaces |
| **Sleight of Hand** | 10% | Pickpocketing, palming objects, subtle manipulation |
| **Lockpicking** | 5% | Open locks without keys (requires tools) |

### Thief Code

Many thieves belong to organized Thief Associations that enforce:
- **Honor among thieves** — Don't steal from other association members
- **Protection fees** — Pay a cut of earnings to the association for protection and fencing stolen goods
- **Territory** — Respect other thieves' claimed territories

### Becoming a Thief

Thieves gain their occupational skills through:
- **Apprenticeship** — Years under a master thief (5+ years typical)
- **Training** — Association members teach new recruits
- **Experience** — Actual thievery and narrow escapes

Base thief skills start at occupational defaults (as stated in character creation section); advancement is through experience rolls.

## 6. Other Guilds and Brotherhoods

### Guild Types

| Guild | Focus | Base Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Alchemist's Guild** | Potion-making, herbalism, reagent extraction | Craft (Alchemy), Lore (Plant), Heal |
| **Crafters' Guild** | Smithing, carpentry, tailoring, leatherworking | Craft, Repair, Appraisal |
| **Merchants' Guild** | Trade, negotiation, appraisal, languages | Bargain, Read/Write, Speak (Other) |
| **Scholars' Conclave** | Knowledge, research, rare languages | Lore (any), Read/Write, Appraisal |
| **Maritime Brotherhood** | Sailing, navigation, swimming | Navigation, Swim, Ride (Swim) |
| **Foresters' Guild** | Tracking, hunting, animal lore | Track, Lore (Animal), Hide |
| **Armorers' Guild** | Armor repair, metalworking, appraisal | Craft (Armor), Repair, Appraisal |
| **Horsemasters' Guild** | Animal handling, horsemanship, breeding | Ride, Lore (Animal), Handle Animal |

### Guild Benefits

Guild membership typically provides:
- **Skill training** — Access to teachers and mentors
- **Resources** — Guild-affiliated suppliers for tools and materials
- **Reputation** — Recognition within the craft
- **Protection** — Guild enforces rules and protects members from exploitation
- **Advancement** — Hierarchical ranks (Apprentice → Journeyman → Master)

### Guild Ranks

| Rank | Requirements | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | Initial entry; 1–2 years service | Training, basic resources |
| **Journeyman** | Complete apprenticeship; demonstrate competence | Moderate access to resources; can take contracts |
| **Master** | 10+ years experience; proven excellence | Full resource access; can train apprentices; voting rights |

## 7. Alchemist's Guild

### What Is Alchemy?

Alchemy is the crafting of potions, salves, and reagents from natural materials. Alchemists:
- Extract essences from plants, minerals, and creatures
- Create healing potions and curative salves
- Brew poisons and toxins (for specific uses like hunting)
- Research and experiment with magical substances

### Alchemical Craft

**Craft (Alchemy)** base: 10% + Craft bonuses from INT and POW

**Creating an alchemical item requires**:
1. **Raw materials** — Ingredients sourced from nature or vendors
2. **Tools** — Alchemical lab or portable kit
3. **Time** — Varies by item complexity (1 day to 1 week typical)
4. **Successful roll** — Roll D100 vs. Craft (Alchemy)

**Success consequences**:
- **Critical**: Perfect result; might get multiple doses or extra effect
- **Special**: Good result; as intended
- **Normal**: Works as intended
- **Failure**: Ingredients wasted; item doesn't work
- **Fumble**: Explosion or toxic exposure; possible injury to crafter

## 8. Free Sages

### What Is a Free Sage?

Free Sages are independent scholars and knowledge-keepers who:
- Are not affiliated with guilds or cults
- Maintain libraries and collect rare knowledge
- Teach for fees rather than guild hierarchy
- Often study obscure lore and forbidden knowledge

### Free Sage Skills

Free Sages excel at:
- **Lore (any)** — Specialized knowledge
- **Read/Write** — Multiple languages
- **Appraisal** — Valuing rare items and texts
- **Research** — Finding information (spends time but generates insights)

### Learning from Free Sages

A character can hire a Free Sage to teach:
- **Cost**: Varies by rarity of knowledge (10–100+ Lunars per week)
- **Time**: 1–52 weeks depending on subject complexity
- **Result**: Gain +10% to target skill after completing study

### Performance and Entertainment

Players (actors/performers) and Minstrels (musicians) have skills in:

| Skill | Base % | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | 15% | Acting, dancing, entertaining |
| **Music** | 10% | Playing instruments; composing |
| **Storytelling** | 15% | Recounting tales, legends, gathering information |

### Minstrel Abilities

Minstrels gain special abilities:
- **Inspire courage** — Perform to boost allies' morale (+10% to rolls for 1 hour)
- **Gather rumors** — Performance grants access to local gossip and secrets
- **Understand cultures** — Music and stories reveal cultural knowledge

## Summary

**Key Takeaways**:
- Non-combat skills improve through experience rolls (except Lore)
- Lore skills must be trained or researched
- Guilds provide training, resources, and protection
- Language learning takes 1D6 weeks per language
- Special professions (Thief, Shaman, Minstrel) have unique abilities
- Untrained skill use rolls characteristics ×5
