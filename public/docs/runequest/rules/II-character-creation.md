# II. HOW TO CREATE AN ADVENTURER

## Overview

Character creation in RuneQuest involves defining a set of core characteristics that drive all abilities, selecting occupations and cults, calculating derived statistics, choosing skills, and gathering equipment.

---

## 1. Characteristics

Characteristics are the foundation of all adventurers. They range from 1–21 (or higher through magic) and represent inherent physical and mental attributes.

### The Seven Characteristics

| Characteristic | Abbreviation | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Strength** | STR | Damage bonus, carrying capacity, melee weapon effectiveness |
| **Constitution** | CON | Hit points, healing rate, disease/poison resistance |
| **Size** | SIZ | Physical mass, hit point modifiers, damage bonus modifier |
| **Dexterity** | DEX | Strike rank, weapon precision, dodge ability |
| **Intelligence** | INT | Reasoning, memory, skill learning, knowledge abilities |
| **Power** | POW | Spiritual presence, magic points, willpower, rune magic |
| **Charisma** | CHA | Leadership, influence, rune magic eligibility |

### Characteristic Rolling

Standard method: **Roll 3D6 for each characteristic** (resulting in 3–18).

Alternative method: **Select from Characteristic Sets** — Pick from six pre-generated characteristic sets during character creation (Step 3). Each set includes:
- Inclination traits (personality descriptors)
- Characteristic values
- Rune affinities (Elemental, Power, and Form Runes)
- Skill category modifiers
- Derived attributes

All modifiers from the chosen characteristic set are already incorporated; no further modification is needed.

---

## 2. Abilities and How Characteristics Affect Them

### The Core Resolution System

RuneQuest uses a **percentile (D100) system** where all actions are resolved against an ability percentage.

**Success Condition**: Roll equal to or less than the ability rating to succeed.
- **Natural 01–05**: Always succeeds, even if ability is lower than 5%
- **Natural 96–00**: Always fails, even if ability exceeds 95%

### Five Outcome Levels

| Outcome | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical Success** | Roll ≤ 1/20 of ability | Double advantage in opposed contests |
| **Special Success** | Roll ≤ 1/5 of ability | Extra advantage in opposed contests |
| **Success** | Roll ≤ ability rating | Standard success |
| **Failure** | Roll > ability rating | Action fails |
| **Fumble** | Roll ≤ 1/20 of failures | Worst outcome; often with complications |

### Using Characteristics for Untrained Actions

When no specific ability applies, use **characteristic rolls**: typically **characteristic ×5** on D100.
- **STR×5**: Strength checks (lifting, pushing)
- **CON×5**: Endurance tests (resisting poison, fatigue)
- **SIZ×5**: Size-based judgments
- **DEX×5**: Reflexes (catches, balance)
- **INT×5**: Logic and memory
- **POW×5**: Willpower and spiritual resistance
- **CHA×5**: Social appeal

---

## 3. Increasing Characteristics

Characteristics can increase through:
- **Experience rolls** (between adventures, using specific progression rules)
- **Magic** (temporary or permanent boosts from spells)
- **Aging** (sometimes increased through natural development, varies by age)

For mechanics on advancing characteristics between adventures, see **Between Adventures** rules.

## 4. Skills

### Skill Categories

Skills are organized into eight categories, each tied to specific characteristics:

| Category | Tied Characteristics | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Agility** | STR, SIZ, DEX, POW | Climb, Dodge, Jump, Swim |
| **Communication** | INT, POW, CHA | Bargain, Intimidate, Oratory, Persuade |
| **Knowledge** | INT, POW | Lore (various), Navigation, Repair |
| **Magic** | POW, CHA | Spirit Combat, Sorcery casting |
| **Manipulation** | STR, DEX, INT, POW | All Weapon Skills, Sleight of Hand, Craft skills |
| **Perception** | INT, POW | Scan, Sense, Track, Search |
| **Stealth** | SIZ, DEX, INT, POW | Conceal, Sneak, Hide |
| **Weapon Skills** | Uses Manipulation modifiers | Axe, Sword, Spear, Bow, etc. |

### Calculating Starting Skill Percentages

**Base Chance** + **Skill Category Modifier** = **Starting Skill %**

1. Each skill has a **base percentage** (e.g., Climb starts at 40%)
2. Add the **skill category modifier** from your characteristics (derived from the Characteristics Table during creation)
3. The result is your starting percentage
4. Minimum skill value: **00%** (zero chance)
5. Maximum before experience: **95%** (unless modified by magic or special rules)

**Example**: If your Manipulation modifier is +15% and your starting Sword skill base is 20%, your Sword skill is 20% + 15% = 35%.

### Skill Advancement

Between adventures, skills with a (☐) checkbox can be improved through:
- **Experience rolls**: After using a skill during an adventure, roll a D100. If above the current percentage, the skill increases by 1D6%.
- **Training**: Some skills must be trained by a teacher and cannot advance through experience alone

Lore skills and certain specialized knowledge cannot improve through experience; they must be learned or trained.

## 5. Money and Equipment

### Starting Resources

All adventurers begin with:
- **Movement Rate**: 8 MOV (see Section III: Mechanics and Melee)
- **Magic Points**: Equal to POW
- **Rune Points**: 3 (with restrictions on POW conversion)

### Equipment Acquisition

During character creation, adventurers receive equipment based on their occupation and background. Standard equipment includes:
- Armor (if applicable to occupation)
- Weapons (1–2 suitable to occupation)
- Tools and mundane items (rope, torches, bedroll, etc.)
- Trade goods or specialized equipment

### Currency

RuneQuest uses several forms of currency (varies by region and culture):
- **Lunars**: Primary currency in many regions (silver coins)
- **Wheels**: Often used for larger transactions
- **Clacks**: Smaller denomination coins
- Other trade goods and barter systems

Starting money is typically allocated through occupation or is a small amount (1D6×10 Lunars, varies by campaign).
