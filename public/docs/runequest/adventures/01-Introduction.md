# INTRODUCTION

This booklet provides the Colymar tribe as a detailed setting area for the gamemaster and players of RuneQuest: Roleplaying in Glorantha, with three scenarios set in the lands of the Colymar.

## Background

The first part of this book provides information on the Colymar tribe, their history, notable individuals, and points of interest. The tribal center Clearwine Fort is described in detail, as is the hamlet of Apple Lane, a well-established setting for many a RuneQuest campaign. This material is presented to enable a sandbox campaign amongst the Colymar tribe, such as with the classic RuneQuest setting book Griffin Mountain.

A collection of rumors is presented as a source of potential adventure sources.

If you’re new to Glorantha, welcome! We hope we have provided you here with enough background and resources to ground yourselves in this fantastic world. Glorantha’s history and culture are extremely rich and can at times seem overwhelming to newcomers. The information presented in these pages is interwoven and mutually supportive, allowing an easy entry point. As you read through this book you will find lore and important personages mentioned in one part cropping up elsewhere to play an important role in a scenario, or to feed a rumor.

If you’re familiar with Glorantha, welcome back! You may notice that this book contains many tidbits of Gloranthan lore and references to classic locations, people, and adventures, as well as the return of some implacable foes...

## Scenarios
The latter part of this book contains three scenarios centered on Apple Lane. Defending Apple Lane, the first of these, makes the adventurers responsible for defending the hamlet from a band of marauding Tusk Riders and can result in one of the adventurers becoming the new Thane of Apple Lane.

The second scenario, Cattle Raid, has the adventurers protect a sacred herd of cattle from monsters and escalates into a potential feud with a rival tribe. The adventurers’ Passions and social skills are tested as much or more than their combat skills.

The final scenario, The Dragon of the Thunder Hills, has the adventurers confront a dream dragon that demands to be worshiped as a goddess. Once again, the adventurers’ Passions are tested against an extremely dangerous and powerful adversary. The adventurers interact with the tribal leaders of the Colymar and potentially even with an immortal Dryad who has resided in these lands since before the Dawn.

These scenarios are loosely linked, and the outcome of one adventure may affect another, so the gamemaster may need to adjustment things to suit an unexpected turn of events or unanticipated result.

## Setting Date
Although the gamemaster is free to set the scenarios at whatever time works for their campaign, the default assumption is that this book is set shortly after the Dragonrise in Earth Season 1625. This is a period of great turmoil in Dragon Pass, and the unsettled nature of the time gives the gamemaster great latitude to upset and overturn things. Your Glorantha will vary!

A variety of current events have been provided so that the gamemaster can describe happenings taking place at the time.

## Making Characters
It is perhaps not surprising that it is suggested that one or two adventurers be Sartarites from the Colymar tribe and with a Loyalty (Colymar Tribe) Passion. The material within should make it easy for the players to choose a local clan (although the default of the Ernaldori clan certainly works here).

However, the other adventurers can certainly be Esrolians, Grazelanders, Praxians, Old Tarshites, or even Lunar Tarshites. Dragon Pass is a violent and dangerous war-torn land, and it is useful that most (although not necessarily all) of the adventurers are at least competent in combat.

It is also suggested that at least one adventurer should be an initiate of Orlanth and at least one an initiate of Ernalda.
These are the most important cults of the Colymar. More than one member of either cult is certainly acceptable— indeed there is nothing wrong with a party that consists entirely of Orlanth and Ernalda initiates. That said, the other Lightbringer or Earth cults are locally important: even cults like Humakt, Storm Bull, and Yelmalio have nearby temples or shrines.

Seven Mothers cultists will have more difficulty here, as there are no public temples or shrines to the Seven Mothers in Western Sartar at this time. Shrines that did exist were destroyed in the wake of the Dragonrise. However, the gamemaster may decide that there is a private shrine to the Seven Mothers somewhere in the Colymar lands, being tended by secretive Seven Mothers cultists (see Rumor 85, page 75).
