
# Defending Apple Lane
This scenario is intended for four to six starting adventurers. At least one should be a member of the Colymar tribe and at least one should be an initiate of Orlanth or an associated cult. Some, although not necessarily all, of the adventurers should be reasonably skilled at combat (75% or better), as the scenario requires that the adventurers defend the hamlet of Apple Lane from a band of Tusk Riders.

## Gamemaster Background
With the collapse of Lunar rule in Dragon Pass resulting from the Dragonrise, many have taken to banditry. Few bandits are more feared than the bands of Tusk Riders who pillage and murder from atop their giant boars.

Xiobalg’s band is one of these—former mercenaries for the Lunar Empire who now prey on whoever they want. A few days ago, Xiobalg raided several farms near Apple Lane, but was angered that there was so little to plunder. After Yaram tortured a captured farmer named Saraskos, Xiobalg learned the reason: the harvest was stored in the Apple Lane granary, along with other goods. Xiobalg told the apparently dying Saraskos that he planned to raid Apple Lane soon and would pillage the hamlet—and its Uleria temple—and raze it to the ground.

Despite all odds, Saraskos survived and made his way to Apple Lane to warn Aileena and Brightflower of the Tusk Riders’ plans. The women recognized that the hamlet could not defend itself—they would need to hire defenders.
Fortuitously, the adventurers arrive in Apple Lane soon after.

### Current Events
This scenario is assumed to take place in Earth Season 1625, a week or two after the harvest. Only a few weeks before was the cataclysmic event known as Dragonrise. The heavens shook: the constellation known as Orlanth’s Ring appeared on the wrong day and quickly spiraled around the sky as several planets and constellations streaked out of place to explode against the orange stars. Other stars disappeared or fell out of the sky. Finally, an impossibly huge True Dragon erupted out of the ground some 60 kilometers to southeast and then rose into the sky. In a matter of minutes, the bulk of the Lunar Army in Dragon Pass was gone. For a week, the True Dragon could be seen in the sky, blocking out the Red Moon.

Sartar has been thrown into turmoil with the collapse of Lunar rule. Queen Leika returned to Clearwine Fort with her supporters and was hastily acclaimed tribal king. The other tribes of Sartar have also proclaimed their independence from the Lunar Empire. The sacred city of Boldhome has already fallen to the warlord Kallyr Starbrow and she has acclaimed herself Prince of Sartar, but there remains a small Lunar garrison in Jonstown.

The timeless ritual of the harvest carried on despite these world-shaking events. Cereals, apples, grapes, and figs all need to be harvested, and so despite the fears of war and dragons, life has carried on.

## Involving the Adventurers
The adventurers need only be passing through Apple Lane to get involved in this adventure; perhaps they are returning from cult activities, from visiting kinfolk, traveling along the trade roads, or even going to visit the Uleria Temple. Apple Lane is a local crossroads and travelers are common there.

### Meeting Aileena and Brightflower
The Tin Inn is quiet and largely empty—bandits have troubled this area and travelers are scarce in this time of war and strife. Only the inn staff, Aileena the Priestess, and Hendroste Horsemaster are present. Brightflower the innkeeper provides the adventurers with a round of drinks on the house—while the adventurers are drinking, Aileena the Priestess, Brightflower, and Hendroste Horsemaster join them.

Aileena introduces herself as the local Priestess of Uleria.
She is an attractive woman in her mid-30s, wearing kohl and fragrant perfume. She asks about the adventurers’ deeds, comments on their weapons, any markings of war gods (such as Orlanth, Argan Argar, Babeester Gor, Humakt, Maran Gor, Storm Bull, Waha, Yelmalio, or Yelm), notable scars, and so on—she seeks to ascertain that the adventurers include warriors (or at least competent fighters). Once she is satisfied that the adventurers might be competent fighters, she gets right to the point:

“We have desperate need of your prowess! Apple Lane is in terrible danger—a gang of Tusk Rider bandits is threatening to destroy our community. We are a small community and, other than Hendroste here, we have no warriors. We wish to hire you to defend Apple Lane from these Tusk Riders.” The adventurers likely have questions:

#### Q: How do you know this?
A: “A local farmer, named Saraskos, was captured and tortured by the Tusk Riders. While being tortured, he overheard that the Tusk Rider chief planned to come to Apple Lane and pillage the hamlet and the storeroom. Despite his wounds, Saraskos managed to escape and made it here with the news.
He is recovering from his wounds in the Temple.”

#### Q: What do you know about Tusk Riders?
A: “Tusk Riders are hate-filled hybrids of men and trolls.
They ride giant boars and offer human sacrifice to their chthonic gods. They enjoy torturing and killing their victims.”

#### Q: Why can’t Uleria protect you?
A: “My goddess is not a martial deity—she is the power of Love, not Death. With time, I have no doubts She could demonstrate that embracing Love is preferable to embracing Death, but I do not believe these Tusk Riders will give us that opportunity. I fear they must be confronted with force.
Such is the broken world we mortals inhabit.”

#### Q: How many Tusk Riders are there?
A: “Saraskos mentioned that there were less than a dozen.”

#### Q: How many fighters are here in Apple Lane?
A: “Other than Hendroste here, I think only Postal even knows how to handle a spear. And old Kareena the Godtalker, of course.”

Brightflower handles negotiations for remunerations. She offers 10 L for each adventurer to defend the village, plus free food, drink, and lodging in the Tin Inn until the Tusk Riders are driven off. Brightflower offers 10 Wheels for the leader of the Tusk Riders—dead or alive, plus another 1 Wheel for each additional Tusk Rider killed or captured. She can be bargained up as per the RuneQuest rules (page 168).

If the adventurers seem reluctant, Aileena will appeal to their Honor or test their Fertility Rune. She is desperate for their aid and will offer whatever she can, including the blessings of her goddess.

## The Villagers
Aileena, Brightflower, and Hendroste have the villagers gather at the Tin Inn so that the adventurers can discuss their plan of defending the village. There is a total of thirteen adults in the hamlet. As Aileena warned, few of the residents have any proficiency at fighting. Although Hendroste Horsemaster and Kareena the God-talker are skilled fighters, and Postal served in the local militia in his youth, the other villagers are effectively noncombatants. They either cannot or will not fight against Tusk Riders except in the direst of circumstances.

If the gamemaster believes that the adventurers have a fair or better chance of defeating the Tusk Riders on their own, then Postal plans to defend the Tin Inn, Hendroste plans to defend his horses, and Kareena plans to defend the Temple to All the Deities and heal the wounded. It will require successful Orate or Fast Talk skill tests to persuade them to alter their plans.

If the gamemaster believes that the adventurers are grossly outnumbered or outclassed by the Tusk Riders, then Postal, Hendroste, and even Kareena can directly assist the adventurers (depending on how outclassed the adventurers are).

The villagers are most insistent that their lives are preserved and that the Uleria temple, Temple to All the Deities, Tin Inn, and the Storeroom are defended. Other buildings are acknowledged as expendable.

Let the players describe their plan for defending Apple Lane. Any plan should answer the following questions at the very least:
- How do they watch for the Tusk Riders?
- Where are the combatants located? Are they on foot or mounted? Are they stationary or are they patrolling?
- Where are the non-combatants?

If the players’ plan does not include this information, the gamemaster may ask suitable characters to make Battle rolls to realize that these critical elements are missing from the plan. If this is unsuccessful, the gamemaster may choose to exploit this lack of preparedness as seems appropriate.

## The Attack
### The Feint
The initial attack on Apple Lane is a feint. Mardu and Yodram ride in from the North Street towards the Uleria Temple, whooping and yelling. They plan to ride past the Uleria Temple towards the orchards to the west of the hamlet, trying to draw off any defenders. Meanwhile, the rest of the Tusk Riders hide in the orchards south of the old Smithy. If either takes enough damage to incapacitate a hit location, they ride away, blowing their horns in panic.

### Rush for the Gate
Once Mardu and Yodram ride past the Well (or if Mardu or Yodram blow their horn in panic), the rest of the Tusk Riders ride out. Zeoldan, Durug, Xiobalg, and Qualbalg form a single file line and charge the gate of the Tin Inn with the Tuskers. It will take 3 full melee rounds for them to arrive at the gate of the Tin Inn—each Tusker will slam into the gate, starting at SR 3, then (assuming the first boar fails to crash the gate) SR 6, then SR 9, then SR 12. Each boar does damage equivalent to its Gore attack to the gate.
The gate has 18 hit points and 10 armor points: attacks that do damage for less than the armor points will tear into the wood but are otherwise ineffectual.

If all four boars fail to break the gate, they will withdraw for a round, turn around and charge again (taking two complete rounds). They will repeat this as long as is necessary or feasible.

### Roving Reinforcements
Kranag and Yaram act as roving reinforcements. If Mardu and Yodram are under attack, they come to reinforce them.
If Mardu and Yodram have drawn off defenders, Kranag and Yaram attack the defenders from the rear.

If Xiobalg’s group gets unexpected resistance at the Tin Inn, Kranag and Yaram ride to reinforce them.

### Breaking Into the Tin Inn
If the Tusk Riders break through the gate of the Tin Inn, they will enter the courtyard the following round. They search within looking for hostages from among the non-combatants.
If there are none in the courtyard, they enter the Inn itself.

With hostages in hand, Xiobalg proposes a deal—he offers to return the hostages in exchange for the defenders leaving the village to the Tusk Riders to be pillaged. Xiobalg plans to renege on the deal if possible—the moment the defenders leave their defensive positions, he will order an attack on the defenders.

### Chasing off the Tusk Riders
If any of the Tusk Riders are killed or incapacitated, all Tusk Riders except Xiobalg and Kranag need to roll for an appropriate Passion to stay engaged in melee. If a Tusk Rider fails, they will disengage and try to retreat. If at least half of the surviving Tusk Riders flee, Xiobalg and Kranag will also try to withdraw—they do not need to make a Passion roll.

Retreating Tusk Riders will retreat to the safety of Pig Hollow in the Thunder Hills. They are easy to track (double the effective Track skill of the trackers). Once at Pig Hollow, they offer blood and promises to Redeye the Boar if it will protect them.

## Pig Hollow
Pig Hollow is a narrow “V”-shaped valley in the Thunder Hills. It was cut by a seasonal stream and runs less than five kilometers. Hound Knob rises some 600 meters above the bottom of the hollow, while the Dragonewt Plinth on the eastern hills is some 400 meters above the floor. The floor of the valley is heavily wooded with oak and pine, and rich with animal life. There are numerous spirits in the valley, but none will engage in Spirit Combat with corporeal beings.

A successful Track can locate the camp. If the adventurers are following the giant Tusker boars, it is easy to track them to their lair (see Chasing Off the Tusk Riders, page 78), otherwise they need to make a normal Track roll.

If there are no Tusk Riders at their lair (for example if all the attackers were killed or taken captive), then there is 20% chance that Redeye the Boar will be near the Tusk Rider lair.
If Tusk Riders are present, there is a 50% chance that Redeye the Boar will be nearby.

Give the adventurers the opportunity to flee from Redeye the Boar, as he can easily wipe out a party of unprepared adventurers! Redeye does not pursue fleeing adventurers.

The Tusk Rider camp consists of pine branches and other vegetation piled up high enough to serve as shelters for the Tuskers. There is a clearing around the fire pit and a midden pile of bones. A successful Search roll locates the Tusk Riders treasure hoard, buried in the midden pile: 617 L, 15 golden Wheels, a golden arm ring (worth 350 L), a golden neck ring (worth 1,250 L), three golden pendants (worth 950 L each), a golden fibula (worth 300 L), and six silver dishes (two silver skillets, one worth 250 L and the other decorated with the figure of Asrelia and worth 325 L; a large fluted washingbowl with Esrola, Ernalda, and Maran Gor in the center and worth 300 L; another fluted dish worth 400 L; and a large silver plate and small silver cup worth 250 L and 200 L).

### The Tusk Riders
Xiobalg’s band of eight Tusk Riders has taken advantage of the collapse of Lunar rule in Sartar to plunder and pillage to their black hearts’ content. For the last season, they have used a camp near Pig Hollow as a refuge. From there, this small band of marauders has, with impunity, terrorized the locals and any traveling without a sizable escort.

This cocky group scoffs at the suggestion that the local farmers pose any threat. The arrogant Xiobalg leads the raiders. More feared than admired, Xiobalg’s words are closely heeded. The grizzled veteran, Kranag, is Xiobalg’s second-in-command.

#### Xiobalg
Initiate of the Bloody Tusk.

Male Tusk Rider, age 30+.

Xiobalg is ready to claim Rune Lord status upon his return to the Ivory Plinth. He plans to sacrifice any hostages there and bind their spirits into their hands. Xiobalg is cruel but clever. His strategies tend to be simple and effective. He possesses a POW-storing crystal containing 11 magic points and a necklace with two severed hands (both human), each containing the bound spirit of its prior owner. Xiobalg can order the spirits to cast spells they possess.

Before engaging in combat, Xiobalg will spend two rounds to have his spirits cast Countermagic, Bladesharp, and Protection. He will then cast Demoralize on the most dangerous looking foe and charge.

If pressed, Xiobalg will use his remaining Rune points to cast Pain Tooth (see page 87), but not before, He has an ivory wand, valued at 500 L, which has a Befuddle matrix.
He also owns a necklace worth 300 L, with an Extinguish matrix on it.

## Consequences
If the adventurers defeat or chase off the Tusk Riders, they each get a +1D3% increase in their Reputation. They also get whatever reward they negotiated with Aileena and Brightflower.

### Thane of Apple Lane
If the adventurers were successful, acted honorably towards the villagers, were suitably respectful of Aileena and her initiates, and at least one is an initiate of Orlanth or of an associated cult, Aileena offers them something greater than mere coin: the title of Thane of Apple Lane and the buildings and land that go with it. The prospective thane must:

- Be an initiate of Orlanth (or an associated cult).
- Swear to defend Apple Lane, uphold the law of the tribe and of Sartar.
- Swear to serve the Colymar tribe in war and peace.

The oath is performed in the Temple to All the Deities before a votive image of Orlanth, with all the villagers (and many nearby farmers) present.

Afterwards, the newly acclaimed Thane of Apple Lane must travel to Clearwine Fort (about 30 kilometers away) to be recognized by Queen Leika. The new thane must take Loyalty (Colymar Tribe) or Loyalty (Queen Leika) at 60% or add +20% to one of these existing Passions. In return, the queen presents the new thane with an exceptionally fine broadsword (1D8+2 damage, 13 HP, worth 150 L) and confirms their status. The ceremony serves to permanently increase the new thane’s CHA by +1.

### Benefits of Being the Thane of Apple Lane

The Thane of Apple Lane is entitled to take possession of the Thane’s House and associated buildings (barn and corral). The two-story stone house has been neglected for four years and is need of repairs and renovation but is in good structural shape.

Approximately 50 acres of apple orchards (equivalent to five hides) have been allocated for the support of the thane and their household. The orchards are divided into five locations (each about ten acres), each tended and harvested by a tenant family that takes half of the harvest and remitting the other to the thane. With decent management, the orchard generates about 200 L a year for the support of the thane as described in the RuneQuest rules.

The ransom price for a thane is 1,000 L, paid by the tribe.

### Responsibilities
The thane is the representative of the tribal king in the Apple Lane area, and tasked with the defense of Apple Lane, its residents, and local travelers. The thane is also tasked with maintaining the local peace, serving as the local judge, and assisting the local clans in tribal matters.
The thane is also expected to participate regularly in Orlanth cult activities and must serve as a lay member of Orlanth Rex.

### Tenants of the Thane
There are five tenant families working the orchards. They reside outside of the hamlet, near the orchards, in small rectangular stone cottages with an acre or less of tilled land.
Some of the tenants are potentially usable as a local militia—all possess at least a spear, shield, and battle axe.
Skills of 50+% are listed: if required, the gamemaster can determine these as 50+1D20%.

#### Tenant Family 1
Good farmers and proud Orlanthi, three children.
- Rastolf: Adventurous, stubborn, proud, former rebel. Skills: Farm, Herd.
- Serla: Shrewd, superstitious, ambitious. Skills: Farm, Herd.

#### Tenant Family 2
Mediocre farmers, former scribes who left the Jonstown Library for mysterious reasons, no children.
- Ivo Stone Brook: Lay member of Lhankor Mhy, bitter, idealistic. Skills: Farm, Read/Write Theyalan.

The Rotroots are poor farmers; they survive through the generosity of their Varmandi kin and from thieving. They are surprisingly loyal to their patron.

## Tusk Rider Skills and Magic
The Glorantha Bestiary contains a writeup for the Cult of the Bloody Tusk, to which the Tusk Riders in this adventure belong.
The following are summaries of a skill and Rune magic taught by that cult.

#### Bloody Cut (10) (Manipulation Skill)
This skill is used to dispatch animals and captured prisoners, causing the victim great trauma and pain so that its spirit may be trapped within a part of its body. The Bloody Cut is often combined with the Death Binding spell but is also practiced on its own.

#### Appease Earth (Rune spell)
1 Point

Ceremony

This ritual is used to perform sacrifices to the Bloody Tusk, through either a sacrifice of grain or other food fed to tuskers; blood sacrifice (preferably human sacrifice); or sacrifice of magic items or treasure of great worth. When cast successfully (see the Glorantha Bestiary for specifics), one characteristic point of the supplicant’s tusker mount is increased by +1 (chosen by the rider). No characteristic may be increased upon the normal maximum by this means.

#### Death Binding (Rune spell)

1 Point

Ceremony

When combined with Bloody Cut and a sacrifice of 1 POW, the victim’s spirit is trapped and bound within its tail (or hand, if humanoid). The caster can use the victim’s magic points to cast spells and can cast the victim’s spirit magic spells, if any. This magic is only usable by the one that trapped the spirit and is broken if a proper burial ceremony (such as Peaceful Cut or Prepare Corpse) is intoned over the tail or hand, or if it is destroyed.


#### Pain Tooth (Rune spell)

2 Points

Ranged, Temporal, Nonstackable

When cast on a weapon, this spell causes any hit that penetrates armor to incapacitate the location struck, in addition to the weapon’s normal damage. The victim can recover from this incapacitation by rolling under their CON×2 and can try to do so each round. If a target has been injured in more than one location by a Pain Tooth, they need only roll CON×2 a single time to be freed of all incapacitating effects.

#### Seal Wound (Rune spell)

2 Points

Ranged, Temporal, Nonstackable

Damage done by the weapon this spell is applied to will not regenerate or heal magically but must be healed in the normal time it would take. Thus Healing 2 will cauterize a wound, but not heal it, Healing 6 will restore a limb but not mend it, etc.
