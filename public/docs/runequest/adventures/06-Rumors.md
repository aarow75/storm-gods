# Rumors
These rumors are intended as spurs to adventure or roleplaying opportunities. When in an area where rumors could reasonably be encountered (e.g., towns and villages, but not wilderness), the adventurers can encounter one or more rumors chosen by the gamemaster or randomly generated from the following list. The gamemaster should present the rumors in an interesting and natural way, treating each as a guideline, rewording it based on the situation in which it is encountered.

To randomly determine a rumor, the gamemaster should roll a D100.
Each rumor is prefaced by a single letter indicating its veracity: T: The rumor is true, F: The rumor is false, M: The rumor is meaningless, R: The rumor may or may not be true at the gamemaster’s discretion, B: The rumor is generally true but also has a substantial false component.
