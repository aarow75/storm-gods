
# Appendix 1: Adventure Seeds
## The Baboon Crystal

A young Esrolian Issaries trader named Irilla comes to Apple Lane with a small caravan and claims she was born there. She further claims to own property and market rights in the hamlet and has a scroll showing a building at the location of Gringle’s Pawnshop. She claims that she is the daughter of Gringle and shows a Crystal of Toothsharp (a baboon variant of Bladesharp that functions identically) on a necklace that she says her father gave when he returned to Nochet. Her reappearance coincides with a troop of baboons arriving in town. She won’t part with the crystal, but the baboons want it back badly.

Alternately, a Sartarite female mercenary and her Grazelander husband Dalfar (both initiates of Orlanth) arrive in the hamlet. The mercenary says she is Raveena, the daughter of the previous thane of Apple Lane, Drenlon Swordsharp. She’s been fighting in far off lands for more than a decade and claims her father’s status as Thane of Apple Lane. Both are skilled warriors.

## The Blue Eye
While digging for treasures in the Ruined Hut, Phineas and Vigil dig up a magical blue stone eye. Their mother tries to sell it to the adventurers as an heirloom, but trouble is clearly following it—angry dwarfs who claim it is stolen from them, or thieving newtlings who want it.

## The Black Altar
A dark troll gang has come down from Dagori Inkarth and started offering blood sacrifices to Zorak Zoran at the Black Altar.

## The Earth Loaf
A large, roughly spherical head about a meter wide is seen rolling around the orchards near Apple Lane. It appears to be made of honey cake and smells of ginger or cinnamon.
It sings the following in Earthspeech:

“I got away from Grandmother, I got away from Aunt, and I will certainly get away from you!”

It has a movement rate of 9 and Dodge 75%. If it is caught, it is easily broken open, revealing a moist (and delicious) core. Eating the core adds +1 to CON—eating the Earth Loaf will affect any eater, only once in their life.
The Earth Loaf has 1D6 servings.

Once opened, the odor of the Earth Loaf attracts dinosaurs from as far away as 25 kilometers. Within a day or two, several triceratopses (or other herbivorous dinosaurs) come to the place the Earth Loaf was opened. The dinosaurs will devour fields, uproot trees, and cause destruction as they look for the source of the odor.

## From Out of the Blue

It is a fine day, sunny and clear. Furthermore, it is a wedding day, a blessed event. The bride and groom are bedecked in flowers and finery, and a great feast is laid out to recognize the occasion. Is one of the adventurers due to be married?
Perhaps this is their day. Quite unfortunate, for tragedy is about to strike!

As the wedding party makes its way to the hilltop where the final blessings are to be intoned, something very large and very heavy falls without warning from the skies overhead and lands with a tremendous thud—right on top of one of the bethrothed! The luckless soul is killed instantly.

What is truly remarkable is the nature of the object. Roll 1D6 to find out what it is or come up with something of your own devising. Remember, this is Glorantha, so don’t be afraid to get weird with it!

|Roll|Random Object
--|--
|1|The severed hand of a giant, some 15 meters wide across the palm. Who or what could have cut it off and sent it flying? And is that being heading this way?
|2|A huge, coiled serpent, two meters in diameter and 20 meters long when stretched out. Although it appears dead, it is merely stunned and will soon wake up and start attacking other members of the wedding party.
|3|A large shard of the Spike itself! Has it been flying through space and time these many Ages only to come to rest now? Many factions will now come seeking for it; amid tragedy, can and will the clan defend it?
|4|A piece of the Sky Dome, crackling, steaming, and sizzling like dry ice. Looking up, a small hole in the sky may be discerned far overhead. How did this bit come loose, and who is going to put it back?
|5|An elaborate miniature palace, now in crumbled ruins from the force of the impact. Among the rubble may be seen tiny people, looking much like Orlanthi, albeit with green-tinted skin. Are there any survivors among the dead to tell of their society and from whence they came?
|6|A great bronze shield, 20 meters in diameter, which lands rim-side down and buries itself 3–4 meters deep in the earth. Its face shows signs of battle damage, and the torn leather thong on the inverse side is soaked with sweat. What happens when its owner, no doubt a powerful god, comes searching for it?

## The Besotted Giant
A giant has come down from the north, raiding local sheep herds and generally causing trouble. However, before a war party can be raised to deal with the problem, the troubles cease. The giant has caught sight of a local lady of great beauty and martial prowess (perhaps one of the adventurers themselves, or even Queen Leika!) and now spends his days in moony contemplation of his love.

He is far too shy to approach her, but she may approach him and ask him kindly to leave. (Even when not raiding, giants tend to consume a lot of local resources.) He agrees to leave on one condition: that he shall return in a year’s time to take the lady’s hand in marriage!

## Beacon Fire
Abruptly one night, the beacon of Sartar’s Tower erupts spontaneously with eerie red and green flame, burns for one hour, then vanishes. Two nights later, the same event occurs atop Tarkalor’s Keep.

## Lost Spirit

While one or more of the adventurers is passing near Tarndisi’s Grove, a spirit calls out repeatedly for Kareena to aid her, though says nothing else. Anyone familiar with Apple Lane knows of the God-talker there. The spirit is trapped in a ghostbush in the Silver Wood on the Spirit Plane, and only someone who can walk the Spirit World can reach her.

## The Other Side of the Eye

One of the children of Apple Lane went exploring in the ruins of Gringle’s Pawnshop. She never came back out, but now an old spirit with many eyes is active there, watching anyone who attempts to investigate.
