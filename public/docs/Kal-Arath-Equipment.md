# Kal-Arath Equipment

**Carrying capacity: PCs can carry STR+8 items.** More than that and all physical rolls are made at disadvantage; at twice the number, they cannot move.

For starting gear, roll d6×10 silver, choose one weapon, and roll d6 for total rations. That's it — *now survive!*

All prices are in silver. Most commerce in Kal-Arath is done by trading items or services of comparable value — take the prices below as guidelines and use your excellent judgment for the rest. As a rule of thumb from the rulebook:

| Price tier | Typical goods |
|-----------|----------------|
| d6 silver | Meal (day of rations), poor lodging, rough clothes, rope, simple tools |
| d6×10 silver | Light armor, simple weapons, poor-to-average horse, fine lodging, healing herbs, torches/lamp and oil, tent |
| d6×100 silver | Medium armor; large, fine, metal weapons; good horse; wagon |
