# Mothership Equipment

Oftentimes the difference between life and death in Mothership is having the right tools for the job. Costs are in Credits (Cr).

**Starting loadouts & credits** — pick one of your class's starting loadouts and get 5d10 Credits, or forgo the loadout and roll 5d10×10 Credits to buy equipment piecemeal.

**Addiction** — excessive use of Pain Pills or Stimpaks requires a Body save to prevent addiction. Once addicted, make a daily Body save or gain 1d10 Stress each day without. Taking more than one pill at a time: roll 1d10 — equal to or under the number taken forces a Body save; failure means unconsciousness.

## Survival

### Oxygen Tank
*50Cr.* Attached to a vaccsuit, allows up to 12 hours of oxygen under normal circumstances — 4 hours under stress. Explosive.

### Water Filter
*75Cr.* Pumps 50 liters of filtered water an hour from even the most brackish swamps.

### Rebreather
*120Cr.* Filters air and allows underwater breathing for up to twenty minutes at a time without resurfacing.

### MRE (×7)
*70Cr.* "Meals, Ready-to-Eat" — self-contained field rations in lightweight packaging. Each has sufficient sustenance for one person for one day (water not included).

### Camping Gear
*100Cr.* Tent, canteen, stove, backpack, sleeping bag.

### Mag-Boots
*200Cr.* Magnetic grip that lets the wearer walk on the surface of a ship (in space, docked, or free-floating) or on metal-based asteroids.

## Medical

### First Aid Kit
*75Cr.* Adds +10% to rolls made to bandage wounds and stop bleeding.

### Medscanner
*150Cr.* Scans a living or dead body and analyzes it for disease or abnormalities without a biopsy (or autopsy).

### Automed (×6)
*300Cr.* Nanotech pills that assist the body in repairing damage: +10% to Body saves against disease, poison, and healing attempts, and +10% to Fear saves made to reduce Stress.

### Pain Pills (×6)
*450Cr.* When ingested, immediately heals 1d10 damage and lowers Stress by 1. Danger of addiction and/or overdose with frequent use.

### Stimpak (×6)
*600Cr.* Grants an immediate 2d10 Health and temporarily increases Strength and Combat by 2d10 each for 1d10 hours. Danger of addiction and/or overdose with frequent use.

## Tools

### Electronic Tool Kit
*650Cr.* A full set of tools for detailed repair or construction work on electronics. Confers +10% to rolls to repair electronics.

### Cybernetic Diagnostic Scanner
*400Cr.* Scans androids and other cybernetic organisms to diagnose physical or mental issues. Can be used as a locating device for synthetic organisms. Often distrusted by androids.

### Body Cam
*50Cr.* A camera worn on your clothing that streams video back to a control center so crew members can see what you're seeing.

### Flashlight
*20Cr.* Handheld or shoulder-mounted; illuminates 20m ahead of the user.

### Short-range Comms
*80Cr.* Ship-to-ship communication within a reasonable distance, and surface-to-surface within a dozen kilometers.

### Long-range Comms
*300Cr.* For ship-to-surface communication.

### Heads-Up Display
*75Cr.* Often worn by marines. Lets the wearer see through the body cams of others in their unit and tap into their guns' smart-link capabilities.

### Lockpick Set
*40Cr.* Highly advanced tools for hacking basic airlock and electronic door systems. Confers +10% on rolls to open these doors.

### Binoculars
*50Cr.* 20× magnification. Often come with thermal and night-vision options.

## Sensors

### Bioscanner
*200Cr.* Scans the immediate area for signs of life — generally 100m in all directions, unblocked by most known metals. Reveals the location of life signs, but not what that life is.

### Infrared Goggles
*150Cr.* Lets the wearer see heat signatures, sometimes several hours old.

### Survey Kit
*250Cr.* Used on a planet's surface for quick mapping of the nearby few kilometers, plus data on air breathability, gravity, and other notable features of the landscape.

### Locator
*100Cr.* When worn, allows crew members at a control center (or the bridge of a ship) to track the wearer's location.
