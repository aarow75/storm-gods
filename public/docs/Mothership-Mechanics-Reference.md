## Character Creation

**Steps:**

1. Roll 6d10 for each Stat (Strength, Speed, Intellect, Combat) — record in order
2. Pick a Class and note starting Saves
3. Mark starting Skills and spend starting Skill points
4. Note your class's Stress & Panic rules
5. Pick a starting Loadout; roll a random Trinket and Patch
6. Fill in Stress (starts at 2), Resolve (starts at 0), Max Health (Strength × 2), starting Credits (5d10×10)

## Core Mechanics

### Dice

- **xd10:** Roll that many d10s and add together.
- **xd10 (underlined):** Roll that many d10s, add together, multiply by ten.
- **d%:** Roll two d10s and read as percentile (00–99).

### Stat Checks

Roll **d% equal to or under** the relevant Stat to succeed. Stats are: **Strength**, **Speed**, **Intellect**, **Combat**.

### Advantage & Disadvantage

- **Advantage:** Roll d% twice, take the **best** result.
- **Disadvantage:** Roll d% twice, take the **worse** result.
- Advantage and Disadvantage cancel each other out. Multiple of one outweighs the other.
- Advantage/Disadvantage applies to any roll type: Saves, damage, Stress, etc. For damage, "best" means highest; for Saves and Stat checks, "best" means lowest passing roll.
- Shorthand: **[+]** for Advantage, **[-]** for Disadvantage.

### Critical Hits & Failures

Anytime you **roll doubles on a d% roll** (e.g. 11, 55, 00), that roll is a **critical**. Success = **critical hit**; failure = **critical failure**. Critical failures may incur additional penalties. Critical hits grant additional benefits depending on context.

### Opposed Checks

Both parties roll under their relevant Stat or Save on d%. The one who rolls **higher while still under their stat** wins. Ties go to whoever rolled higher.

### Skills

Skills add a bonus to the relevant Stat, giving a higher number to roll under.

| Rank | Bonus |
|------|-------|
| Untrained | +0% (may roll at Disadvantage for complex tasks) |
| Trained | +10% |
| Expert | +15% |
| Master | +20% |

**Skill costs:** Trained = 1 point, Expert = 2 points, Master = 3 points. Must have a prerequisite Trained skill before taking Expert; must have Expert before Master.

Roll **d% equal to or under** the relevant Save to succeed. Failing a Save generally grants 1 Stress plus any other consequence. Critically failing a Save triggers a Panic Roll.

| Save | Covers |
|------|--------|
| **Sanity** | Rationalization, logic, detecting illusions, thinking under pressure |
| **Fear** | Emotional trauma, fear, loneliness, depression |
| **Body** | Reflexes, hunger, disease, infection, invasion |
| **Armor** | Physical damage from combat (bullets, claws, etc.) |

## Survival

### Crisis Checks

For prolonged tasks (surgery, mining an asteroid), the Warden sets a **Crisis Difficulty** of 1–3 — the number of consecutive Stat checks needed to succeed.

- Fail a check → gain Stress equal to the Crisis Difficulty and fail the Crisis.
- Re-roll a failed check by immediately taking 1d10 Stress.
- On the **third failure**, the Crisis ends with full Stress damage plus consequences.

### Food & Water

- **Without food:** After 24 hours, Disadvantage to all rolls.
- **Without water (1L/day minimum):** Disadvantage to all rolls; strenuous activity requires a Body save or pass out.

### Oxygen

- Without oxygen: unconscious in 15 seconds, dead in 3–5 minutes.
- If all Life Support modules are destroyed: (current hull − 1d10) ÷ living humans = days of breathable oxygen remaining per person.
- **Less than half original oxygen:** Disadvantage on all rolls.
- **Less than a quarter original oxygen:** Body check once per day (and after strenuous activity) or fall unconscious.
- **Out of oxygen:** Body save or die; success = fall unconscious and leach oxygen from a random crew member daily.
- Crew in cryosleep or resting use ¼ oxygen unit per day.

## Combat

### Turn Order

1. **Check for Surprise:** If surprised, make a Fear save — failure means stunned for 1 round.
2. **Speed Check:** Pass = act before enemies. Fail = act after enemies. Critical success = extra action; critical failure = reduced to 1 action.
3. Characters with **Tactics** may add their Skill bonus to Speed (and grant it to one other player). Characters with **Command** grant their bonus to all nearby crew members.

### Turns & Rounds

- **Turn:** One individual's or group's actions.
- **Round:** ~10 seconds; everyone takes a turn in order. Characters killed before their turn do not act.

### Actions

Each character takes **two significant actions** per turn.

*Significant actions include (but are not limited to):* Attacking, bandaging a wound, checking vital signs, firing vehicle weapons, maneuvering a vehicle, opening an airlock, operating a machine, reloading, running/walking, throwing, using a computer terminal.

*Insignificant actions (free):* Talking, taking cover as part of a move, looking around.

### Attacking

**Ranged:** Attacker makes a **Combat check** vs. defender's **Armor save** (opposed check). Attacker wins → roll weapon damage.

**Close-Quarters Combat (CQC):** Attacker makes a Combat check. Defender chooses to oppose with:
- **Armor save** — defend against damage
- **Combat check** — counter-attack
- **Body save** — attempt to flee CQC

**Wrestling/Grappling:** Opposed Strength check.

### Cover

Concealment of at least half your body grants **Advantage** on Armor saves against ranged attacks.

### Movement

- **Normal armor:** Move ½ Speed in meters per action (full Speed with both actions).
- **Heavy armor (Vaccsuit, Advanced Battle Dress):** Strength check to move full allotment; failure = half allotment (¼ Speed per action).

### Hit Location (Optional)

Roll d10 (evens = left side, odds = right side):

| Roll | Location |
|------|----------|
| 10 | Head |
| 3 | Right Arm |
| 4 | Left Arm |
| 5–9 | Torso |
| 2 | Left Leg |
| 1 | Right Leg |

### Damage

Losing an opposed Combat check = take damage subtracted from Health.

A **Panic roll** is required if:
- The hit was a critical hit
- You take more than half your Max Health in one hit

### Healing

Rest for at least 6 hours → make a **Body save**:
- **Success:** Heal Health equal to the amount you succeeded by (i.e. your Body score minus the roll).
- **Critical success:** Double the amount healed.
- **Failure:** No healing.
- **Critical failure:** Lose 1d10 Health.

**Only heal from rest once per day.** Medbays grant Advantage on Body saves for healing. Adverse circumstances confer Disadvantage.

### Unconsciousness & Death

At **0 Health**, make a **Body save** or die. On success, the Warden makes a secret roll:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Comatose and brain-dead. Only extraordinary measures can help. |
| 2–3 | In 1d10 days, 1 Health. −5 Str, −5 Spd, −5 Int (permanent). Disadvantage all rolls for 1d10 days. +1d10 Stress. |
| 4–6 | In 1d10 hours, 1 Health. −5 Str, −5 Spd (permanent). Disadvantage all rolls for 1d10 hours. +3 Stress. |
| 7–9 | In 1d10 minutes, 1 Health. −5 Str (permanent). Disadvantage all rolls for 3d10 minutes. +2 Stress. |
| 10 | Immediately, 1 Health. Disadvantage all rolls for 1d10 minutes. +1 Stress. |

### Weapon Range

- **Short Range:** No penalty.
- **Medium Range:** −10% to Combat.
- **Long Range (max):** Disadvantage on Combat check.

### Aiming

Spend both actions aiming (take no damage that turn) → gain Advantage on next ranged shot. Can hold aim indefinitely as long as not damaged, grappled, shoved, or moved.

### Reloading

Reloading = one significant action. With **Military Training** or **Firearms** skill, reload as a free action (assuming ammo is accessible).

### Automatic Weapons

Fully automatic weapons must be reloaded after every shot — unless the wielder has Military Training or Firearms, in which case fire 3 times before reloading. Shown as Shots: 1(3) or 1(5) on the weapons chart.

### Special Weapon Abilities

- **+10 (Combat):** Add 10 to Combat Stat while using this weapon.
- **+10 (Firearms):** Add 10 to Combat if you have the Firearms skill.
- **−10 (vs. Armor save):** Opponent subtracts 10 from their Armor save against this weapon.

### Addiction

Excessive Pain Pill or Stimpak use requires a **Body save** to prevent addiction.

- **Once addicted:** Daily Body save or gain 1d10 Stress per day without the substance.
- **Used once/day for a week:** Must take double the dose for the same effect.
- **Multiple pills at once:** Roll 1d10 — if result ≤ number taken, make a Body save or fall unconscious (roll on the Unconsciousness table).
- Addiction can be cured through in-game treatment or leveling up.

## Stress

Stress by itself does nothing, but the higher your Stress, the more likely you are to Panic and the more severe the Panic results.

**Gain 1 Stress when:**
- Failing a Sanity, Fear, Body, or Armor save
- Any time the ship you're in takes a hit
- Getting knocked unconscious
- Going 24 hours without rest
- Going without food or water
- Near misses with asteroids or massive objects
- *(Scientist-specific:)* Whenever a nearby Scientist fails a Sanity save, nearby players also gain 1 Stress

**Relieve Stress by:**

Rest for at least 6 hours → make a **Fear save**:
- **Success:** Relieve 1 Stress per 10 points you succeeded by (rounded down).
- **Critical success:** Double Stress reduced.

Other methods (amount at Warden's discretion): docking at a safe starport, escaping deadly peril, sex/drugs/alcohol/carousing, defeating a difficult horror, going a week without rolling a save.

**Notes:**
- Cryosleep generally does not relieve Stress.
- A Psychology Skill can assist another character's Fear save (usable once per day; can't use it on yourself the same day).
- Fear saves for Stress relief have Disadvantage in the presence of Androids.

## Panic & Resolve

### Panic Check (Two Steps)

**Step 1 — Stress Check (2d10):** Roll 2d10.
- Roll **over** current Stress → don't Panic; relieve 1 Stress.
- Roll **equal to or under** current Stress → Panic; proceed to Step 2.

**Step 2 — Panic Effect (2d10 + Stress):** Roll 2d10 and add current Stress; consult the table below.

### Resolve

Every point of Resolve = −1 to all Panic Effect rolls. If the result reaches 0 or less, no Panic effect occurs. Maximum 5 Resolve. Starts at 0.

### Panic Effect Table

| Roll (2d10 + Stress) | Effect |
|----------------------|--------|
| 2–3 | **Laser Focus.** Advantage on all rolls for 1d10 hours. |
| 4–5 | **Major Adrenaline Rush.** Advantage on all rolls for 3d10 minutes. |
| 6–7 | **Minor Adrenaline Rush.** Advantage on all rolls for 1d10 minutes. |
| 8–9 | **Anxious.** Gain 1 Stress. |
| 10–11 | **Nervous Twitch.** Gain 2 Stress; nearest crew member gains 1 Stress. |
| 12–13 | **Cowardice.** Gain 1 Stress. Must make a Fear save to engage in combat for next 1d10 hours or flee. |
| 14–15 | **Hallucinations.** Trouble distinguishing reality from fantasy for 2d10 hours (secret roll). |
| 16–17 | **Crippling Fear.** Gain a permanent phobia. When encountered, make a Fear save at Disadvantage or gain 1d10 Stress. |
| 18–19 | **Overwhelmed.** Gain 1d10 Stress. |
| 20–21 | **Rattled.** Scream. Disadvantage on all rolls for 2d10 minutes. |
| 22 | **Paranoid.** For 1d10 days, make a Fear save when anyone joins your group or gain 1 Stress. |
| 23 | **Death Drive.** For Xd10 days (X = Stress), Sanity save when encountering a stranger or enemy or immediately attack them. |
| 24 | **Catatonic.** Unresponsive for Xd10 minutes (X = Stress). |
| 25 | **Broken.** For Xd10 days (X = Stress), make a Panic roll whenever a nearby crew member fails a save. |
| 26 | **Psychotic.** Immediately attack the nearest crew member until dealing at least 2d10 damage. |
| 27 | **Compounding Problems.** Roll twice on this table. |
| 28 | **Descent into Madness.** Gain 2 new phobias. Stress cannot be relieved below 5. |
| 29 | **Psychological Collapse.** Permanently, irreparably insane — character is now played by the Warden. |
| 30 | **Heart Attack.** Instant death. |

### When to Roll Panic

- Seeing more than one crew member Panic simultaneously
- Encountering a horrifying alien for the first time
- Taking a critical hit
- Losing more than half Max Health in one hit
- Rolling a critical failure on any save
- Seeing a crew member die
- When all hope seems lost
- When the ship takes a critical hit

## Space Travel

### Travel Times (Thrusters Only)

| Ship Speed | Interplanetary | Interstellar | Intergalactic |
|------------|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| 10–30 | Weeks | Years | Millennia |
| 31–50 | Days | Months | Decades |
| 51–80 | Hours | Weeks | Years |

### Jump Drives & Hyperspace

Jump drives rated 1–9 (rating = size of jump possible). Crew typically spends jumps in cryosleep. Time dilation is uncertain — a standard jump may pass weeks or years of outside time.

### Captaining a Ship

To be considered a **Captain** (required to purchase or own a ship), you need **Vehicle Specialization** in the ship's class and the **Command** skill.

### Buying a Ship

Each point of hull ≈ 10 million credits. Recommended starting point: minimum hull for the ship's class, with 6d10% of cost already paid off. 10% of all credits earned goes toward debt.

### Refueling

- Thrusters: 1 fuel unit per day.
- Jump Drive: fuel equal to jump rating (Jump 2 = 2 units).
- Orbit: 1 fuel unit per week.
- Launching from average gravity planet: 3 fuel.
- Unrefined fuel: requires ×2 as much as refined for the same job.
- Refined fuel: ~10,000cr per unit (varies by system).

### Ship Repairs

- Anyone with Mechanical Repair, Engineering, or similar skill may attempt an Intellect check to repair hull.
- **For every 5 points the check succeeds by, repair 1 Hull.**
- A ship that has taken 25%, 50%, or 75% damage cannot be repaired beyond that threshold without a starport.
- Only **one repair attempt** allowed before returning to a starport.

**At a starport:** 1 day per 10 hull repaired; 100,000cr per hull. Upgrades: 1 week per 10 hull; 10 million cr per hull.

## Ship Design

### Primary Modules (Required)

| Module | Effect / Cost |
|--------|---------------|
| **Life Support** | Each point supports up to 10 humans. Below minimum: non-vaccsuited crew make a Body save every hour or take 1d10 dmg (includes cryochambers; excludes androids). |
| **Command** | Bridge/cockpit. 1 module per 4 officer positions. |
| **Armor** | Each point costs 3 hull; grants +10% Armor save (max 80%). |
| **Thrusters** | Each module: +10% Speed (max 80%). Extra cost: +1 hull per 10 base hull. |
| **Engine** | 1 module per jump drive + 1 per 4 thrusters + 1 per 20 base hull. |
| **Fuel** | Jump requires double the jump rating in fuel (Jump 2 = 4 fuel). Thrusters: 1 fuel/day. Minimum 3 fuel per engine module. Extra fuel: 1 fuel per 10 Cargo. |
| **Frame** | 1 point per 10 base hull. Covers docking gear, airlocks, corridors, comms, etc. |

### Secondary Modules (Optional)

| Module | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Jump Drives** | Each point = +1 jump capability (max 9). Costs: 1st = 1 hull, 2nd = 2 hull, 3rd = 3 hull, etc. Requires 1 Computer module per drive. |
| **Computer** | Intellect = (modules × 10) + 30%. Combat = (modules × 10) + 10%. Each module = 1 autonomous action per turn. |
| **Galley** | Required for trips over 1 day: 1 galley per 2 Life Support modules. Missing: crew makes Body saves once/day (fail = 2d10 dmg + 1d10 Stress). Restock monthly; extra stores = 1 Cargo. |
| **Weapon Mount** | 1 mount per ship weapon. |
| **Medical Bay** | Each connected medbay: +5% Intellect to Scientists/Androids using it. Grants Advantage on Body saves for healing. |
| **Cryochamber** | Each hull point = 4 cryosleep pods. Androids do not need them. |
| **Living Quarters** | Private staterooms for officers on trips of 1+ weeks. |
| **Barracks** | Non-private; houses up to 12 crew. Ships that need them but lack them: +1 Stress per journey/month. |
| **Cargo Hold** | Each hold = 20×20m room; holds 10 cargo units. Can be repurposed for any basic room. |
| **Science Lab** | Each connected lab: +5% Intellect to Scientists/Androids for research. Can be designated a repair shop for Teamsters. |

| Stat | Derivation |
|------|-----------|
| **Armor** | Based on Armor modules (each +10%, max 80%) |
| **Combat** | (Computer modules × 10) + 10% |

## Ship-to-Ship Combat

### What's the Same

Surprise, turn order (Speed check), two significant actions per turn.

### Key Differences

- Ships use their own Stats (separate sheet).
- Most ship weapons must be manned to fire; use whichever is higher — your Combat or the ship's.
- Ships deal **MDMG** (Megadamage; 1 MDMG ≈ 100 damage).
- Ships use their **Armor save** to absorb damage to their **Hull**.
- Hull reduced to 0 = ship destroyed.
- The ship's **Computer** takes as many autonomous actions per turn as it has computer modules.
- Gunnery skill improves ship weapon rolls.
- Regular personal combat can occur simultaneously during ship-to-ship combat.

### Repairing Hull in Combat

Make an **Intellect check** on your turn. For every 5 points the check succeeds by, repair 1 Hull.

### Ship Critical Hits

Ships roll on the **Critical Hit Effect Table** whenever:
- The ship takes a critical hit
- The ship critically fails an Armor save
- The first time the ship takes any damage
- The ship loses 25%, 50%, or 75% of its hull
- The ship takes damage at 20 or less hull

Rolling doubles on the critical hit table = additional effect (roll again; repeat while doubles continue).

Anyone in the affected room must make a Body save or take 1d10 damage. Anyone in a destroyed room must make a Body save or be destroyed.

If the rolled effect is impossible (e.g., all weapons already destroyed), use the next entry up the table.

### Ship Critical Hit Table

| Roll | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 00–09 | **Cargo destroyed.** 1d10 cargo units destroyed. |
| 10–14 | **Life support.** Reduce life support by 1 module. |
| 15–19 | **Massive hull damage.** Ship takes ×2 hull damage; crew Body saves or take 1d10 dmg. |
| 20–24 | **Armor breach.** Disadvantage on armor saves until repaired. |
| 25–29 | **Data storage wiped.** All navigation and research data destroyed; crew doesn't know their location. |
| 30–34 | **Thrusters.** Reduce thrusters by 1. |
| 35–39 | **Random stateroom or non-essential room destroyed.** Body saves to escape or be destroyed. |
| 40–44 | **Random weapon disabled.** Cannot fire until repaired. |
| 45–49 | **Jump engine.** Reduce jump engine by 1. |
| 50–54 | **Engines.** Reduce engine by 1. |
| 55–59 | **System overload.** Computer and jump drives cease for 1d10 rounds unless repaired. −10% Combat. |
| 60–64 | **Artificial gravity failure.** Everything floats. Body saves or take 1d10 dmg from jolt. |
| 65–69 | **EMP.** All systems shut down for 1 round. Androids must be rebooted. |
| 70–74 | **1d10 cryosleep chambers destroyed.** |
| 75–79 | **Hull breach.** Ship depressurizes (starting in random room); vaccsuits required; Body save at Disadvantage to avoid death near breach. |

## Leveling Up

Can level up in any place safe enough to rest (even adverse conditions that impose Disadvantage on healing rolls qualify).

**1. Pick One Major Improvement:**
- **(A)** Improve 1 Stat by 5 and another by 3 (max 85 each)
- **(B)** Improve 2 Saves by 4 each (max 85 each)

**2. Pick One Minor Improvement:**
- **(A)** Gain 1 Resolve (max 5)
- **(B)** Remove 1 phobia or addiction
- **(C)** Heal all Stress

**3. Gain 2 Skill Points**

- Trained = 1 point, Expert = 2 points, Master = 3 points.
- Must have prerequisite Trained before taking Expert; must have Expert before taking Master.
- Unused points can be banked for future levels.
