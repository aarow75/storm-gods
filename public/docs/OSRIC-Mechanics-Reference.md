# OSRIC Mechanics Reference

**OSRIC** (Old School Reference and Index Compilation) — a retroclone compatible with 1st Edition AD&D rules.  

---

## How To Play

Players create characters and record their stats. The GM sets the scene — a tavern, dungeon entrance, wilderness, or elsewhere — then describes what the characters perceive. Players declare actions; the GM resolves them and describes the results. The GM controls all NPCs, roleplaying them directly or summarizing their behavior. Ultimately, "the course and success of the party is down to the players' judgment and creativity."

---

## Ability Scores

Characters have six core attributes: **Strength (Str)**, **Dexterity (Dex)**, **Constitution (Con)**, **Intelligence (Int)**, **Wisdom (Wis)**, and **Charisma (Cha)**. Each ranges from 3–18 (higher is better). The GM determines the generation method:

- Roll 3d6 per ability in order (strict, brutal)
- Roll 6 scores and assign freely
- Roll 4d6, drop lowest die (yields higher scores)

Governs mental acuity and problem-solving. Essential for magic users and illusionists. Determines bonus languages. Characters with Int 19+ gain scaling immunity to illusion spells.

Magic users and illusionists also consult Intelligence for spell acquisition. Intelligence determines both the chance to understand a spell and the maximum number of spells per spell level that can be known.

**Spell Acquisition Table:**

| Intelligence Score | Chance to Understand Spell | Maximum Spells Per Spell Level |
|--------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 9–10 | 35% | 3 |
| 11–12 | 45% | 7 |
| 13–14 | 55% | 9 |
| 15–16 | 65% | 11 |
| 17 | 75% | 14 |
| 18 | 85% | 18 |
| 19 | 90% | 22 |

**Learning Extra Spells:** If a caster finds an arcane spell scroll while adventuring—or obtains another magic-user's spell book—they must cast *read magic* on the spell (use your Int score to understand it). You must then *read* the actual spell, taking twice the normal casting time. You retain the option to "drag" it from your understanding; if you fail, the spell is lost from memory.

To learn a magical ink spell, a caster must make a magical ink special in a town or city. Research and experimentation costs 10 gp per level of the spell. As soon as the casting is finished, the spell emerges from the caster's text, and at the same time the spell disappears from the source text. Once the spell is copied, the original text remains but the spell is lost.

## Races

### Dwarfs

Hardy underground dwellers reaching 350+ years of age.

- **Ability Modifiers:** +1 Constitution, -1 Charisma (vs. non-dwarfs)
- **Combat Bonuses:** +1 to hit vs. goblinoids and orcs; giants/ogres suffer -4 to attack dwarfs
- **Magic Resistance:** +1 to saves vs. magic and poison per 3.5 points of Con (rounded down)
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Stonesense (within 10 ft):** Detect slopes 75%, new construction 75%, shifting rooms 66%, stone traps 50%, depth underground 50%
- **Languages:** Dwarfish, gnomish, goblin, kobold, orcish, common, alignment tongue; max 2 additional languages
- **Movement:** 90 ft
- **Permitted Classes:** Assassin, Cleric, Fighter, Thief, Fighter/Thief
- **Level Caps:** Assassin 9, Cleric 8, Fighter 7–9 (by Str), Thief unlimited
- **Ability Score Ranges (after adj.):** Str 8–18, Con 12–19, Cha 3–16
- **Starting Age:** Cleric 250+2d20, Fighter 40+5d4, Thief 75+3d6

---

### Elves

Thin, otherworldly beings living 1,000+ years. "They do not have souls; their thinking and motives are quite alien to those of humankind."

- **Ability Modifiers:** +1 Dexterity, -1 Constitution
- **Magic Resistance:** 90% immunity to sleep and charm spells
- **Combat Bonuses:** +1 to hit with any pulled bow; +1 to hit with longsword and short sword
- **Secret Doors:** Detect 1–3 in 6 depending on circumstance; automatically notice concealed doors within 10 ft (1 in 6)
- **Surprise:** 4-in-6 chance under optimal conditions
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Movement:** 120 ft
- **Languages:** Common, Elven, Gnoll, Gnomish, Goblin, Halfling, Hobgoblin, Orcish (more with Int 16+)
- **Permitted Classes:** Assassin, Cleric, Fighter, Magic User, Thief, various multiclass combinations
- **Level Caps:** Magic Users max 11 (Int 18+); Fighters capped 5–7 by Str; Thieves unlimited
- **Ability Score Ranges:** Dex 7–19, others vary

---

### Gnomes

Diminutive gem-lovers with innate magic resistance; lifespan exceeds 650 years. Shorter than dwarves with large noses and extended beards.

- **Magic Resistance:** +1 to saves vs. magic and poison per 3.5 Con points
- **Combat Bonuses:** +1 to hit vs. kobolds and goblins; -4 from larger creatures
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Stonesense:** Within 10 ft (50–80% success rates)
- **Languages:** Common, dwarfish, gnomish, goblin, halfling, kobold; can communicate with burrowing animals
- **Movement:** 90 ft
- **Permitted Classes:** Assassin, Cleric, Fighter, Illusionist, Thief, Fighter/Illusionist, Fighter/Thief, Illusionist/Thief
- **Level Caps:** Vary by class; Thief unlimited
- **Armor Restriction:** Multi-classed gnomes limited to leather armor

---

### Half-Elves

Born of human and elven parents; typically adopt the culture of whichever society raised them.

- **Magic Resistance:** 30% immunity to sleep and charm spells
- **Secret Door Detection:** 2-in-6 chance (secret), 3-in-6 (concealed)
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Movement:** 120 ft
- **Languages:** Common, Elven, Gnoll, Gnome, Goblin, Halfling, Hobgoblin, Orcish
- **Permitted Classes:** Assassin, Cleric, Fighter, Magic User, Ranger, Thief, various multiclass
- **Ability Score Ranges:** Str 3–18, Dex 6–18, Con 6–18, Int 4–18, Wis 3–18, Cha 3–18

---

### Half-Orcs

Most resemble full orcs; some develop enough human traits for character advancement. Lifespan 70+ years.

- **Ability Modifiers:** +1 Strength, +1 Constitution, -2 Charisma
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Movement:** 120 ft
- **Languages:** Common and Orcish; max 2 additional languages regardless of Int
- **Permitted Classes:** Assassin, Cleric, Fighter, Thief, Cleric/Fighter, Cleric/Thief, Cleric/Assassin, Fighter/Thief
- **Level Caps:** Assassin 15, Cleric 4, Fighter 10, Thief 7
- **Ability Score Ranges:** Str 6–18, Dex 3–17, Con 13–19, Int 3–17, Wis 3–14, Cha 3–12
- **Starting Age:** Cleric 20+1d4, Fighter 13+1d4, Thief 20+2d4

---

### Halflings

Diminutive homebodies preferring comfort near human communities; lifespan 150+ years.

- **Ability Modifiers:** -1 Strength, +1 Dexterity
- **Magic & Poison Resistance:** +1 per 3.5 Con points to saves
- **Combat Bonus:** +3 to attacks with bow or sling
- **Surprise:** 4-in-6 chance traveling in non-metal armor alone or 90+ ft ahead (2-in-6 if opening doors)
- **Infravision:** 60 ft
- **Movement:** 90 ft
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarfish, Gnome, Goblin, Halfling, Orcish, up to 2 additional (Int 16+)
- **Permitted Classes:** Fighter, Druid, Thief, Fighter/Thief
- **Level Caps:** Druid 6, Fighter 4, Thief unlimited
- **Ability Score Ranges:** Str 6–17, Dex 8–19, Con 10–18, Int 6–18, Wis 3–17, Cha 3–18
- **Starting Age:** Fighter 20+3d4, Druid 40+3d4, Thief 40+2d4

---

### Humans

The baseline race with no special modifiers. Unrestricted class advancement across most classes.

- **Movement:** 120 ft
- **Level Limits:** Assassin 15, Druid 14, all other permitted classes unlimited
- **Starting Age:** Cleric 20+1d4, Fighter 15+1d4, Mage 24+2d8, Thief 20+1d4
- **Note:** Game masters are advised to ensure humans remain an attractive choice relative to demi-humans.

## Classes

### Assassin

Stealthy killers trained in espionage, disguise, poison, and murder. Must begin with evil alignment.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 12, Dex 12, Con 6, Int 11, Wis 6
- **Hit Die:** d6 (max 15 levels)
- **Alignment:** Any evil
- **Armor:** Leather or studded leather (shields permitted)
- **Weapons:** Any
- **Proficiencies:** 3 + 1 per 4 levels; non-proficiency penalty -3

**Key Abilities:**
- **Assassination:** With surprise, 50% instant kill + 5% per assassin level − 5% per two victim levels
- **Backstab:** +4 to hit from behind; ×2 damage (levels 1–4), ×3 (5–8), ×4 (9–12)
- **Disguise:** 2% daily base chance for observers to detect
- **Thief Abilities:** Available from level 1 onward, granting assassins access to core thief skills (Climb Walls, Find/Disarm Traps, Hide in Shadows, Move Quietly, Open Locks, Pick Pockets, Read Languages)
- **Level 9+:** High-Int (15+) assassins can learn special languages
- **Level 12+:** Can cast from arcane scrolls

**Guild Milestones:** Level 4 (recruit assassin henchmen), Level 8 (recruit thief henchmen), Level 12 (any class henchmen), Level 14 (Guildmaster), Level 15 (Grandmaster)

---

### Cleric

Moral and spiritual leaders; warriors of faith who preach the will of the gods.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 6, Dex 3, Con 6, Int 6, Wis 9, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d8 (max 9 dice)
- **Armor/Shields:** Any
- **Weapons:** Blunt only (club, flail, hammer, mace, staff)
- **Proficiencies:** 2 + 1 every 3 levels; non-proficiency penalty -3

**Key Abilities:**
- **Turn Undead:** Compel undead to flee (3d4 rounds) by rolling ≥ target value on d20 while holding a holy symbol. Evil clerics may control undead instead.
- **Spellcasting:** Spells acquired automatically on leveling; no proficiency check. Wis 13+ grants bonus spells.
- **Level 9:** May establish a temple or stronghold and attract followers.

---

### Druid

Nature priests who worship the spiritual power of the natural world; maintain balance between civilization and wilderness.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 6, Dex 6, Con 6, Int 6, Wis 12, Cha 15
- **Alignment:** Neutral only
- **Hit Die:** d8 (max level 14)
- **Armor:** Leather and wooden shields only (no metal)
- **Weapons:** Club, dagger, dart, hammer, oil, scimitar, sling, spear, staff

**Spell Components:** Requires mistletoe, holly, or oak leaves. Full moon/new moon harvested components provide full power; improperly gathered materials reduce to half or quarter power.

**Key Abilities:**
- **Level 3:** Identify plants/animals; move silently through undergrowth
- **Level 7:** Immunity to fey charm; shapeshift into animals up to 3×/day
- **Druids' Cant:** Secret language
- **+2 saving throw** bonus against fire and lightning

**High-Level Caps:** Only 3 High Druids (12th), 2 Archdruids (13th), and 1 Supreme Druid (14th) may exist simultaneously; advancement requires challenging the incumbent.

---

### Fighter

Melee combat specialists; among the most durable classes.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 9, Dex 6, Con 7, Int 3, Wis 6, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d10 (max 9 dice)
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Experience Bonus:** Str 16+
- **Armor/Shields/Weapons:** Any
- **Proficiencies:** 4 initial + 1 per 2 levels; non-proficiency penalty -2

**Key Abilities:**
- **Bonus Attacks:** Advance on a per-level basis rather than banded ranges. **TODO: Exact per-level progression requires verification against the OSRIC 3.0 Fighter table.**
- **Fighting Unskilled Opponents:** vs. creatures with <1d8 HP, one attack per character level
- **Weapon Specialization (Optional):** +1 to hit, +2 damage, bonus attacks; double specialization: +3 to hit, +3 damage

**Titles:** 9th level = Lord/Lady

---

### Illusionist

Practitioners of phantasmal magic, influencing minds and senses.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 6, Dex 16, Int 15, Wis 6, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d4 (max 10)
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Armor/Shields:** None
- **Weapons:** Dagger, dart, oil, staff
- **Proficiencies:** 1 + 1 every 5 levels; non-proficiency penalty -5

**Spellcasting:** Memorize and cast from spell books written in phantasmal script (readable only by illusionists). Begin with four 1st-level spells (two chosen, two random). Gain one new spell of highest castable level each time they advance.

**Level 10:** May establish a stronghold similar to a fighter's.

---

### Magic User

Rare arcane spellcasters who, despite combat weakness, possess "the most powerful and versatile spells in the game."

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 3, Dex 6, Int 9, Wis 6, Con 6, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d4 (max 11)
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Experience Bonus:** Int 16+
- **Armor/Shields:** None
- **Weapons:** Daggers, darts, oil, staffs only
- **Proficiencies:** 1 initially + 1 per 5 levels

**Spellcasting:** Depend entirely on spell books. Int determines spell acquisition via a Spell Acquisition Table (minimum and maximum spells per level). **TODO: Exact table and starting spell system require verification against the OSRIC 3.0 rulebook.** Receive bonus spells of their highest available level when gaining a level.

**Key Abilities:**
- **Level 7 (Eldritch Craft):** Create potions, scribe scrolls, recharge rods/staves/wands
- **Level 11:** Establish a tower or keep
- **Level 12 (Eldritch Power):** Attempt creation of other magical items

---

### Paladin

Righteous warriors bound to Lawful Good. Breaking this vow requires atonement; intentional breach permanently reduces the character to fighter status.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 12, Dex 6, Con 9, Int 9, Wis 13, Cha 17
- **Hit Die:** d10 (max 9)
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good only
- **Armor/Shields/Weapons:** Any
- **Proficiencies:** 3 + 1 per 2 levels; non-proficiency penalty -2

**Key Abilities:**
- **Improved Saving Throws:** More favorable table than other classes
- **Cure Disease:** Once per week by touch (more uses at higher levels)
- **Detect Evil:** Within 60 ft while concentrating
- **Protection From Evil:** 10-ft aura equivalent to the spell
- **Lay On Hands:** Once daily, heal 2 HP per character level
- **Level 3:** Turn undead
- **Level 4:** Summon a special warhorse (once per decade)
- **Level 8:** Gain bonus attacks (3/2)
- **Level 9:** Access to clerical spellcasting

---

### Ranger

A special variation of the fighter, expert in wilderness situations. Modeled on Aragorn and Robin Hood.

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 13, Dex 6, Con 14, Int 13, Wis 14, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d8 (2 dice at 1st level; max 11 at 10th level)
- **Alignment:** Good (any shift requires becoming a standard fighter; redemption possible through questing)
- **Armor/Weapons:** Any
- **Proficiencies:** 3 + 1 per 2 levels

**Key Restrictions:** Cannot employ mercenaries or servants until 8th level; max three rangers may operate together; limited treasure-carrying capacity by Str.

**Key Abilities:**
- Enhanced surprise detection
- Damage bonus vs. evil humanoids (scales with level)
- Tracking: 90% base in wilderness, 65% in urban settings
- **Level 8+:** Spellcasting from druid and magic-user spell lists; bonus attacks; special followers

---

### Thief

Stealthy characters who operate in urban shadows, often trained by criminal guilds. Must be neutral or evil alignment (neutral good permitted in some campaigns).

- **Min. Ability Scores:** Str 6, Dex 9, Con 6, Int 6, Cha 6
- **Hit Die:** d6
- **Armor:** Leather or studded leather only
- **Weapons:** Club, dagger, dart, oil, sling, single-handed swords (no bastard swords)

**Core Abilities:**
- **Backstab:** Unobserved melee: +4 to hit, ×2 damage (×3 at 5th, ×4 at 9th, ×5 at 13th); modifiers don't multiply
- **Climb Walls:** Scale vertical surfaces impossible for non-thieves
- **Find Traps:** Minute visual inspection of new area (10-minute duration)
- **Hide in Shadows:** Effectively invisible until attacking or leaving shadow
- **Move Quietly:** Supernatural silence across any surface
- **Open Locks / Disarm Traps:** Class-exclusive skill
- **Pick Pockets:** 20%+ failure alerts the target
- **Read Languages:** Decipher non-magical ciphers and languages
- **Thieves' Cant:** Specialized criminal language
- **Level 10 (Read Scrolls):** Cast from arcane or illusionist scrolls (variable success by Int)

## Alignment

Alignment is a character's position on two axes: **good vs. evil** and **law vs. chaos**. Good and evil are objective moral realities in this world, detectable by magic. Each alignment has an associated **alignment language** — words, gestures, and expressions that only same-aligned characters share; cannot be written down.

| Alignment | Nickname | Description |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Lawful Good | Crusader | Discipline + active opposition to evil; tells truth, keeps word, helps others |
| Neutral Good | Benefactor | Does good without strict adherence to law or order |
| Chaotic Good | Rebel | Follows conscience above all; rejects authoritarianism while remaining moral |
| Lawful Neutral | Judge | Order and organization paramount; follows codes with unwavering commitment |
| Neutral | — | Lacks strong commitment to either axis; pragmatically prefers good outcomes |
| Chaotic Neutral | Free Spirit | Prioritizes personal freedom; avoids authority and restrictions |
| Lawful Evil | Dominator | Operates within personal code but shows no mercy; views evil as a duty |
| Neutral Evil | Malefactor | Pursues self-interest without moral restraint; ignores codes as irrelevant |
| Chaotic Evil | Destroyer | Destructive impulses; greed, hatred, and lust for destruction |

## Money & Starting Funds

10 coins = 1 pound. Gold acquisition drives gameplay — 1 GP recovered = 1 XP.

**Currency Conversion:**
- 1 Platinum = 5 Gold
- 1 Gold = 2 Electrum
- 1 Gold = 10 Silver
- 1 Gold = 100 Copper

**Starting Funds by Class:**

| Class | Starting Gold |
|-------|---------------|
| Cleric / Druid | 3d6 × 10 gp (30–180 gp) |
| Fighter / Ranger / Paladin | (3d6+2) × 10 gp (50–200 gp) |
| Magic User / Illusionist | 2d4 × 10 gp (20–80 gp) |
| Thief / Assassin | 2d6 × 10 gp (20–120 gp) |

Multiclass characters receive the highest-tier starting funds among their classes.

## Time & Movement

### Time Scales

| Scale | Duration | Typical Use |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Segment | 6 seconds | Finest granularity; spell casting times |
| Round | 1 minute (10 segments) | Combat |
| Turn | 10 minutes | Dungeon exploration, torch tracking |

The GM normally records passage of time. Wandering monster checks occur periodically; since these encounters rarely have treasure, the mechanic penalizes dawdling.

### Movement Rates

Base movement varies by race and encumbrance (see Encumbrance table below). Standard human base move is 120 ft/round. Small races (dwarves, gnomes, halflings) base at 90 ft; subtract 30 ft from all their movement rates (minimum 30 ft).

## Experience & Gaining Levels

### Earning XP

- **Treasure:** 1 XP per GP value when brought to home base. Magic items kept yield max 1/10 their GP value in XP; full value if sold to an NPC.
- **Monsters:** XP based on Hit Dice tier, modified by special abilities.

### Monster XP Tiers

Creatures are classified levels 1–10+ for XP purposes. Each tier has a base amount + per-HP bonus + special ability bonus + exceptional ability bonus.

### Level Adjustment

If character level vastly exceeds monster level, proportionally reduce XP to prevent farming trivial encounters.

### Training to Advance

After accumulating sufficient XP, characters must train before the level becomes official:
- **Cost:** 1,500 gp per level
- **Duration:** 1d3 weeks per level

**Optional Rule — Random Variable:** 50/50 chance the XP requirement increases or decreases; roll d20 × target level for the adjustment. *(Note: this optional rule is excluded from Open Game Content.)*

## Multi-classing & Dual-classing

### Multi-classing (Demi-humans only)

Non-human characters may advance in multiple classes simultaneously.

- XP gained is divided evenly between all active classes
- HP gained: roll appropriate dice, apply Con modifier, then divide by number of classes
- Different classes may be at different levels simultaneously (e.g., a half-orc Cleric/Fighter 4/15 is possible)
- Racial descriptions specify which armor allows spellcasting (gnomes are more restricted than elves)
- **Special:** Cleric/fighter combinations may use edged weapons

### Dual-classing (Humans only)

Humans may permanently switch from one class to another.

**Requirements:**
- Original class prime requisite: 15+
- New class prime requisite: 17+

**Mechanics:**
- Retain existing HP; can still use original class abilities
- Using original class abilities forfeits all session XP until the new class reaches one level higher than the abandoned class

## Combat

### Combat Sequence Each Round

1. Determine Surprise (d6)
2. Declare spells and general actions (before initiative)
3. Determine Initiative (d6)
4. Party with initiative acts first
5. Losing party acts
6. Round ends; declare next actions if combat continues

---

### 1. Surprise

Each side rolls a d6 at the start of an encounter (once per encounter):
- **Roll 1:** Surprised for 1 segment
- **Roll 2:** Surprised for 2 segments
- **Roll 3–6:** Not surprised

**Dexterity Negation:** A character's Dex surprise bonus negates surprise segments they would suffer.

**Unsurprised Party:** If one side avoids surprise, they gain that many segments to act first (attack, charge, begin spellcasting).

**Monster Variations:** Some monsters surprise on higher rolls (e.g., "surprises on 1–3"), creating more surprise segments.

---

### 2. Declare Actions

Before rolling initiative:
- Spellcasters must announce which spell they'll cast; they may cancel but cannot substitute a different action
- Non-casters declare general intent (attacking, retreating, climbing, etc.)
- GM secretly plans monster actions

---

### 3. Initiative

Both sides roll d6. The party with the **lower** roll acts first. Segments 7–10 are reserved for spell completion or held actions.

**Missile Bonus:** Characters with missile weapons add their missile attack bonus to the initiative roll.

**Ties:** Simultaneous action — damage is inflicted even if one combatant dies during the round.

**Multiple Attacks:** Characters with bonus attacks get entire additional attack routines, usable only after the opponent's initiative segment resolves.

---

### 4. Spellcasting in Combat

Spells have casting times measured in segments. The caster doesn't begin until their initiative segment — that segment is segment 1 of the casting time.

- **Interruption:** Damage taken during casting loses the spell
- **AC Penalty:** Casters lose Dexterity AC bonuses while casting
- **Example:** Party rolls 5, monsters roll 4. A 2-segment spell begins in segment 4 (monster's segment) and completes in segment 6, unless interrupted

---

## Combat Actions & Modifiers

### Primary Actions

**Charge:** Double movement, +2 to hit; usable once per 10 rounds. Defenders with longer weapons attack first without the attacker's Dex AC bonus. Mounted lancers deal double damage.

**Closing into Combat:** Cautious approach prevents attacks that round but also prevents the opponent from attacking until the following round. May advance full movement.

**Fighting Retreat:** Move backward while parrying; cannot attack. Allows switching places with an engaged ally.

**Fleeing:** Opponents gain +4 to hit on immediate attacks when a character breaks and runs.

**Melee Attack:** Str bonuses to hit and to damage apply. Multiple opponents require random target determination.

**Missile Attack:** Dex bonus applies. Shooting into melee risks hitting allies. Missile wielders add missile bonus to initiative.

**Parrying:** Subtract "to hit" bonus from opponent's attack roll; cannot attack in the same round.

**Set Weapon Against Charge:** Spears, lances (dismounted), pole arms, and tridents braced for charge deal double damage; no other attacks permitted unless charged.

**Unarmed Combat:**
- Standard brawling: 1d2 damage
- Grapple: 0–1 damage; restrains opponent
- Overbearing (from charge): leaves target prone

---

| Situation | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Cover (25%)** | -2 to attacker's AC roll (equivalent to +2 AC) |
| **Cover (50%)** | -4 |
| **Cover (75%)** | -7 |
| **Cover (90%)** | -10 |
| **Concealment (¼)** | -1 to AC |
| **Concealment (½)** | -2 to AC |
| **Concealment (¾)** | -3 to AC |
| **Concealment (9/10)** | -4 to AC |
| **Invisible opponent** | -4 to attacker's hit roll; no backstab/flank possible |
| **Prone opponent** | +4 to hit; negates target's shield and Dex bonuses |
| **Rear attack** | +2 to hit; negates target's shield and Dex bonuses |
| **Sleeping opponent (natural)** | Assassin-level effectiveness |
| **Stunned opponent** | +4 to hit; negates shield and Dex bonuses |
| **Flank attack** | Negates shield; rear negates both shield and Dex |

**Invisible Creatures:** Can only be attacked if general location is known. Creatures with keen senses or >6 HD may detect invisibility; those with 11+ HD typically see invisible creatures normally.

---

### Two-Weapon Fighting

- Off-hand weapon must be a dagger or hand axe
- Primary hand: -2 to hit; off-hand: -4 to hit
- Dex bonuses apply to both (but can't turn penalties into bonuses)
- Off-hand weapon cannot parry

## Damage, Death & Healing

### Hit Points & Unconsciousness

- At **0 HP:** Character becomes unconscious; loses 1 additional HP per round from bleeding
- At **-10 HP:** Death
- Any damage to an unconscious character (other than bleeding) kills instantly
- Bleeding stops immediately if aid is provided in the same round

### Recovery from Unconsciousness

Regaining HP through magic or potions doesn't mean immediate recovery:
- Coma lasts 1d6 turns
- Must rest at least one week before strenuous activity
- Severe injuries (-6 HP or below) often cause permanent scarring

### Natural Healing

- **Basic:** 1 HP per day of uninterrupted rest
- **Constitution Penalties:** Characters with negative Con modifier must wait (in days equal to the penalty) before healing begins
- **Constitution Bonuses:** After one week of rest, characters with positive Con modifier gain accelerated recovery in the second week (bonus HP equal to Con modifier)
- **Full Recovery:** Four weeks of rest restores any character to full HP regardless of how many HP were lost

### Subdual Damage

When desired, damage can be composed of half "real" and half "subdual" (non-lethal). Subdual damage heals at 1 HP per hour. Only applicable to humanoids and special creatures such as dragons. Both parties must agree to subdual terms before combat begins.

### Resurrection

Dead characters may return via clerical magic. Elves cannot be resurrected (they lack souls). Low-level parties typically must seek NPC clerics, who charge at least 1,000 gp for the service.

### Falling Damage

| Fall Distance | Damage |
|---------------|--------|
| Less than 5 ft | None |
| Up to 10 ft | 1d6 |
| Up to 20 ft | 3d6 |
| Up to 30 ft | 6d6 |
| Up to 40 ft | 10d6 |
| Up to 50 ft | 15d6 |
| Over 50 ft | 20d6 |

**Optional:** GM may allow a saving throw; success halves damage.

### Level Drain (Energy Drain)

- The drained character loses one complete experience level and returns to the starting XP of their new lower level
- Multi-/dual-classed characters lose the highest level they've attained; ties resolved randomly
- A character drained below level 1 is slain (and may rise as undead)
- NPCs lose a level or hit die (whichever applies)

## Turning the Undead

Clerics (and paladins at level 3) can turn undead by rolling ≥ the target number on a d20. The cleric must hold a holy symbol aloft and cannot attack in the same round. Turned undead flee at maximum speed for 3d4 rounds.

**Evil clerics** may control undead instead of turning them.

## Morale

Most monsters check morale when losing a fight. Fearless creatures (trolls, most undead) never check. Maximum of two morale checks per battle; passing a second check means the creatures fight to the death.

**Base Morale:** 50% + 5% per Hit Die

| Situation | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Per friend killed, surrendered, or fled | +5% |
| Own side at 25% casualties | +5% |
| Numerical inferiority | +10% |
| Own side at 50% casualties | +15% |
| Own side greatly outnumbered (2:1+) | +20% |
| Own leader hors de combat | +25% |
| Per foe killed, surrendered, or fled | -5% |
| Own side inflicted 25% casualties on enemy | -5% |
| Numerical superiority | -10% |
| Own side inflicted 50% casualties on enemy | -15% |

**Failure Results:**
- Failed by ≤25%: Fighting withdrawal
- Failed by 26–50%: Flight
- Failed by >50%: Surrender (GM considers intelligence and tactics)

**No Quarter:** Creatures who believe surrender means execution fight to the death if escape is impossible.

**Player Characters** never check morale. Hirelings, henchmen, and men-at-arms do (modified by employer's Cha and treatment).

## Poison, Disease & Insanity

### Poison

Three categories: animal venoms, vegetable toxins, and magical poisons. On contact, target makes a saving throw; failure typically results in death (character incapacitated immediately, not instantly — window of ~15 minutes for *slow poison* or *neutralise poison* spells).

- Assassins may craft vegetable toxins with longer shelf lives
- Venom from creature glands loses effectiveness immediately for player use
- Good-aligned classes (paladins, etc.) refuse to use poison
- Dragons and similarly powerful creatures are typically immune

### Disease

- Plague: saving throw vs. poison; success grants +4 bonus on future saves vs. that disease
- Failure: 2d8-day incubation, then 2d8 days of -1d6 to all characteristics and rolls; rolling 8 on either die is fatal
- Infected wounds (sewers, unsanitary conditions): similar mechanics with onset in hours

### Insanity

GM selects or randomly determines condition:

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Homicidal Mania | Compulsive murder of same-race individuals |
| Kleptomania | Irresistible theft urge; 50% violent response if items confiscated |
| Megalomania | Belief in absolute superiority; violence at challenges |
| Paranoia | Dangerous distrust affecting party cohesion |
| Pathological Liar | Compulsive deception |
| Schizophrenia | Personality splits into 1d3 separate identities |
| Suicidal Mania | Compulsion toward self-harm and reckless behavior |

### Henchmen (Classed NPCs)

Henchmen are leveled adventurers who function as "companions, friends, and supporters." Maximum number limited by Charisma score.

**Availability:** In a city of 30,000, roughly 300 advancement-capable characters exist, of which ~10% serve as henchmen.

**Recruiting (once monthly, up to four methods):**

| Method | Cost | Response |
|--------|------|----------|
| Public crier | 10 gp | 1d10% of available |
| Posted notices | 50 gp | 10d4% of available |
| Agents | 300 gp | 5d10% (minimum 10%) |
| Taverns/inns | 10 gp/visit | 1d4% per 10 gp (max 5d4%) |

**Class Distribution (random):** Fighters 40%, Magic Users 20%, Clerics 20%, Thieves 20%.

**Starting Level:** Typically 1st. Characters 6th+: 1-in-10 applicants are 2nd level. Characters 12th+: 1-in-4 are 2nd, another 1-in-4 are 3rd.

**Compensation:**
- Base salary of 100 gp/level yields 30% acceptance; +1% per additional 10 gp (max 60%)
- Providing equipment: +15% acceptance
- No room/board: -25% acceptance
- Typical treasure share: half-share + healing support
