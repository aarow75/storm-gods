# RuneQuest Equipment

Costs are in **Lunars (L)** — the silver standard coin of the Lunar Empire (a Guilder or other silver piece is worth the same). Encumbrance is in ENC "things"; carrying more ENC than your STR allows slows you down and penalizes defense.

**Starting money** in RuneQuest Classic depends on background: a peasant starts with D100 L, a townsman 2D100 L, a barbarian D100 L (plus a riding animal, leather armor, and basic weapons), and nobles draw a yearly stipend. All beginning adventurers own the clothes on their back, a belt knife, and fire-making gear.
