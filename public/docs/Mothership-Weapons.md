# Mothership Weapons & Armor

In Mothership you use your **Combat** stat when fighting. A relevant skill (Firearms, Close-Quarters Combat) will often increase your Combat stat temporarily. Weapons often have special abilities of their own.

**Range** — each ranged weapon has Short/Medium/Long ranges (meters):
- **Short**: no penalty to the shooter's Combat stat.
- **Medium**: −10% to the shooter's Combat stat.
- **Long**: maximum effective range; firing confers Disadvantage on the Combat check.

**Aiming** — spend a full turn (both actions) aiming without taking damage to gain Advantage on your next ranged shot. You can hold an aim indefinitely as long as you aren't damaged, grappled, shoved, or moved.

**Reloading** — takes a significant action, unless you have Military Training or Firearms, in which case it's a free action (assuming ammo is easily accessible).

**Automatic weapons** (Pulse Rifle, SMG) must reload after every shot unless you have Military Training or Firearms, in which case you can fire three times before reloading — shown as "Shots: 1(3)".

**Special abilities** — "+10 (Combat)" adds to your Combat stat while using the weapon; "+10 (Firearms)" adds only if you have the Firearms skill; "−10 (vs. Armor save)" means your opponent subtracts 10 from their Armor save against this weapon.

### Combat Shotgun
The Kano X9. Damage 2d10, but **½ damage at medium range, ¼ damage at long range**. Audio range-finder beeps when non-friendlies approach within 10m. Stormassault slugs: knockback on hit, knockdown on critical hit. 4 shots per reload.

### Crowbar
Doubles as a tool: confers Advantage on Strength checks to open jammed airlocks or lift heavy objects.

### Flame Thrower
The Ramhorn 1. On hit, target must make a Body save or be **set on fire, taking 1d10 damage per turn** until extinguished. Fires liquid flame up to 20m; the fire stays where it lands. Holds 8 shots.

### Flare Gun
The Peabody. High-intensity flare visible day and night from 25km away. 2 shots per reload.

### Frag Grenade
Deals 1d10 damage to **everyone within a 20m radius**. Can often do 1 Megadamage to a ship if placed in its engine room or bridge. Often loaded in pulse rifle grenade launchers.

### Hand Welder
The EVA Mk II. Ultra heat emitter cuts through airlocks and heavy doors; **−5 (vs. Armor save)**. Compressed tank holds 6 charges — explosive if punctured.

### Laser Cutter
The MNC Model A, built to scrap ships. Damage 1d% (or **1 Megadamage against a vehicle or ship**). Wide-beam laser, 700m range; **−15 (vs. Armor save)**; takes 1 round to recharge between shots. Solar battery holds 6 shots — recharges in 1 hour from a ship's generator, or 6 hours via solar power.

### Nail Gun
The Rosco S56, built for spacecraft repair. Heavy-duty nail slugs are **−10 (vs. Armor save)**. Auto-loader magazine holds 32 shots.

### Pulse Rifle
The F20 "Arbiter", fully automatic — Shots: 1(3). Smart-link system: **+5 (Combat if wearing a HUD)**. Phosphorus rounds do **×2 damage on a critical hit**. Pump-action grenade launcher holds 6 frag grenades.

### Revolver
The FN "Slug Gun". Kineti-slugs: **knockdown on critical hit, −5 (vs. Armor save)**. 8 shots per reload.

### Rigging Gun
The HAN-290 mining harpoon. On hit, target must make a Body save or become **tied** by the 500m micro-filament (hard to cut). 1d10 damage if the harpoon is pulled out. Holds 1 shot.

### Scalpel
Grants **+10% to Surgery skill rolls**. Can be used as a weapon.

### Smart Rifle
The SK 109 "Seeker". **Triple damage on critical hits.** Armor-piercing rounds: **−10 (vs. Armor save)**, 12 shots per magazine. Smart-link system: **+10 (Combat if wearing a HUD)**. Thermal scope and spectroscope grant night/thermal vision.

### SMG
The Arma 29 submachine gun, fully automatic. Collapsible stock allows one-handed use. Ultra-capacity magazine: Shots 1(5).

### Stun Baton
Electrified melee baton carried by security teams. Deals 1d10 damage; targets struck are prone to being stunned rather than killed — the tool of choice for taking someone alive.

### Vibechete
A machete that vibrates at ultra-high speeds to cut cleanly through dense foliage. Can be used as a weapon (2d10).

## Armor

Armor in Mothership adds a bonus to your **Armor save**.

### Standard Crew Attire
*Armor save +0%, 20Cr.* Coveralls and leather jackets — the standard outfit worn by crew members aboard spacecraft. Basic assumed attire for all classes.

### Hazard Suit
*Armor save +5%, 750Cr.* Built for scientists and explorers on alien planets. Air filter with up to 1 hour of stored air; filters most toxic atmospheres. Protects against extreme heat and cold. Hydration reclaimer makes 1 liter of water last 4 days. Includes shortwave comms and headlamp.

### Vaccsuit
*Armor save +7%, 1,000Cr.* The basic suit worn while operating in space. Connected to an oxygen tank, holds enough O2 for 12 hours (6 under stress). Contains short-range comms and two headlamps. **Speed checks in a Vaccsuit are always at Disadvantage.** If punctured, an internal monitor announces decompression within 60 seconds.

### Standard Battle Dress
*Armor save +10%, 750Cr.* Light plated armor — the standard dress for marines going into combat.

### Advanced Battle Dress
*Armor save +15%, 1,500Cr.* Standard dress for marines in combat-heavy offworld engagements. Includes closed-system shortwave comms, body cam, and headlamp. A small exoskeleton weave lets the wearer **carry twice the weight** they normally could. **Speed checks in ABD are always at Disadvantage.**
