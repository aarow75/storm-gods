# Dragonbane Spells

> This page describes the spell library used by the character creator. It is a streamlined library organized into General Magic and the three schools of Animism, Elementalism, and Mentalism; details may differ from the printed core rulebook's spell list.

## Casting Rules

To cast a spell you spend **Willpower Points (WP)** and roll against your skill level in the relevant school. Any school can be used to cast General Magic spells. On a failure the spell has no effect, but the WP are still spent.

- **Power level** (1–3): casting costs **2 WP per power level**. Spells that don't use power level always cost 2 WP. **Magic tricks** always succeed and cost 1 WP; they don't count against prepared spells.
- **Prepared spells**: you can hold prepared a number of spells equal to your base chance for INT. Unprepared spells can be cast from your grimoire at twice the casting time (reaction spells cannot).
- **Magic and metal**: you cannot use magic while wearing metal armor or holding a metal weapon (including partially metal items like axes, spears, studded leather, and arrows — but not staves, clubs, or slings).
- **Requirements**: *Word* (a chant or power word, not a whisper), *Gesture* (specific hand movements), *Focus* (an item held in hand), *Ingredient* (consumed in casting).
- **Casting time**: an action in combat unless stated. *Reaction* spells are cast outside your turn and don't replace your action. *Rituals* take a stretch or shift.
- **Range**: *Personal* affects only the caster. Area spells (*sphere*, *cone*) can be dodged but not parried; exempting targets in the area gives a bane on the roll.
- **Duration**: *Instant*, *Round* (until your turn next round), *Stretch*, *Shift*, or *Concentration* (ends if you act, take damage, or fail a WIL roll against fear).
- **Rolling a Dragon** (1): the target must roll a dragon to resist/parry/dodge, and you choose one: double damage or range; the spell costs no WP; or immediately cast another spell with a bane.
- **Rolling a Demon** (20): the roll cannot be pushed and a **magical mishap** occurs (see table at the end).

## General Magic

### Cantrip
*Magic trick · Requirement: gesture · Action · Range 2m · Duration: Instant.* A minor, harmless display of magic: snuff or light a candle, chill a drink, make sparks dance, clean a garment. Always succeeds, costs 1 WP.

### Comprehend Language
*Rank 1 · Word, focus · Action · Range: personal · Duration: stretch.* You understand a spoken or written language you are exposed to. The spell does not let you speak or write it. Each additional power level extends the duration one step (stretch → shift).

### Fetch
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* An unattended object of up to 2 kg floats to your open hand. Each additional power level increases the weight limit by 2 kg or the range by 10 meters. Snatching a held item requires an opposed roll against the holder's STR.

### Flaming Hands
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 2m (cone) · Duration: Instant.* Fire fans out from your fingers. Everyone in the cone takes 2D6 fire damage; each additional power level adds one die. Flammable objects may catch fire.

### Harm
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* Black energy wracks the target's body for 2D6 damage, ignoring armor. Each additional power level adds one die. Has no effect on the undead.

### Heal
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* The target regains 2D6 HP. Each additional power level adds one die. Cannot be cast using Power from the Body.

### Identify
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Ritual (stretch) · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* You learn the properties, purpose, and command words of one magical item, and whether it is cursed. The GM may hide particularly deep secrets behind a bane on the roll.

### Illusion
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You create a silent, immaterial illusion up to the size of a horse. Each additional power level lets it make sound or grow one size category. A viewer who has reason to doubt may make an opposed INT roll to see through it.

### Knock
*Rank 1 · Word · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* One lock, bar, or bolt within range springs open. Magically sealed closures require the spell's power level to equal or exceed the sealing spell's.

### Levitate
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 6m · Duration: Instant.* You levitate yourself or another person or object of up to human size and let it float up to 6 meters in any direction, after which it lands gently or drops (you decide). Each additional power level moves the target another 2 meters or affects an additional person or object. Levitating an unwilling creature gives a bane on the roll.

### Light
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: shift.* A hovering globe of light illuminates a 10-meter radius. It can be attached to an object or made to follow you. Each additional power level doubles the radius or creates another globe.

### Lock
*Rank 1 · Word · Action · Range 10m · Duration: shift.* One door, chest, or container within range seals shut. Opening it by force requires a STR roll with a bane; KNOCK of equal or higher power level opens it instantly. Each additional power level extends the duration one step.

### Mend
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Ritual (stretch) · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* A broken mundane object no larger than a human is restored to a whole state — a shattered pot, a torn cloak, a snapped sword. Magical items cannot be mended this way.

### Missile Shield
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Reaction · Range: personal · Duration: Instant.* An invisible barrier deflects one ranged attack against you. At power level 2 it protects against all ranged attacks until your next turn; at power level 3 it also covers those adjacent to you.

### Protection
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture, focus · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* A ward settles over the target, giving a boon to all rolls to resist spells and magical effects. Each additional power level protects one more target.

### Push/Pull
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* A wave of force shoves or drags one creature or object of up to human size 2D4 meters, dealing the same amount in bludgeoning damage if it strikes something solid. Each additional power level adds one die or one size category.

### Reveal/Conceal
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant (reveal) or shift (conceal).* Reveal exposes hidden or invisible creatures, objects, and magical auras in the area. Conceal hides one object or creature of up to human size from sight; it is exposed if it attacks or is touched.

### Second Sight
*Rank 1 · Word, focus · Action · Range: personal · Duration: stretch.* You perceive magical auras: enchanted items, active spells, and creatures sustained by magic glow to your eyes. Each additional power level extends the duration one step.

### Silence
*Rank 2 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: stretch.* A globe of stillness 10 meters across swallows all sound. No noise can be made inside; spells requiring a Word cannot be cast within it. An unwilling target caught at the center may make a WIL roll to step free.

### Sleep
*Rank 2 · Word · Action · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* The target must succeed with a WIL roll or fall into a deep sleep for one stretch. The victim rolls with a boon at power level 1, normally at power level 2, and with a bane at power level 3. On a success the victim still becomes Dazed. A sleeper is very difficult to wake, but wakes upon taking damage. Only affects the living; no effect on monsters.

### Sneak
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The target's footfalls and rustling gear are muffled, granting a boon to SNEAKING rolls. Each additional power level affects one more target.

### Stun
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: round.* A crack of force staggers the target, who must make a WIL roll or become Dazed and lose their next action. Each additional power level gives the victim a bane on the roll or affects an additional target.

## Animism

### Animal Friendship
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: shift.* One ordinary animal regards you as a trusted friend. It will follow, protect, and perform simple tasks for you within its nature. Each additional power level affects a larger or an additional animal. No effect on monsters.

### Bewitch Animal
*Rank 2 · Word · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You take direct control of an ordinary animal's actions, making an opposed roll against its WIL. Larger or magical beasts give a bane on the roll. When the spell ends, the animal remembers what it was made to do.

### Commune with Nature
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture, ingredient (sacred herbs) · Ritual (shift) · Range: personal · Duration: Instant.* The spirits of the land answer three questions about the surrounding terrain, weather, creatures, or hidden places within a day's travel. Their answers are truthful but often oblique.

### Drain Life Force
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* You tear the vitality from a living target: it takes 2D6 damage (opposed by its CON) and you regain the same amount of HP. Each additional power level adds one die. No effect on the undead or monsters.

### Find the Path
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Ritual (stretch) · Range: personal · Duration: shift.* You unerringly know the most direct safe route toward a destination you name, so long as a path exists. Gives a boon to BUSHCRAFT rolls for leading the way.

### Life Sense
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range: personal · Duration: stretch.* You feel the presence and rough direction of living creatures within 20 meters, even through walls. Each additional power level doubles the radius.

### Nature's Armor
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture, ingredient (bark) · Action · Range: personal · Duration: stretch.* Bark and thorny vines wrap your body, giving armor rating 2 without hindering movement or spellcasting. Each additional power level increases the armor rating by 2. Only the highest armor rating counts if you also wear armor.

### Nature's Servant
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Ritual (stretch) · Range 10m · Duration: stretch.* A mass of roots, stone, and soil rises as a servant of roughly human size to labor or fight for you. Each additional power level increases its size one category. When the spell ends it collapses into dead matter.

### Pass Without Trace
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The target leaves no tracks, scent, or broken twigs. Attempts to track them automatically fail; each additional power level covers one more traveler.

### Plant Growth
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: stretch.* Vegetation in a 10-meter radius erupts into dense growth. The area becomes difficult to cross (movement halved), and creatures in it when the spell is cast must make an EVADE roll or be caught fast until they break free with a STR roll.

### Purify
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* Spoiled food, fouled water, or poison in one meal or dose is rendered harmless. At power level 2 the spell also gives a boon to HEALING rolls to resist disease; at power level 3 it can neutralize poison already in a creature's body.

### Spirit Ward
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* A protective circle of whispering spirits surrounds the target. Undead and spirits get a bane on all attacks against them. Each additional power level protects one more target.

### Tree Walk
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: Instant.* You step into a tree and out of another tree you have seen, up to one kilometer away. Each additional power level doubles the distance or brings one more person with you.

### Warp Wood
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* Wooden objects twist and buckle: a door springs or jams, a bow warps useless, a haft bends. A held or wielded item is opposed by the holder's STR. Each additional power level affects a larger object or an additional item.

## Elementalism

### Air Elemental
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture, ingredient (a breath in a bottle) · Ritual (stretch) · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You summon a howling elemental of wind to fight and serve you. It moves swiftly, can carry a person aloft, and batters enemies with gusts. If your concentration breaks, it disperses — or worse, runs wild.

### Breathe Water
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The target can breathe water as easily as air. Each additional power level affects one more target or extends the duration one step.

### Call Lightning
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 20m · Duration: Instant.* Under open sky, a bolt of lightning strikes a target of your choice for 3D6 damage; anyone adjacent takes half. Each additional power level adds one die. Requires clouds above — under a clear sky the spell fails, though the WP are not lost.

### Dust Devil
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: round.* A whirl of dust and grit engulfs the target, who gets a bane on attacks and AWARENESS rolls until your next turn. Each additional power level affects an additional target or extends the duration one step.

### Earth Strength
*Rank 1 · Word, ingredient (stone) · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The steadiness of stone flows into the target: boon on STR rolls, and they cannot be toppled. Each additional power level affects one more target.

### Earthquake
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 20m (sphere) · Duration: round.* The ground heaves and cracks. Everyone in the area must make an EVADE roll or fall and take 2D6 bludgeoning damage; structures take 2D10. Each additional power level adds one die to both.

### Extinguish
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* Fires up to the size of a campfire in the area are snuffed out instantly. Each additional power level extinguishes larger fires, up to a burning building at power level 3. Magical fire is opposed by the caster's power level.

### Fire Elemental
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture, ingredient (burning coal) · Ritual (stretch) · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You summon a raging elemental of living flame. It ignites what it touches and strikes with burning limbs. If your concentration breaks, it disperses — or rampages freely.

### Flaming Weapon
*Rank 1 · Word, ingredient (sulfur) · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* One weapon wreathes itself in fire, dealing an extra D6 fire damage. Each additional power level adds one die or enchants an additional weapon. The flame casts light as a torch.

### Freeze
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: round.* Frost seizes the target's limbs: their movement is halved and they get a bane on AGL-based rolls. Water in the area freezes solid. Each additional power level affects an additional target or extends the duration one step.

### Gust of Wind
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m (cone) · Duration: Instant.* A great gust of wind: all untethered objects and creatures up to human size in the area are pushed 2D4 meters away from you and suffer the same amount of bludgeoning damage. Against a swarm the spell deals 2D6 damage. Each additional power level adds one die. No effect on Large or Huge monsters.

### Ice Blast
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 20m · Duration: Instant.* A spear of razor ice strikes the target for 2D8 piercing damage; on a hit the target must also make a STR roll or be Frozen in place until they break free. Each additional power level adds one die.

### Stone Skin
*Rank 1 · Word, gesture, ingredient (stone) · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The target's skin turns hard and gray, gaining armor rating 4. Each power level beyond the first increases the armor rating by 2. If you wear armor, only the highest armor rating counts.

### Tornado
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 20m · Duration: concentration.* A roaring funnel of wind tears across the battlefield. Each round you may move it 10 meters; creatures it touches are flung 2D6 meters and take that much bludgeoning damage, and loose objects are scattered. Large and Huge monsters are unaffected.

### Water Elemental
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture, ingredient (vial of spring water) · Ritual (stretch) · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You summon a surging elemental of water. It can douse fires, sweep enemies off their feet, and drag victims beneath the surface. If your concentration breaks, it collapses into a harmless flood.

## Mentalism

### Calm
*Rank 1 · Word · Action · Range 10m · Duration: stretch.* Soothing stillness settles over the target: rage, panic, and fear drain away, and ongoing fear effects end. An unwilling target opposes with WIL. Each additional power level affects one more target.

### Daze
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: round.* The target's thoughts scatter. They must make a WIL roll or become Dazed and get a bane on their next roll. Each additional power level gives the victim a bane on the roll or affects another target.

### Detect Thoughts
*Rank 2 · Focus · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You hear the surface thoughts of one creature you can see, opposed by its WIL. Deeper secrets require winning a new opposed roll each round. The target feels a faint pressure but does not know its source.

### Dominate
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* You take complete control of another person's actions, making an opposed roll against the victim's WIL. At power level 1 you get a bane, at power level 2 you roll normally, at power level 3 you get a boon. If you win, the victim immediately makes a movement and performs an action of your choice (except actions that spend WP), and loses their next turn. No effect on monsters.

### Fear
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* Terror floods the target's mind: they must make a WIL roll or become Scared and flee from you for one stretch. At higher power levels the victim rolls with a bane, or the spell affects an additional target.

### Forget
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* You erase the target's memory of the last stretch of time, opposed by its WIL. Each additional power level erases a longer span (shift, then day). The lost memories can be restored only by DISPEL or powerful magic.

### Hallucination
*Rank 2 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You plant a vivid false perception in one mind — a figure that isn't there, a bridge over the chasm, flames licking the walls. Opposed by the target's WIL; only the target perceives it. Each additional power level affects one more mind.

### Haste
*Rank 2 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: round.* Time quickens around the target: they may immediately perform an extra action, and their movement is doubled until your next turn. Each additional power level extends the effect one round. When the spell ends the target becomes Exhausted.

### Hex
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Action · Range 10m · Duration: stretch.* A crawling misfortune clings to the target, who gets a bane on all skill rolls, opposed by its WIL. Each additional power level extends the duration one step. DISPEL lifts the hex.

### Invisibility
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range: touch · Duration: stretch.* The target fades entirely from sight. Attacking, casting a spell, or taking damage ends the effect. Each additional power level conceals an additional target.

### Mental Blast
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* A hammer of pure will strikes the target's mind for 2D6 damage, ignoring armor, opposed by its WIL. Each additional power level adds one die. No effect on mindless creatures.

### Mind Bond
*Rank 1 · Word, focus · Action · Range: touch · Duration: shift.* You link your mind to a willing creature. You can exchange thoughts and feelings at any distance for the duration and always know the direction to one another. Each additional power level adds one more mind to the bond.

### Mind Shield
*Rank 1 · Gesture · Reaction · Range: personal · Duration: Instant.* A wall of will deflects one spell or effect that targets your mind, which has no effect on you. At power level 2 the protection lasts until your next turn; at power level 3 it also covers one adjacent ally.

### Paralyze
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: round.* The target's body locks rigid, opposed by its WIL. A paralyzed victim can neither move nor act, though they remain aware. Each additional power level extends the effect one round. No effect on monsters.

### Project Mind
*Rank 3 · Word, focus · Ritual (stretch) · Range: personal · Duration: concentration.* Your senses leave your body, which lies defenseless. Your invisible presence can travel up to one kilometer, passing through anything but solid stone and warded barriers, seeing and hearing all it passes.

### See Through Eyes
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You see through the eyes of another creature within range (unwilling targets oppose with WIL). Once established, the link persists to any distance while you concentrate.

### Suggestion
*Rank 2 · Word · Action · Range 10m · Duration: Instant.* You plant a single reasonable-sounding course of action in the target's mind, opposed by its WIL. The victim follows the suggestion as if it were their own idea, but will not knowingly harm themselves. No effect on monsters.

### Telekinesis
*Rank 2 · Gesture · Action · Range 10m · Duration: concentration.* You move an object or creature of up to human size with your mind, up to 10 meters per round. Unwilling creatures oppose with STR. A held object can be wrenched away by winning an opposed roll against the holder's STR. Each additional power level increases the size limit one category.

### Terror
*Rank 3 · Word, gesture · Action · Range 10m (sphere) · Duration: Instant.* Waves of dread crash over everyone in the area, who must make WIL rolls or become Scared and flee for one stretch; even on a success they become Dazed. Each additional power level gives victims a bane on the roll.

### True Sight
*Rank 2 · Word, focus · Action · Range: personal · Duration: stretch.* Your eyes pierce all deception: illusions, invisibility, disguises, and shapeshifted forms appear as they truly are. Each additional power level extends the duration one step.

## Magical Mishap Table

A magical mishap occurs if you roll a Demon (20) when casting a spell. Roll a D20:

| D20 | Magical Mishap |
|-----|----------------|
| 1 | The magical powers leave you Dazed. |
| 2 | The spellcasting suddenly makes you Exhausted. |
| 3 | The energies take a toll on your body and make you Sickly. |
| 4 | You lose control of the spell, which makes you very Angry. |
| 5 | The spell subjects you to demonic visions that leave you Scared. |
| 6 | You see the world beyond the veil and realize your own insignificance. You feel Disheartened. |
| 7 | The magic ravages your body, inflicting D6 damage per power level. |
| 8 | The spell drains your willpower and you lose D6 WP per power level. |
| 9 | The spell gives rise to a magical disease with virulence 3D6. You and everyone you contact during the next shift are exposed. |
| 10 | Another random spell of yours activates instead, with the same target and power level. |
| 11 | You vomit a frog the moment you tell a lie. Roll D4 every morning; on a 1 it wears off. DISPEL also lifts it. |
| 12 | Any gold or silver you touch withers into dust. Roll D4 every morning; on a 1 it wears off. DISPEL also lifts it. |
| 13 | The spell blinds you; you act as if in total darkness. Roll D4 every morning; on a 1 you recover. DISPEL also lifts it. |
| 14 | Amnesia — you forget who you and the other player characters are. Roll D4 every morning; on a 1 your memory returns. |
| 15 | The spell also affects a friend or other unintended victim. A healing or helping spell affects an enemy. |
| 16 | The spell backfires: an offensive spell affects you instead; a protecting or healing spell inflicts damage instead. |
| 17 | You turn into an animal (D6: 1 cat, 2 fox, 3 goat, 4 wolf, 5 deer, 6 bear), retaining your mental acuity. Roll D4 every morning; on a 1 you revert. |
| 18 | You become one age category younger. Permanent; you age normally from your new age. |
| 19 | You become one age category older. Permanent; you age normally from your new age. |
| 20 | Your magic attracts a demon from another dimension. It shows up within the next shift and attacks or causes trouble. |
