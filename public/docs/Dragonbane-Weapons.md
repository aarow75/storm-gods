# Dragonbane Weapons & Armor

> This page describes the weapon list used by the character creator (a streamlined list; damage dice and prices may differ from the printed core rulebook tables). Costs are in silver.

**Weapon features** (from the core rules):

- **Subtle**: grants a boon and increased damage on sneak attacks.
- **Long**: melee attacks can hit an enemy up to 4 meters away (two squares).
- **Toppling**: grants a boon when trying to topple an opponent.
- **Piercing / Slashing / Bludgeoning**: the damage type can affect the effectiveness of armor. A weapon that is both slashing and piercing must choose before the attack roll.
- **STR requirement**: you need the listed STR to use the weapon effectively.
- **Mastercrafted weapons** cost ×10, reduce the STR requirement by 3, and parry more damage before breaking.

A shield lets you parry attacks with the Shields (STR) skill and adds +1 to your armor rating.

## Armor

You can only wear one suit of armor at a time (a helmet can be added in the full rules). The **armor rating** is subtracted from the damage of each hit.

| Armor | Armor Rating | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| None | 0 | — |
| Leather | 1 | No penalties; the adventurer's default |
| Studded Leather | 2 | Bane on SNEAKING rolls |
| Chain Mail | 3 | Bane on EVADE and SNEAKING rolls; metal — prevents spellcasting |
| Plate Armor | 4 | Bane on ACROBATICS, EVADE, and SNEAKING rolls; metal — prevents spellcasting |
| Full Plate | 6 | Custom-fitted battle plate; bane on ACROBATICS, EVADE, and SNEAKING; metal — prevents spellcasting |

**Magic and metal**: a mage cannot cast spells while wearing metal armor (chain mail and heavier, including studded leather in the printed rules) or holding a metal weapon.
