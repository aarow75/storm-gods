# Dragonbane Equipment

Costs are in silver. **Supply** indicates availability: *Common* items can be found in any village or marketplace; *Uncommon* items can usually only be purchased from the artisan making them; *Rare* items must generally be specially ordered.

**Encumbrance**: you can carry a number of items equal to half your STR, rounded up. Carrying more gives a bane on all STR- and AGL-based rolls. A backpack raises the limit.
