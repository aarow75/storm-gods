# Dragonbane Mechanics Reference

*Extracted from Dragonbane Core Rulebook — mechanical rules only, no lore or flavor text.*

---

## Abbreviations & Dice

**Attributes:** STR (Strength), CON (Constitution), AGL (Agility), INT (Intelligence), WIL (Willpower), CHA (Charisma)
**Derived:** HP (Hit Points), WP (Willpower Points)
**Dice:** D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20. D3 = roll D6, divide by 2, round up.

---

## Core Mechanics

### Skill Rolls
Roll D20 equal to or under your skill level to succeed. A result of **1 = Dragon** (extra success). A result of **20 = Demon** (automatic failure, cannot push).

### Time Units
- **Round** = 10 seconds (used in combat)
- **Stretch** = 15 minutes (dungeon exploration scale)
- **Shift** = 6 hours (4 shifts per day)

### Currency
- 10 copper coins (CP) = 1 silver coin (SP)
- 10 silver coins (SP) = 1 gold coin (GP)

---

## Character Creation

**Steps:**
1. Choose or roll Kin (D12)
2. Choose or roll Profession (D10)
3. Roll Age (D6)
4. Roll and assign Attributes
5. Calculate derived values (HP, WP, Movement, Damage Bonus)
6. Pick trained skills per age
7. Record heroic ability from profession
8. Roll starting gear
9. Choose a Memento
10. Choose a Weakness (optional, D20)

## Kin

**Kin Table (D12):** 1–4 Human, 5–6 Halfling, 7–8 Dwarf, 9–10 Elf, 11 Mallard, 12 Wolfkin

### Human
- **Adaptive** (3 WP): When making a skill roll, declare you are using a different skill than normally required. Must be a skill you have trained. Succeeds on a Dragon roll automatically.

### Halfling
- **Hard to Catch** (3 WP): Gain a boon on EVADE when dodging an attack.

### Dwarf
- **Unforgiving** (3 WP): Gain a boon on attack rolls against a creature that has damaged you this session.

### Elf
- **Inner Peace** (—): During a stretch rest, enter deep meditation instead of sleeping. Gain an extra D6 recovery to both HP and WP, plus recover one additional condition. While meditating, you are unresponsive to the world around you.

### Mallard
- **Ill-Tempered** (3 WP): Gain a boon on any skill roll and become Angry. Cannot be used for INT-based rolls.
- **Webbed Feet** (—): Gain a boon on SWIMMING rolls. Move at full speed in water.

### Wolfkin
- **Hunting Instincts** (3 WP): Spend an action to designate a creature as prey. You can track it by scent for up to 1 day. Spend 1 WP for a boon on any attack roll against your designated prey.

## Attributes

**Rolling Attributes:**
1. Roll 4D6, drop the lowest die. Record the result.
2. Repeat 6 times for a total of 6 values.
3. Assign each value to one of the 6 attributes (STR, CON, AGL, INT, WIL, CHA).
4. You may swap any two attribute values.
5. Apply age modifiers.
6. Maximum attribute value is 18.

**Attributes:**
- **STR** (Strength): Physical power, melee damage, encumbrance
- **CON** (Constitution): Toughness, HP
- **AGL** (Agility): Speed, reflexes, ranged combat
- **INT** (Intelligence): Knowledge, magic, spells prepared
- **WIL** (Willpower): Mental resilience, WP, spellcasting
- **CHA** (Charisma): Social interaction, leadership

|-----|-------------|

### Movement
Base movement from Kin, modified by AGL (see Kin section).

### Encumbrance
Maximum items carried = half STR, rounded up. Items over limit impose a bane on all physical rolls.

## Memento
A tiny keepsake item of personal significance. Once per session, during a stretch rest, you may draw on the memento to recover one additional condition.

---

## Weakness (Optional)
Roll D20 on the Weakness table for a personal flaw. Weaknesses are voluntary and provide additional roleplaying depth.

---

## Experience & Advancement

**Earning Advancement Marks:**
- Each time you roll a Dragon (1) on a trained skill during play, mark that skill.
- At the end of a session, for each marked skill, roll D20. If the result is **higher** than your current skill level, the skill increases by 1.

**Advancing Other Stats:**
- Attributes and secondary skills can be advanced through training during downtime (GM discretion).

# Skills

## Boons and Banes

**Boon:** Roll 2D20 and take the **lower** result.
**Bane:** Roll 2D20 and take the **higher** result.
Multiple boons or banes each add another D20 to the pool. Boons and banes cancel each other out one-for-one before rolling.

---

## Pushing a Roll

If you fail a skill roll, you may **push** it:
- Reroll all dice from the failed roll.
- The new result stands, even if worse.
- After pushing, suffer one **condition** (your choice; cannot choose a condition you already have; must narrate how it applies).
- **Cannot push** a Demon (20) roll.
- **Cannot push** if you already have all 6 conditions.

---

## Opposed Rolls

Both parties roll. The one with the **lower successful result** wins. If both succeed, lower result wins. If both fail, neither succeeds (or GM decides outcome). If one succeeds and the other fails, the successful party wins.

---

## Secondary Skills

Some skills are listed as "secondary." You can use them untrained at their base chance (without the ×2 trained bonus). All skills listed under your profession that you didn't pick as trained start as secondary skills at base chance.

# Combat

## Rounds & Initiative

- Combat is divided into **rounds** (10 seconds each).
- At the start of combat, each participant draws an **initiative card** (numbered 1–10). Lower numbers act first.
- On your turn, you take **one action** and **one fast action** (or two fast actions instead of one action).
- **Surprise:** If you surprise enemies, choose any initiative card value.

---

## Actions

**Actions (cost your action for the round):**
- Attack (melee or ranged)
- Cast a spell
- Use a skill
- Retreat (move away without provoking free attack if successful EVADE)
- Grapple
- Help an ally (+boon to their next roll)

**Fast Actions:**
- Draw or sheathe weapon
- Open a door
- Pick up item
- Stand up from prone
- Drink a potion
- Reload a crossbow
- Move (move up to your movement value in meters)

**Reactions (outside your turn):**
- **Parry:** Roll weapon skill to reduce damage (see Parrying)
- **Dodge (EVADE):** Roll EVADE to avoid hit entirely

---

## Movement

- Move up to your **movement value** (in meters) as a fast action.
- **Dash:** Use your action to move an additional full movement value.
- **Leap:** Jump over obstacles up to 2m wide (horizontal) or 1m high; requires ACROBATICS roll if contested.
- **Doors:** Opening a door or hatch costs a fast action.
- **Moving past enemies:** Moving through an enemy's space triggers a **free attack** against you unless you succeed on an EVADE roll.
- **Prone:** Melee attacks against prone targets get a boon; ranged attacks against prone targets get a bane.

---

## Sneak Attacks

If you attack a target that is unaware of you, you gain a **boon** on the attack roll. On a hit, the target cannot parry or dodge.

---

## Terrain & Visibility Modifiers

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Cramped space | Bane on attacks with two-handed weapons or long weapons |
| Rough ground | Bane on AGL-based rolls while moving |
| Dim light | Bane on attacks and AWARENESS rolls |
| Darkness | Cannot make ranged attacks; bane on all rolls requiring sight |

## Melee Combat

### Attacking
1. Roll your weapon skill (D20 ≤ skill level = hit).
2. If hit, roll weapon damage + damage bonus.
3. Target subtracts Armor Rating (AR). Remaining damage reduces HP.

### Damage Bonus
Add your damage bonus to melee weapon damage (see Derived Values). Not applied to ranged attacks.

### STR Requirement
Some weapons have a minimum STR requirement. Using a weapon with STR below the requirement imposes a bane on all attack rolls with it.

### Grip
- **One-handed:** Can use a shield in the other hand.
- **Two-handed:** Both hands required; cannot use a shield.

### Long Weapons
Weapons tagged as Long can attack before shorter weapons when both sides attack simultaneously. A fighter with a short weapon fighting someone with a Long weapon suffers a bane to attack.

### Critical Hits
A Dragon (1) on a melee attack roll is a critical hit. Roll on the **Critical Hit Table (Melee)**:

| D6 | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Double damage |
| 2 | Opponent drops weapon |
| 3 | Opponent knocked prone |
| 4 | Ignore armor (damage bypasses AR) |
| 5 | Opponent suffers a condition of your choice |
| 6 | Severed limb / lethal wound (Severe Injury) |

### Demon Rolls (Melee)
A Demon (20) on an attack roll is a fumble. Roll on the **Fumble Table (Melee)**:

| D6 | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Drop your weapon |
| 2 | Fall prone |
| 3 | Hit an ally (if any adjacent) |
| 4 | Weapon breaks (reduce Durability by 1) |
| 5 | Lose your next action |
| 6 | Suffer D6 damage |

### Parrying
**Reaction:** Roll your equipped weapon skill (or Shields skill if using a shield). If you succeed, you negate the damage. On a Dragon (1) when parrying, you may make a free counterattack. On a Demon (20) when parrying, your weapon or shield takes a Durability hit.

You may only parry **once per round** as a reaction, unless a heroic ability says otherwise.

### Dodging
**Reaction:** Roll EVADE. If you succeed, the attack misses entirely. On a Demon (20) on EVADE, you fall prone.

You may parry **or** dodge — not both — against a single attack, unless a heroic ability allows otherwise.

---

## Special Attacks

### Find Weak Spot
Spend a fast action. Make an AWARENESS roll. On success, your next attack this round ignores the target's AR.

### Topple
Instead of normal damage, make an opposed STR roll vs. target's STR. On success, target falls **prone** and loses their next fast action to stand.

### Disarm
Make an opposed weapon skill roll vs. target's weapon skill. On success, target drops their weapon (lands D3 meters away in a random direction).

### Grapple
Make an opposed STR roll vs. target's STR or AGL (target's choice). On success, target is **grappled**:
- Both you and the target are grappled (neither can move or attack normally).
- Each round, make opposed STR roll to maintain (or escape if target wins).
- While grappling, you can only use knives or similar small weapons.
- A grappled target can attempt to break free as an action (opposed STR).

### Shove
Make an opposed STR roll. On success, push target back D3 meters. If they hit a wall or obstacle, they take D3 damage and fall prone.

---

## Ranged Combat

### Range
Each ranged weapon has a listed range. Targets beyond that range but within double range suffer a bane. Beyond double range: cannot target.

### Obscured Targets
If the target is partially behind cover, the attacker suffers a bane.

### Critical Hits (Ranged)
Dragon (1) on a ranged attack:

| D6 | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Double damage |
| 2 | Opponent drops weapon |
| 3 | Opponent knocked prone |
| 4 | Ignore armor |
| 5 | Opponent suffers a condition of your choice |
| 6 | Pinned (opponent cannot move until condition removed) |

### Fumble (Ranged)
Demon (20) on a ranged attack:

| D6 | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Weapon jams / breaks string (needs fast action to fix) |
| 2 | Hit a nearby ally (if any in line of fire) |
| 3 | Ammunition lost / wasted D3 shots |
| 4 | Drop the weapon |
| 5 | Lose next action |
| 6 | Weapon breaks (Durability −1) |

## Death & Dying

- When HP reaches **0**, you are **Broken** (helpless but not dead).
- While Broken, you must make a **Death Roll** (D20 roll) at the start of each round:
  - 1–10: Stable (no change, roll again next round)
  - 11–19: Closer to death (roll again next round; third consecutive failure = dead)
  - 20: Immediate death
- **Rally:** An ally can spend an action to make a HEALING roll. On success, you regain 1 HP and are no longer Broken.
- **Saving a Life:** If no HEALING roll is made within 3 rounds of reaching 0 HP, the character dies.

### Instant Death
Damage that equals or exceeds your **maximum HP** in a single hit = instant death (no death rolls).

### Severe Injuries
When rolling a critical hit result of "Severed limb / lethal wound," roll D20 on the Severe Injury table:

| D20 | Injury |
|-----|--------|
| 1–2 | Broken hand (weapon falls, bane to all STR/AGL rolls) |
| 3–4 | Broken arm (cannot use arm; lose weapon if held) |
| 5–6 | Broken leg (movement halved, cannot run) |
| 7–8 | Damaged eye (bane to all sight-based rolls) |
| 9–10 | Ruptured ear (bane to hearing-based AWARENESS) |
| 11–12 | Deep wound to torso (lose D6 HP each round until treated) |
| 13–14 | Crushed ribs (bane to all STR/CON rolls) |
| 15–16 | Knocked out (unconscious until rally; D6 rounds) |
| 17–18 | Lost finger(s) (minor but permanent) |
| 19 | Disemboweled (will die in D6 rounds unless HEALING roll, difficulty bane) |
| 20 | Decapitated — dead instantly |

## Conditions

Each condition is linked to an attribute. A conditioned character suffers a **bane** on all rolls using the linked attribute.

| Condition | Attribute | Cure |
|-----------|-----------|------|
| Exhausted | AGL | Rest |
| Sickly | CON | Rest or HEALING |
| Dazed | INT | Rest |
| Angry | CHA | Rest |
| Scared | WIL | Rest |
| Grievous Wound | STR | Rest or HEALING |

**Gaining Conditions:** Through combat damage (specific injuries), push rolls, hazards, spells, and monster abilities.

A character with **all 6 conditions** cannot push rolls.

---

## Healing & Rest

### Round Rest
A brief pause in combat (action, no threats). Recover **D6 WP**.

### Stretch Rest
~15 minutes of recuperation. Recover:
- **D6 HP**
- **D6 WP**
- Remove **1 condition**
- Memento: remove 1 additional condition (once/session)
- Elf Inner Peace: D6 HP + D6 WP + 1 additional condition removed

### Shift Rest
~6 hours of sleep in reasonable safety. Recover:
- **All HP**
- **All WP**
- **All conditions**

You need to complete **2 shift rests** per day (roughly 8 hours of sleep total) to avoid **Exhausted**.

---

## Hazards

### Darkness
- Cannot make ranged attacks.
- Bane on all rolls requiring sight.
- Enemies in darkness: attacker acts as if blind (bane); if target is hidden in darkness = cannot target.

### Fear
When you encounter something terrifying, make a **WIL roll**. On failure, roll D8 on the Fear table:

| D8 | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Paralyzed with fear (lose all actions this round) |
| 2 | Flee (must move away from source at full speed) |
| 3 | Scream (alert all nearby creatures) |
| 4 | Drop held items |
| 5 | Frozen (cannot act until snapped out by ally action) |
| 6 | Panic attack (bane on all rolls for D6 rounds) |
| 7 | Become Scared (condition) |
| 8 | Faint (unconscious for D6 rounds) |

### Poison

**Lethal Poison:** Virulence rating (typically 6–15). Victim makes CON roll each round vs. virulence. On failure, suffer D6 damage. Three failures = death.

**Paralyzing Poison:** CON roll vs. virulence. On failure, paralyzed for D6 stretches.

**Sleeping Poison:** CON roll vs. virulence. On failure, fall asleep for D6 shifts.

### Disease
Each disease has a **Virulence** rating. At the end of each shift, make a CON roll vs. Virulence. On failure, gain a condition. When all 6 conditions are filled = death. Recovery: three consecutive successful CON rolls.

### Falling
- Up to 3m: D6 damage, no AR applies.
- 4–6m: 2D6 damage.
- 7–10m: 4D6 damage.
- 11m+: 8D6 damage (likely instant death).

### Swimming & Drowning
Non-swimmers need to roll SWIMMING each round in deep water. On failure, they begin to drown. Drowning characters lose D6 HP per round until rescued or out of water.

### Hunger
After 24 hours without food: gain **Exhausted**. Each additional 24 hours without food: gain another condition.

### Cold
After a shift exposed to severe cold without proper gear: gain **Sickly**. Each subsequent shift: gain another condition.

### Sleep Deprivation
After missing one shift rest: gain **Dazed**. Each additional shift without rest: gain another condition.

---

## Riding

- Mounts move using the rider's movement action.
- Normal riding requires no roll.
- In combat or difficult terrain, roll **RIDING**. On failure, the mount behaves erratically (GM decides).
- Bane on attack rolls while mounted if you lack the RIDING skill as a trained skill.
- Boon on attacks against unmounted targets while mounted.
- A mount reduced to 0 HP throws its rider (ACROBATICS roll or fall prone and take D6 damage).

---

## Improvised Weapons
Use the weapon stats for the closest equivalent. Typically deal D6 damage, no damage bonus, Durability 1.

# Magic

## Magic Schools

| School | Skill | Focus |
|--------|-------|-------|
| General Magic | General Magic | Universal spells, utility |
| Animism | Animism | Nature, healing, life |
| Elementalism | Elementalism | Elements (fire, water, earth, air) |
| Mentalism | Mentalism | Mind, body, space |

Mages must choose which schools to learn. Each school is a separate skill.

---

## Prepared Spells

- Number of spells you can have **prepared** = your INT base chance (not trained skill).
- Spells must be prepared before they can be cast (during a stretch rest).
- **Magic Tricks** (a special category within each school) are always prepared and never count against this limit.
- Spells in your **grimoire** can be prepared each day as part of rest.

---

## Grimoire

A physical book containing spells. A spell must be in your grimoire before you can prepare it. You can carry spells you haven't learned as well — you just can't cast them.

---

## Magic Tricks

Always prepared. Cost 1 WP. Automatically succeed (no roll required). A **gesture** (visible hand movement or equivalent) is always required to cast a trick.

---

## Casting Spells

1. Declare the spell and its **Power Level** (1–3 where applicable).
2. Pay WP: **2 WP per Power Level**. (PL1 = 2 WP, PL2 = 4 WP, PL3 = 6 WP)
3. Roll the relevant magic school skill.
4. Success = spell works as described. Failure = spell doesn't work but WP is still spent.

### Power Levels
Many spells have scaling effects at PL1, PL2, PL3. Higher PL = stronger effect.

### Magic and Metal
Wearing **metal armor** while casting imposes a **bane** on the magic school roll.

---

## Casting Requirements

Some spells require one or more of:
- **Word** — Must speak an incantation. Cannot cast silently.
- **Gesture** — Must make visible hand movements. Cannot cast with hands bound.
- **Focus** — Must hold a focus item (wand, staff, orb, etc.)
- **Ingredient** — Must have and consume a specific component.

---

## Casting Time

Most spells are cast as a single action. Spells with longer casting times are noted in the spell description.

---

## Range

- **Touch** — Must be in contact with the target.
- **Reach** — Within a few meters (arm's length or short distance).
- **Short** — Up to ~10m.
- **Long** — Up to ~30m.
- **Sphere (Xm)** — Affects everything within X meters of caster.
- **Cone** — Affects everything in a cone-shaped area in front of caster.

---

## Dragon & Demon on Spells

- **Dragon (1):** Spell works with maximum effect (as if highest power level, or double effect).
- **Demon (20):** Spell fails and triggers a **Magical Mishap**.

### Magical Mishap Table (D20)

| D20 | Mishap |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Caster takes D6 damage |
| 2 | Lose D6 WP |
| 3 | Spell affects caster instead of target |
| 4 | Spell affects a random nearby creature |
| 5 | Caster becomes Dazed |
| 6 | Violent magical discharge — all within 2m take D6 damage |
| 7 | Caster loses their next action |
| 8 | Caster is flung back D6 meters |
| 9 | Spell triggers but at maximum PL regardless of WP spent |
| 10 | Wild surge: random nearby spell effect (GM decides) |
| 11 | Caster's voice silenced for D6 rounds (cannot cast spells requiring Word) |
| 12 | Hands numbed (bane on all spell rolls for D6 rounds) |
| 13 | Portal opens briefly — something comes through |
| 14 | Caster ages D6 years |
| 15 | Reverse effect (spell causes opposite of intended) |
| 16 | Caster becomes Scared |
| 17 | Gravity around caster reverses for 1 round |
| 18 | Caster's focus/grimoire is destroyed |
| 19 | All spells prepared are unprepared; must re-prepare |
| 20 | Catastrophic — double all damage or effects of the mishap |

---

## Learning Spells

**From a teacher:**
- Spend 1 shift training.
- Roll INT with a boon.
- On success, add the spell to your grimoire; mark the school for advancement.

**From a grimoire:**
- Spend 1 shift studying.
- Roll LANGUAGES (no boon).
- On success, add spell to your own grimoire; no advancement mark.

## General Magic Spells

### Tricks

**Flame** — Conjure a small flame in your palm. Lasts 1 stretch. Cannot be used as a weapon.

**Detect Magic** — Sense whether an object or area is magical (yes/no). Range: Touch.

**Telepathic Message** — Send a brief mental message (one sentence) to a creature you can see.

**Ward** — Draw a magical symbol that glows when an invisible or hidden creature passes through it. Lasts 1 shift.

**Magic Hand** — Move an object up to 1 kg within short range. Precise manipulation possible.

**Light** — Create a soft, palm-sized light that illuminates a 10m radius. Lasts 1 stretch.

### Spells

**Dispel (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Gesture
- PL1: End one magical effect on a creature or object (your touch).
- PL2: End all magical effects on a creature or object (your touch).
- PL3: End all magical effects in a 5m sphere around you.

**Protector (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Word, Gesture
- PL1: One willing creature gains +1 AR for 1 stretch.
- PL2: One willing creature gains +2 AR for 1 stretch.
- PL3: Up to 3 willing creatures gain +2 AR for 1 stretch.

**Magic Shield (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Gesture
- Creates a shimmering barrier. Target can use your General Magic skill to parry once (PL1), twice (PL2), or three times (PL3) this stretch. Works like a Shield skill parry.

**Transfer (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Touch, Word
- PL1: Move up to 3 WP from yourself to a willing creature (or vice versa).
- PL2: Move up to 6 WP.
- PL3: Move up to 10 WP.

**Magic Seal (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Ingredient (wax or similar), Gesture
- PL1: Seal a door, chest, or container. Requires STR roll (with bane at PL2, two banes at PL3) to open by force.
- PL2: Alarm triggers (loud noise) if seal is broken.
- PL3: Breaking seal causes 2D6 damage to whoever breaks it.

**Charge (PL1–3)**
- Requirements: Focus, Touch
- Infuse a weapon with magical energy. Next attack with the weapon deals +D6 (PL1), +2D6 (PL2), or +3D6 (PL3) extra damage. Effect lasts until used or end of stretch.

**Permanence**
- Requirements: Word, Gesture, Ingredient (rare), 1 shift casting time
- Make a currently active spell permanent. The WP cost of the original spell is locked (unavailable) until the spell is dispelled. Can only maintain one Permanence spell at a time.

# Bestiary

## Monster Rules

### Ferocity
A rating (1–4) representing how many **Monster Attacks** a monster can perform per round.

### Size
| Size | Example | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Small | Cat, goblin | — |
| Normal | Human, orc | — |
| Large | Troll, minotaur | Takes up ~2×2m space |
| Huge | Giant, dragon | Takes up ~4×4m space |
| Swarm | Bats, rats | Multiple creatures acting as one |

### Movement
Monster movement values are per round (like player characters).

### Monster Attacks
Monsters do **not** roll to attack. Instead, a player rolls EVADE or a weapon skill (to parry) against the attack. If the player fails, the attack hits.

**Defending against monster attacks:**
- Roll your **EVADE** to dodge, OR
- Roll your **weapon skill** to parry.
- Base difficulty for these rolls is **15** (i.e., you need to roll ≤ your skill level; if the monster has a special modifier, the difficulty changes).
- A Dragon (1) on your defense = critical defense (no damage; you may make a free attack).
- A Demon (20) on your defense = you are struck with maximum damage.

### Resistance & Immunity
- **Resistance:** Halve damage from specified damage type (rounded down).
- **Immunity:** Take 0 damage from specified type.

### Fear & Persuasion
- Some monsters trigger fear; players must make WIL rolls on first encounter.
- **Persuadable** monsters can be spoken to and convinced not to attack (PERSUASION roll, often with bane).

# Journeys & Adventures

## Travel

- **On foot:** ~15 km per shift
- **Horseback:** ~30 km per shift
- **Maximum:** 2 shifts of travel per day
- **Forced March:** Travel a 3rd shift — gain **Exhausted** at the end.

---

## Pathfinding

Each shift of travel, a designated **Pathfinder** rolls **BUSHCRAFT**:
- **No map:** Bane on the roll.
- **Spyglass:** Boon on the roll.
- **Failure:** The party takes a wrong turn; GM adds D6 km to travel distance or introduces a complication.
- **Dragon (1):** Find a shortcut; reduce distance by D6 km.

### Mishap Table (D12 — on failed BUSHCRAFT)

| D12 | Mishap |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Lost — travel D6 extra km in the wrong direction |
| 2 | Ambush — encounter hostile creatures |
| 3 | Treacherous terrain — risk of injury (AGL roll or take D6 damage) |
| 4 | Separated — one random party member gets lost for a stretch |
| 5 | Equipment lost — one random item dropped/broken |
| 6 | Weather turns — storm begins; bane to travel rolls next shift |
| 7 | Dead end — cliff, swamp, or river blocks path; lose 1 shift |
| 8 | Exhausting route — each party member gains Exhausted |
| 9 | Wrong destination — arrive at different (possibly interesting) location |
| 10 | Natural hazard — rockfall, flash flood, etc. (CON roll or D6 damage) |
| 11 | Insect swarm — bane to all rolls for remainder of shift |
| 12 | No mishap — safe arrival despite poor roll |

---

## Making Camp

Roll **BUSHCRAFT** to establish a safe camp:
- **No sleeping fur:** Bane on the roll.
- **Tent:** Boon on the roll.
- **Failure:** Camp is uncomfortable or poorly placed; shift rest only restores HP and WP (not conditions).
- **Dangerous location** (dungeon, active battle area): Cannot make camp.

| 6 | Nothing found |

### Fishing
- **Rod:** D4 rations per stretch spent fishing.
- **Net:** D6 rations per stretch (net deployed; check each shift).

### Foraging
Roll **BUSHCRAFT** each stretch spent foraging. On success: D3 rations. On Dragon (1): D6 rations.

### Cooking
- Raw fish or meat: risk of disease (virulence 10) if eaten.
- Cooking: spend 1 stretch + roll BUSHCRAFT. On success: food is safe and edible.
