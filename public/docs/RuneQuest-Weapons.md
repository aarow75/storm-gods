# RuneQuest Weapons & Armor

Costs are in Lunars (L). **SR** is the weapon's strike rank contribution — total strike rank = SIZ modifier + DEX modifier + weapon SR. **Min STR/DEX** are the minimum characteristics to use the weapon effectively.

**Weapon rules worth remembering:**

- **Similar weapons**: knowing a weapon of one type and mode (e.g. one-handed cutting) lets you use any similar weapon at ½ effectiveness.
- **Weapon damage**: weapons absorb damage cumulatively when they parry a successful attack (or attack into a successful parry). When damage taken exceeds the weapon's hit points, it breaks. Short stabbing weapons and long-hafted weapons don't damage other weapons when parrying.
- **Two-weapon use**: a weapon in each hand gives 2 attacks, 2 parries, or 1 of each. Off-hand use starts at 05% and requires DEX 1.5× the weapon's minimum. The second attack comes at the first attack's strike rank plus the second weapon's SR — if the total exceeds 12, both can't attack in one round.
- **Impaling weapons** (spears, arrows, daggers) do double damage on a roll of 20% of the attack chance; **critical hits** ignore armor.

Missile weapons impale. Arrows and bolts do not add the firer's damage bonus; thrown weapons (javelin) add half the damage bonus. Firing at a moving target or while moving reduces the chance to hit; a large shield can be planted as cover against missiles.

## Unarmed

| Attack | Damage | SR | Notes |
|--------|--------|----|-------|
| Fist | 1d3 | 3 | Ironhand spirit magic improves unarmed strikes |
| Kick | 1d6 | 3 | — |
| Grapple | Special | 3 | See below |

**Grappling**: a successful grapple attack catches the hit location rolled. On following rounds the grappler can attempt to immobilize the limb (STR vs. STR on the resistance table) or throw the foe (STR+DEX vs. SIZ+DEX). A thrown character must roll DEX×5 or take 1D6 damage in a random location (armor protects).

## Shields

A shield must successfully parry to interpose itself; it then absorbs damage before armor and body. Damage beyond what the shield absorbs strikes the rolled hit location.

| Shield | Parry Bonus | HP | ENC | Cost (L) | Description |
|--------|-------------|----|-----|----------|-------------|
| Small Shield | +15% | 9 | 1 | 15 | Buckler, 30–35 cm, held by one handgrip |
| Medium Shield | +20% | 12 | 2 | 20 | ~60 cm round shield or knight's heater |
| Large Shield | +25% | 15 | 2 | 25 | Foot shield: Greek hoplon, Norman kite, Viking round |

Unlike weapons, shields and armor do not break from parrying — a shield's points show how much it absorbs per blow before the remainder gets through.

## Armor

Armor is the last barrier between you and the incoming blade: it absorbs its point value from every hit to a protected location. Heavier armor is noisier (penalties to Move Quietly) and more encumbering.

| Armor | Points | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| None | 0 | Bare skin and bravado |
| Leather | 1 | Soft or shoe-leather thickness; cheap and quiet; can be worn as padding under metal |
| Ring Mail | 2 | Metal rings butted together and sewn to a leather backing |
| Scale Mail | 3 | Small overlapping metal plates sewn onto leather — excellent if heavy protection |
| Chain Mail | 4 | Metal links woven into a mesh and formed into garments |
| Bronze Plate | 4 | Molded bronze plates in the ancient style — as good as chain, and prouder |
| Plate Mail | 5 | Large metal plates fitted to the wearer; the best (and heaviest, and loudest) protection money can buy |

In the full RuneQuest Classic rules armor is bought by the piece per hit location (greaves, vambraces, hauberks, cuirasses, helms); the armor types above represent complete suits. Cuirboilli (wax-boiled leather, 3 points) and linen (quilted waxed cloth, 3 points) fall between ring and scale.
