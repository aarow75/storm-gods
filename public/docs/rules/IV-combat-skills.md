# IV. COMBAT SKILLS

## Overview

Combat skills encompass weapon use, defense techniques, and special combat situations. This section details the mechanics for fighting with various weapons, defending against attacks, and the rules for critical successes, special successes, and fumbles.

---

## 1. Notes on Combat

### The Combat Loop

1. Declare intent (what you're trying to do)
2. Calculate strike rank (including movement penalties)
3. Roll D100 vs. relevant skill
4. Determine success level (critical, special, normal, failure, fumble)
5. Apply damage or effect
6. Resolve opponent's defense
7. Record damage to appropriate hit location

### Basic Attack Resolution

To hit a target:
- **Roll D100 vs. weapon skill rating**
- Success if roll ≤ skill
- Failure if roll > skill (96–00 always fails regardless of skill)

### Advantage Factors

| Condition | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Attacking prone/unaware opponent | +40% to hit |
| Attacking target in shadows/darkness | –75% to hit |
| Defending while prone | Unmodified parry/dodge (normal chance) |
| Attacking from prone position | –50% to hit |
| Aimed blow at specific hit location | Halve skill, then apply other modifiers |
| Multiple actions in one round | Cumulative –20% for each additional action |

## 2. Special Damages

### Success Levels in Combat

| Success Level | Roll Range | Combat Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical Success** | ≤ 1/20 of skill | Double maximum damage; ignore armor |
| **Special Success** | ≤ 1/5 of skill | Double damage (but armor applies normally) |
| **Normal Success** | ≤ skill rating | Roll normal damage |
| **Failure** | > skill rating | Attack misses; no damage |
| **Fumble** | 96–00 (or 1/20 of failures) | Worst outcome; weapon dropped, disarmed, or other complication |

### Damage Application

**Normal Damage**: Roll weapon damage die + bonuses, subtract armor, apply to hit location.

**Special Success Damage**: 
- Double the rolled damage
- Armor still applies
- Example: Broadsword (1D8+1) on special success = 2 × (1D8+1) = 2D8+2

**Critical Success Damage**: 
- Double the **maximum damage** (not rolled—use max values)
- **Armor is ignored entirely**
- Example: Broadsword (1D8+1) on critical = 2 × 9 = 18 damage, ignoring all armor

### Knockback and Dismounting

When damage exceeds a location's hit point total:
- **Special Success**: Target is knocked down
- **Critical Success**: Target is knocked down AND disarmed (weapon drops)

## 3. Optional Rules — Parries, Critical, and Fumbled

### Parrying

A defender can attempt to parry an incoming attack:
- **Roll D100 vs. parry skill** (weapon or shield parry)
- Success negates the attack entirely
- **First parry per round**: Normal chance
- **Second parry per round**: –20%
- **Third parry per round**: –40%
- **Fourth+ parry per round**: –60% (cumulative)

**Note**: Parrying still costs strike ranks and must be performed within the round's 12 SR slots.

### Dodge

A character can dodge instead of parry:
- **Roll D100 vs. Dodge skill**
- Success moves the character out of harm's way
- **First dodge per round**: Normal chance (unmodified, even if prone)
- **Subsequent dodges**: Apply cumulative –20% penalty per additional dodge

**Dodge Limitations**: 
- Cannot dodge while held/grappled
- Cannot dodge ranged attacks from behind without warning

### Fumbles and Disarming

**Fumble Table (Triggered on 96–00 roll or at GM discretion)**:

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Weapon dropped | Must spend 1 SR picking it up (or 1 full round if not in combat) |
| Weapon disarmed | Opponent may catch it or it lands nearby |
| Off-balance | –20% to next action this round |
| Stumble | Must move 1D3 meters away or fall prone |
| Fall prone | Character knocked down; must stand (uses 2 SR or 1 round out of combat) |

A fumble represents a significant mistake and always results in negative consequences.

## 4. Learning Fighting Skills

### Skill Advancement

After an adventure, a skill with a (☐) checkbox can be improved:

1. **Check if skill was used** in the adventure
2. **Roll D100** vs. current skill rating
3. **If roll is above current rating**: Increase skill by 1D6%
4. **If roll is below or equal**: No improvement this period

Example: Sword skill is 35%. Roll 54 (above 35) → Increases by 1D6% (e.g., to 38%).

### Skill Caps

- **Without training**: Maximum 95% (through experience advancement)
- **With trainer**: Can exceed 100% (trainer must have skill 75%+ and teach for one week)
- **Effective maximum**: 95% (even if skill is 150%, chance to succeed never exceeds 95%)

## 5. Weapon Use and Training

### Weapon Requirements

Each weapon has minimum STR and DEX thresholds:

| Weapon Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **minSTR** | Minimum Strength to use effectively |
| **minDEX** | Minimum Dexterity to use effectively |

**If both stats are below minimums**:
- All attacks and parries with that weapon are at **half skill**

**If only one stat is below minimum**:
- **Excess STR compensates for low DEX on a 2:1 basis**
- Example: Greatsword needs 11 STR / 13 DEX. You have STR 13, DEX 9 (4 short). Since you have 2 extra STR points, you only suffer partial penalty.

### Default Weapon Skills

Each weapon has a **base skill percentage** (before modifiers):

| Weapon Type | Base % |
|---|---|
| Axe | 20% |
| Sword | 25% |
| Spear/Pike | 20% |
| Mace/Hammer | 20% |
| Bow | 15% |
| Crossbow | 15% |
| Sling | 10% |
| Dagger | 20% |
| Unarmed | 30% |

**Modifiers** from Manipulation category bonuses are added to base.

### Weapon Specialization

An adventurer can specialize in a single weapon type by:
- Spending significant training time (weeks/months)
- Rolling to improve through experience
- Gaining +10% to +20% bonus to that specific weapon

## 6. Missile Weapons

### Range Categories

Missile weapons have three effective ranges:

| Range Type | Hit Chance | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Short range** | Normal skill | 40m for a longbow |
| **Medium range** | 50% of normal skill | 100m for a longbow |
| **Long range** | 25% of normal skill | 200m for a longbow |

**Beyond long range**: Practically impossible without magical aid.

### Rate of Fire

| Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S/MR** | Multiple shots per melee round (limited by 5 SR for reload) |
| **1/MR** | One shot per melee round |
| **1/2R, 1/3R, 1/5R** | One shot every N rounds |

Example: Shortbow with "S/MR" means you can shoot multiple times in one round if you have sufficient strike ranks.

### Reload Time

**Typical reload costs**:
- **Bow**: 5 SR per shot (can fire multiple times in a round if SR permits)
- **Crossbow**: Longer reload (varies by type; 10-20 SR typical)
- **Sling**: 5 SR per shot

## 7. Shields and Helmets

### Shield Function

Shields provide:
1. **Armor protection** (reduces damage)
2. **Parry ability** (used to block attacks)
3. **Coverage** (protects specific hit locations)

### Shield Types and Statistics

| Shield Type | Armor Points | Hit Points | Encumbrance | Locations Protected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Small (Buckler)** | 3–4 | 8–10 | 1 | One arm |
| **Target Shield** | 6 | 9 | 1 | One arm + chest |
| **Heater Shield** | 10 | 12 | 2 | Both arms + chest |
| **Kite Shield** | 12 | 14 | 3 | Both arms + chest + abdomen |
| **Tower Shield** | 14 | 16 | 4 | Both arms + chest + abdomen + head |

**Shield Skill**: When parrying with a shield, use Shield skill (default 25%, modified by Manipulation category bonus).

### Helmets

Helmets protect the head location only:

| Helmet Type | Armor Points | Cost (Lunars) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leather hood** | 1 | 3 | Minimal protection |
| **Open helm** | 2 | 10 | Allows face visibility |
| **Closed helm** | 4 | 50 | Full head protection; –10% to vision-based skills |
| **Full plate helm** | 6 | 50 | Maximum protection |

**Note**: Helmets with face protection (closed, plate) apply –10% to Search, Scan, and other vision-based skills.

## 8. Armor

### Armor Types and Protection

Armor is worn on specific hit locations and reduces damage by its armor value.

**Armor Materials** (by protection level):

| Type | Protection | Encumbrance | Quietness Penalty | Cost (Lunars) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leather** | 1 | 0 | None | 10 |
| **Cuirboilli (hardened leather)** | 2 | 1 | –5% | 20 |
| **Linen** | 2 | 1 | None | 15 |
| **Ring Mail** | 3 | 2 | –10% | 50 |
| **Scale Mail** | 4 | 2 | –10% | 100 |
| **Chain Mail** | 5 | 3 | –15% | 300 |
| **Bronze Plate** | 6 | 3 | –15% | 300+ |

### Coverage and Location Protection

Armor can be worn on:
- **Head** (helmet)
- **Chest** (cuirass, hauberk, breastplate)
- **Abdomen** (waist guard, lower plate)
- **Arms** (sleeves, vambraces, full arm protection)
- **Legs** (greaves, leg armor, full leg protection)

**Each location is protected independently**. A character wearing chain mail on the chest only protects the chest hit location; legs and arms receive no protection unless also armored.

### Armor Damage

When armor absorbs damage in combat, it may be damaged:
- **Minor damage**: 1–3 points → Armor still functions
- **Severe damage**: 4+ points accumulated → Armor develops cracks/dents
- **Armor destroyed**: When cumulative damage equals its protection value × 2

**Repair**: A character with Craft skill can repair armor during downtime (costs materials and time; varies by damage severity).

### Special Cases

**Critical Hits Ignore Armor**: A critical success (roll ≤ 1/20 of skill) ignores all armor—damage is applied fully to the hit location.

**Magical Armor**: "Protection" and "Shield" spells add temporary armor points (typically +2 to +4) that stack with worn armor.

## 9. Weapon Statistics

### Reading Weapon Entries

Each weapon in this game has:
- **Name** — Weapon identifier
- **Damage** — Dice notation (e.g., 1D8+1)
- **Skill** — Associated skill category (Sword & Shield, Spear, etc.)
- **Strike Rank** — Number to add to character's base SR
- **Encumbrance** — Weight/bulk in ENC points
- **Hit Points** — Weapon durability (damage from parries/breaks)
- **minSTR / minDEX** — Minimum characteristics required
- **Cost** — Price in Lunars
- **isMissile** — True if ranged weapon; False if melee
- **canParry** — True if weapon can parry; False otherwise

### Damage Type Modifiers

Weapons have damage types:
- **Slashing** (swords, axes) — Good for dismemberment; resisted by plate armor
- **Crushing** (maces, hammers) — Good against armor; less lethal
- **Impaling** (spears, arrows) — Good at penetrating armor; requires precision
- **Cutting Thrust** (rapier-type) — Balanced between slashing and impaling
- **Unarmed** (fists, kicks) — Low damage; versatile

Different armor types have varying effectiveness against each damage type (advanced rule; not detailed here).

---

## Summary

**Key Takeaways**:
- Weapon skills use D100 roll vs. percentage
- Critical (≤1/20) doubles max damage and ignores armor
- Special (≤1/5) doubles rolled damage (armor applies)
- First parry/dodge per round is unmodified; subsequent ones take cumulative –20% penalty
- Armor reduces damage by its protection value
- Shields provide both armor and parry ability
- Missile weapons have range modifiers (short/medium/long)
- Weapon requirements (minSTR/minDEX) must be met or suffer penalties
