# Dragonbane Rules Reference

> **Source**: Dragonbane Quickstart Guide (Free League Publishing, freely distributed).
> This document covers the core rules as presented in the official free quickstart. Verify details against the full rulebook where needed.

## Core Mechanic

Roll a **D20**. A result **equal to or lower than your skill level** means success.

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Dragon** — Critical success |
| ≤ skill | Success |
| > skill | Failure |
| 20 | **Demon** — Automatic failure (cannot push) |

**Opposed Rolls**: Both parties roll. The one who rolls lower (but still succeeds) wins. Ties go to the active party. If both fail, nothing happens.

## Attributes

Six base attributes, rated **3–18**.

| Attribute | Abbreviation | Description |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Strength | STR | Raw muscle power |
| Constitution | CON | Physical fitness and resilience |
| Agility | AGL | Body control, speed, fine motor skills |
| Intelligence | INT | Mental acuity and reasoning |
| Willpower | WIL | Self-discipline and focus |
| Charisma | CHA | Force of personality and empathy |

**Base value**: 10 (before kin and profession modifiers). Generated with 4D6 drop-lowest when rolling.

---

## Derived Ratings

| Rating | Calculation |
|--------|------------|
| Hit Points (HP) | Equal to CON (maximum) |
| Willpower Points (WP) | Equal to WIL (maximum) |
| Movement | Based on STR (varies by kin) |
| Damage Bonus (STR) | Based on STR score |
| Damage Bonus (AGL) | Based on AGL score |

### Damage Bonus Table

| Attribute Score | Damage Bonus |
|----------------|-------------|
| 3–6 | –D4 |
| 7–9 | –D2 |
| 10–12 | None |
| 13–15 | +D4 |
| 16–18 | +D6 |

*Each weapon skill is tied to either STR or AGL; use the appropriate damage bonus.*

## Character Creation

1. **Choose Kin** — Determines innate abilities and attribute modifiers
2. **Assign Attributes** — Base 10; distribute modifier points from kin/profession
3. **Choose Profession** — Determines trained skills and starting skill points
4. **Assign Skill Points** — Distribute points among profession skills
5. **Choose Weakness/Flaw** — Mechanical drawback that grants Inspiration opportunities
6. **Select Equipment** — Starting gear based on profession
7. **Calculate Derived Ratings** — HP, WP, Movement, Damage Bonuses
8. **Choose Heroic Ability** — Special power; spending WP activates it
9. **Assign Memento** — A tiny sentimental item (counts as a tiny item, no encumbrance)

## Skills

Skills are rated **1–18**. Roll D20 equal to or under your skill to succeed.

The **base skill value** equals the linked attribute ÷ 2 (rounded up) for untrained skills. Trained skills start higher based on profession.

### Skills by Attribute

| Attribute | Skills |
|-----------|--------|
| STR | Axes, Brawling, Crafting, Hammers, Spears, Swords |
| AGL | Acrobatics, Bows, Crossbows, Evade, Hunting & Fishing, Knives, Riding, Seamanship, Sleight of Hand, Sneaking, Slings, Staves, Swimming |
| INT | Awareness, Beast Lore, Bushcraft, Healing, Languages, Myths & Legends, Spot Hidden |
| CHA | Bartering, Bluffing, Performance, Persuasion |
| WIL | Fear resistance rolls |

### Magic Schools (separate skills)

| School | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Animism | Spirit magic, nature connection |
| Elementalism | Fire, wind, earth, water spells |
| Mentalism | Mind and illusion magic |

### Skill Descriptions

**Acrobatics (AGL)** — Tumbling, climbing, balancing, and physical feats.

**Awareness (INT)** — General alertness; hearing sounds, noticing details at a glance.

**Bartering (CHA)** — Buying/selling goods; haggling for better prices.

**Beast Lore (INT)** — Identifying creatures, their habits, and weaknesses.

**Bluffing (CHA)** — Deceiving or lying convincingly.

**Brawling (STR)** — Unarmed combat; punches, kicks, grapples.

**Bushcraft (INT)** — Survival, tracking, foraging, navigation in the wild.

**Crafting (STR)** — Making and repairing items.

**Evade (AGL)** — Dodging out of harm's way; reaction skill.

**Healing (INT)** — Treating wounds and illness; first aid and medicine.

**Hunting & Fishing (AGL)** — Catching prey and fish for food.

**Languages (INT)** — Speaking and understanding other languages (one roll per language).

**Myths & Legends (INT)** — Knowledge of history, folklore, ancient stories, and sacred lore.

**Performance (CHA)** — Singing, playing instruments, acting, storytelling.

**Persuasion (CHA)** — Convincing others through honest argument, appeals, or negotiation.

**Riding (AGL)** — Controlling mounts.

**Seamanship (AGL/INT)** — Sailing and navigating watercraft.

**Sleight of Hand (AGL)** — Picking pockets, palming objects, fine dexterity tricks.

**Sneaking (AGL)** — Moving silently and staying hidden.

**Spot Hidden (INT)** — Noticing concealed objects, secret doors, or hidden clues.

**Swimming (AGL)** — Moving through water.

**Weapon Skills (STR or AGL)**: Axes, Bows, Crossbows, Hammers, Knives, Slings, Spears, Staves, Swords — used to attack with the respective weapon type.

## Boons, Banes & Pushing

### Boons
Roll **two D20, take the lower result**. Gained from:
- Assistance from an ally (ally uses their action)
- Favorable conditions
- Heroic abilities

### Banes
Roll **two D20, take the higher result**. Caused by:
- Darkness
- Difficult terrain
- Conditions affecting the relevant attribute
- Helmets (on certain INT/AGL rolls)

Boons and banes cancel each other out. Multiple boons/banes don't stack beyond one each.

### Pushing a Roll
After failing a roll, you may **push it** — reroll, but you automatically gain one **condition** relevant to the action. You **cannot push a Demon (20)**.

## Combat

### Time

Combat is measured in **Rounds** (10 seconds each).

### Initiative

At the start of each round, all participants draw **initiative cards numbered 1–10**. Act in card-number order (lowest first). Monsters with high **Ferocity** draw multiple cards and act multiple times per round.

### Actions Per Turn

Each character gets **one action + movement** per round.

**Reactions** (parry, dodge) can be taken outside your turn but use your action for that round.

### Free Actions (one per type per round)
- Draw or sheathe weapon
- Drop an item
- Change stance or position
- Shout a message

### Standard Actions
- Move (walk up to Movement rate)
- Dash (double Movement; no other action)
- Melee attack
- Ranged attack
- Parry (reaction)
- Dodge (reaction)
- Pick up an item
- First aid on an ally
- Rally a frightened ally
- Cast a spell
- Use an item
- Help another character (grants boon)

### Melee Combat

- **Range**: Within 2 meters (4 meters for Long weapons)
- **Attack roll**: Roll ≤ weapon skill
- **Damage**: Weapon damage dice + damage bonus (STR or AGL) − armor rating
- **Dragon (critical)**: Choose — double damage dice, perform a second free attack, or disarm/trip opponent

### Ranged Combat

- **Short range**: Normal roll
- **Long range** (beyond ×2 weapon range): **Bane** on attack roll
- **Within 2 meters of target**: **Bane** on attack roll
- Parrying ranged attacks requires a **shield**; dodging is allowed
- **Dragon (critical)**: Double damage dice (not bonus), or bypass armor entirely

### Parrying

Use as a reaction when attacked. Roll ≤ weapon or shield skill. On success, the attack is blocked. On failure, you still take damage.

- Parrying with a **shield**: Successful parry blocks all damage
- Parrying with a **weapon**: May take damage based on difference

### Dodging

Use as a reaction using the **Evade** skill. On success, move up to 2 meters and take no damage.

### Hit Points & Damage

**Damage** = Weapon dice + Damage Bonus − Armor Rating (minimum 1 if attacker succeeds)

At **0 HP**: Character is **broken** and falls.

### Death & Dying

When at 0 HP, each round make a **CON death roll** (roll ≤ CON):

| Outcome | Result |
|---------|--------|
| 3 successes (before 3 failures) | Character stabilizes at D6 HP |
| Dragon on death roll | Counts as 2 successes |
| 3 failures (before 3 successes) | Character dies |
| Demon on death roll | Counts as 2 failures |

Allies can perform **first aid** (Healing skill roll) to give one success immediately.

## Conditions

Each condition imposes a **bane on all rolls against the linked attribute**.

| Condition | Linked Attribute | Example Causes |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Exhausted | STR | Over-exertion, heavy labor |
| Sickly | CON | Poison, disease, cold |
| Dazed | AGL | Head blow, stunning attacks |
| Angry | INT | Provocation, frustration |
| Scared | WIL | Fear attacks, horror |
| Disheartened | CHA | Failure, humiliation |

A character can have multiple conditions simultaneously (multiple banes stack). Conditions are healed by resting.

---

## Resting & Healing

| Rest Type | Duration | HP Recovered | WP Recovered | Conditions |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Round Rest | 1 round (once/shift) | None | D6 WP | None |
| Stretch Rest | 15 minutes (once/shift) | D6 HP (2D6 with healer) | D6 WP | Heal one condition |
| Shift Rest | 6 hours (safe location) | Full HP | Full WP | Heal all conditions |

**Healing with assistance**: Another character makes a **Healing skill roll** during stretch rest. On success, the injured character recovers 2D6 HP instead of D6.

## Encumbrance

**Carry limit** = half STR (rounded up) in **Inventory slots**.

| Item Type | Slots |
|-----------|-------|
| Regular item | 1 slot |
| Heavy item | 2–4 slots |
| Tiny item (coins <100, etc.) | 0 slots |
| Equipped weapon (in hand) | 0 slots |
| Worn armor/helmet | 0 slots |

**Over-encumbered**: Must make a STR roll to move in combat.

## Magic

### Overview

Magic is organized into three **schools**, each a separate skill:
- **Animism** — Spirit connection, nature spirits
- **Elementalism** — Control of fire, wind, earth, water
- **Mentalism** — Mind reading, illusions, mental influence

Each spell belongs to one school. Casting uses the appropriate school skill.

### Casting

- **Roll**: ≤ school skill to succeed
- **Cost**: 2 WP per power level (minimum 2 WP)
- **Magic Tricks**: 1 WP, automatic success — no roll needed
- **Failure**: Spend the WP; spell has no effect (cannot push unless noted)
- **Action**: Casting is a standard action unless stated as a reaction or ritual

### Requirements

Spells may require one or more of:
| Requirement | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Word | A spoken chant or incantation |
| Gesture | Hand/body movements |
| Focus | A held item (wand, crystal, etc.) |
| Ingredient | A consumed component |

### Power Levels

Spells can be cast at higher power levels for enhanced effects. Each additional power level costs 2 more WP.

### Dragon Roll (Critical Cast)

When casting succeeds with a Dragon (1), choose one:
- Double damage or range
- No WP cost for this cast
- Cast another spell immediately (with a bane on the second roll)

Target must roll a Dragon to resist a Dragon-cast spell.

### Demon Roll (Fumble Cast)

A Demon (20) on a spell roll is a critical failure. Cannot push this roll.

### Duration

| Duration | Length |
|----------|--------|
| Instant | Immediate effect only |
| Round | Until end of current round |
| Stretch | ~15 minutes |
| Shift | ~6 hours |
| Concentration | While caster maintains focus |

### Area of Effect

- **Sphere**: Affects everyone within a radius (usually excluding caster)
- **Cone**: Cone-shaped spread from caster

## Spell List

### Magic Tricks (1 WP, automatic success)

**Heat/Chill**
Area within 10m becomes comfortably warm or cold for one shift. Protects against cold/heat conditions.

**Ignite**
Light or extinguish any light source within 10m.

**Puff of Smoke**
Create a dramatic puff of smoke within 10m.

---

### Elementalism Spells

**Fireball**
- Power Level: 1 | Cost: 2 WP
- Requirements: Word + Gesture
- Action: Standard | Range: 20m | Duration: Instant
- Effect: 2D6 fire damage to target.
- Higher Power Levels: +D6 damage per level, or create an additional fireball.

**Gust of Wind**
- Power Level: 1 | Cost: 2 WP
- Requirements: Word + Gesture
- Action: Standard | Range: 10m cone | Duration: Instant
- Effect: Pushes creatures and objects 2D4 meters; deals 2D4 bludgeoning damage. Swarms take 2D6 damage.

**Pillar**
- Power Level: 1 | Cost: 2 WP
- Requirements: Word + Gesture
- Action: Standard | Range: 10m | Duration: Shift
- Effect: Creates a 3-meter-tall stone pillar. Each additional power level adds 3 more meters of height (falling damage applies if someone is on top).

## Armor

Armor reduces incoming damage by its **Armor Rating**.

| Armor | Rating | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Leather | 2 | — |
| Studded Leather | 3 | +2 vs. bludgeoning |
| Chainmail | 4 | +2 vs. slashing; bane on Swimming |
| Plate | 5 | Restricts movement; heavy |

### Helmets

| Helmet | Bonus | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Light Helmet | +1 Armor | — |
| Full Helmet | +2 Armor | Bane on Awareness, Spot Hidden |

Equipping or removing armor is a **standard action** in combat.

## Monsters

### Key Differences from Player Characters

- **Attacks never miss** — Monster attacks succeed automatically unless dodged or parried
- **Ferocity** — Number of initiative cards drawn each round (determines how many times the monster acts)
- **Fear** — Fear attacks are automatic; target must make a WIL roll to resist
- **Immune** — Some monsters are immune to specific damage types

### Resistance to Attacks

Monsters can still **dodge** or **parry** (uses one of their actions for the round).

### Monster Stat Block

| Stat | Description |
|------|-------------|
| HP | Hit points |
| WP | Willpower points (used for magic/special abilities) |
| AGL | Determines dodge/parry reactions |
| Armor | Reduces damage |
| Ferocity | Cards drawn per round |
| Skills | Attack skills, Evade, etc. |
| Special | Immunities, fear abilities, special attacks |

## Hazards

### Darkness
- Cannot dash
- Cannot use ranged attacks
- Must make **Awareness** roll before attacking in melee; failure means missing entirely

### Poison
- Opposed roll: poison potency vs. target **CON**
- **Full effect**: Maximum poison damage/duration
- **Limited effect**: Half damage/duration
- Types: Lethal, Paralyzing, Sleeping

### Fear
- Creature makes a fear attack (automatic)
- Target rolls **WIL** to resist
- Failure: roll on fear table (flee, freeze, surrender, etc.)

### Cold
- Periodic **Bushcraft** rolls required
- Failure: lose D6 HP and D6 WP

### Drowning
- **CON** roll each round underwater without air
- Failure: take D6 damage

### Falling
- **D6 damage per 2 meters** fallen
- **Acrobatics** roll reduces number of damage dice (success removes one die, Dragon removes all)
