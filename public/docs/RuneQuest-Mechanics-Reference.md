## Core Mechanics

**Dice notation:** D6 = six-sided, D100 = percentile (two D10s, one as tens digit). Roll ≤ skill% = success. Roll 01–05 = automatic success (attack/parry). Roll 96–00 = automatic failure (attack/parry).

**Characteristics:** STR, CON, SIZ, INT, POW, DEX, CHA — rolled 3D6 (or 2D6+6 for SIZ/INT). Range 3–18 for humans; max 21.

**Characteristic Rolls:** Multiply characteristic by:
- ×5 = Easy
- ×4 = Moderate
- ×3 = Average
- ×2 = Difficult
- ×1 = Very Difficult

## Encumbrance (ENC)

Maximum ENC = average of STR+CON, capped at STR.

**Penalties per ENC point over maximum:**
- Movement: −1 unit
- Defense: −5%
- Strike Rank: +1 SR
- All skills: −5%

## Combat

### Attack and Parry

- Roll D100 ≤ attack skill% = hit
- Roll D100 ≤ parry skill% = successful parry
- 01–05: always hits (attacks/parries)
- 96–00: always fails (attacks/parries)

### Critical Hit

Roll ≤ 5% of skill needed (e.g., skill 60% → critical on 1–3).
- Critical attack vs. normal parry: parry negated; full damage ignores armor
- Critical attack vs. critical parry: parry succeeds normally
- Critical attack vs. normal attack: attacker wins

### Impale

Roll ≤ 20% of attack skill (impaling weapons only).
- Maximum damage + weapon remains in wound
- Each additional round weapon stays: roll D6, 1–2 = falls out; 3–6 = stays (victim may pull out at full damage again)

### Fumble

Roll ≥ 96% OR within 5–20% of maximum needed (varies by weapon):

**Full Fumble Table (D100):**

| D100 | Result |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Drop weapon |
| 11–20 | Fall prone |
| 21–30 | Strike ally (randomly determined) |
| 31–40 | Strike self |
| 41–50 | Weapon breaks |
| 51–60 | Lose next action |
| 61–70 | Stagger (lose this action) |
| 71–80 | Lose shield |
| 81–90 | Stumble — opponent gets free attack |
| 91–00 | Roll twice |

*(Full 40-entry table exists in source; keeper-specific results include armor breaking, losing grip, and other complications.)*

### Defense

Defender may attempt to dodge (Defense skill, DEX×5 base) instead of parrying.

### Armor

Armor absorbs damage after hit. Remaining damage applied to location. Silent armor penalties apply (see Armor Table).

### Grappling

- Attacker rolls Grapple skill (Manipulation-based) to grab opponent
- If successful, initiate hold: each round, both roll STR×5; winner controls position
- Hold can be used to: throw (STR vs. STR+SIZ on Resistance Table), pin (STR vs. STR), or choke (CON vs. STR each round, lose 1 CON per failure)

### Healing

- Healing spell restores HP per POW spent (see Battle Magic)
- Natural healing: 1 HP per week per location
- A limb at 0 must be healed to 1+ before it becomes functional

### Experience (Improving Skills)

After a successful use of a skill in a stressful situation, the character may attempt an **experience roll** at session end:

Roll D100 > current skill% + (INT−12)×3. If successful, add 5% to that skill.

*(INT 12 = no bonus; INT above 12 gives +3% per point over 12 to the experience threshold; INT below 12 gives −3% per point.)*

### Weapon Training Costs

| Weapon Category | Improvement per 5% | Cost (Lunars) |
|---|---|---|
| Any weapon skill | +5% | 50×current rating Lunars |

*(Training requires a teacher with higher skill. Maximum trainable skill = 75% via training alone; above that only experience.)*

### Alchemist Skills

Alchemists can create potions and identify substances. Skills include: Brew Poison, Brew Potion, Identify Substance, Refine Compound. Base values by INT modifier.

### Poison Mechanics

Potency vs. CON on Resistance Table. If poison wins: victim suffers listed effect. If CON wins: no effect. Blade venom: applied to weapon before combat, activates on successful hit.

### Sage Skills

Sages have specialized knowledge in one or more fields. May attempt to answer questions in field at INT×5 (or higher with training). Research time varies.

### Riding

Riding skill: DEX×3 base. Difficult maneuvers require a Riding roll. If a mount is killed or bucks, rider must roll Riding or fall (1D6 damage to a random location per 3m fall).

## Poisons

Poisons have a Potency characteristic. Victim rolls CON vs. Potency on Resistance Table.

- Win: no effect
- Lose: suffer listed effect (paralysis, sleep, damage, death)

Blade venoms last D6 rounds after application. Each successful hit may activate poison once.

## Basic Magic (Battle Magic)

### General Rules

- POW = spell casting resource (also serves as characteristic)
- INT = maximum POW points of spells held in mind simultaneously
- Casting a spell costs POW; spent POW recovers at rate of 1/4 total POW per 6 hours
- Spells take effect on the caster's SR plus spell SR (see Spell Strike Rank Table)

### Spell Types

|---|---|

| Passive | Takes effect, then persists without attention |
| Instant | Takes effect immediately and ends |
| Temporal | Lasts a set number of rounds/turns (Duration) |
| Permanent | Lasts indefinitely until removed |

### Battle Magic Spell Descriptions

**Befuddle (2 POW, Temporal):** Target must resist with INT. If it fails, target is confused: cannot attack coherently, −50% to all skills, random actions if ordered.

**Bladesharp 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +5% to attack and +1 damage to a cutting/thrusting weapon. Lasts until caster terminates or is disrupted.

**Bludgeon 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +5% to attack and +1 damage to a bludgeoning weapon.

**Countermagic 1–3 (1–3 POW, Passive):** Protects target from spells up to the spell's POW level. Incoming spells must overcome Countermagic or fail. Multiple Countermagic spells stack.

**Coordination 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +5% to all DEX-based skills (attack, parry, Manipulation category, Defense).

**Darkwall (2 POW, Temporal):** Creates a zone of darkness in a 3m×3m area. Those inside cannot see. Those outside cannot see in. Lasts until caster terminates.

**Demoralize (2 POW, Temporal):** Target must resist with POW. If it fails, target is demoralized: −30% to all combat skills, will not willingly attack.

**Detection Blank (1 POW, Passive):** Protects target from all Detect spells. The target becomes undetectable to magic for the spell duration.

**Detect Enemies (1 POW, Instant):** Caster senses the direction of all beings within POW×3 meters who intend harm to the caster.

**Detect Life (1 POW, Instant):** Caster senses the direction of all living beings within POW×3 meters.

**Detect Magic (1 POW, Instant):** Caster senses the presence and direction of magic within POW×3 meters.

**Detect Substance (1 POW, Instant):** Caster senses the direction of a specified substance (gold, iron, wood, etc.) within POW×3 meters. Each substance is a separate spell.

**Detect Traps (1 POW, Instant):** Caster senses the location of traps within POW×3 meters.

**Dullblade 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level reduces the target weapon's attack by −5% and damage by −1 (minimum 1 damage).

**Extinguish (1 POW, Instant):** Instantly extinguishes one small fire (torch, candle) per POW spent.

**Farsee 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level multiplies the caster's visual range by 2. Level 1 = ×2, Level 2 = ×4, Level 3 = ×8, Level 4 = ×16.

**Fanaticism (1 POW, Temporal):** Target must resist with POW, or becomes fanatical: +10% to attack skills but may not parry or flee, and will always fight to the death.

**Firearrow (1 POW, Instant):** One missile is enchanted to burst into flame on impact, dealing an extra 1D6 fire damage.

**Fireblade (3 POW, Temporal):** One cutting or thrusting weapon is coated in magical fire. Adds +2D6 fire damage per strike. Lasts until caster terminates.

**Glamour 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +5% to CHA-related social skills and rolls. Works for disguise and persuasion.

**Glue (2 POW, Passive):** Bonds two surfaces together. STR×5 roll to break the bond; each success reduces effective STR by 3 (cumulative). Does not work on metal/stone bonds.

**Harmonize (1 POW, Temporal):** Target group within earshot receives +10% to social interactions with each other. Reduces hostility.

**Healing 1–6 (1–6 POW, Instant):** Restores 1 HP per POW spent to a single hit location. Cannot restore HP above location maximum.

**Ignite (1 POW, Instant):** Sets a flammable target (torch, cloth, dry grass) on fire instantly.

**Invisibility (3 POW, Temporal):** Target becomes invisible. All attacks against target are at −40%. Target cannot attack without the spell being disrupted. Any action other than walking disrupts invisibility.

**Light (1 POW, Temporal):** Creates light equivalent to a torch in a 10m radius. Lasts until terminated.

**Lightwall (2 POW, Temporal):** Creates a wall of blinding light (POW×1 m long). Those looking through it must roll POW×5 or be blinded for 1D3 rounds. Lasts until terminated.

**Mindspeech 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level allows mental communication with 1 additional being within POW×3 meters. Communication is clear and silent.

**Mobility (1 POW, Temporal):** Target moves at double normal speed (running, swimming, etc.). Strike Rank reduced by 2.

**Multimissile 1–4 (1–4 POW, Instant):** One missile becomes 1+level copies, each striking a separate random target within range. All use the same attack roll.

**Parry 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +5% to one specific parry skill.

**Pierce (1 POW, Instant):** One impaling weapon ignores up to 4 points of armor on the next attack. Must be cast before the attack roll.

**Protection 1–4 (1–4 POW, Passive):** Each level adds 1 point of magical armor to all locations of target. Stacks with physical armor.

**Repair (1–3 POW, Instant):** Restores 1 HP per POW spent to a damaged object (weapon, armor, shield). Cannot restore above original HP.

**Second Sight (3 POW, Temporal):** Caster can see invisible beings, detect magic on objects (glowing aura), and see through Darkwall. Active for POW rounds.

**Silence (1 POW, Temporal):** Target or area (3m radius) is completely silent. No sound enters or exits. Lasts until terminated.

**Sleep (3 POW, Temporal):** Target must resist with CON. If it fails, target falls asleep. Lasts until damage wakes them or duration expires. Only affects beings with less than 10 total HP.

**Slow (1 POW, Temporal):** Target must resist with POW or moves at half speed. +2 SR to all actions.

**Speedart (1 POW, Instant):** One missile gains +10% to attack and +3 damage on next shot.

**Spiritscreen 1–4 (1–4 POW, Passive):** Protects target's POW from spiritual attacks. Each level adds 5% to Spirit Combat defense.

**Strength 1–4 (1–4 POW, Temporal):** Each level adds +3 to STR for damage bonus and Resistance Table purposes.

**Translate (2 POW, Temporal):** Caster can understand and speak one language not normally known. Lasts POW Turns.

**Vigor (2 POW, Temporal):** Target ignores fatigue and ENC penalties for the spell's duration. Does not restore actual HP.

**Visibility (1 POW, Instant):** Renders one invisible or concealed being visible for 1 round.

**Xenohealing (2 POW, Instant):** As Healing 2, but works on non-human species (trolls, elves, ducks, etc.) that cannot normally receive Healing from human-taught spells.

## Spirit Combat

Spirits have POW, INT, and sometimes CON and CHA. Physical characteristics (STR, SIZ, DEX) are 0 unless stated.

**Engaging a Spirit:** If a spirit initiates contact, or a shaman makes contact, Spirit Combat begins.

**Spirit Combat Resolution (per round):**
- Both roll D100 ≤ Spirit Combat skill (POW×3 base)
- Winner's roll (if they succeeded) determines damage: D6 + POW bonus
- Damage reduces loser's POW
- POW reaches 0: entity is either dead (spirit) or possessed/unconscious (character)

**Spirit Combat Skills:**
- Normal character: POW×3%
- Shaman: POW×5% (see Shaman rules)

**Binding Spirits:**
After defeating a spirit in spirit combat, winner may bind it into a object (crystal, skull, etc.). Bound spirits provide their characteristic POW as a reservoir for the binder's spells. Bound spirits may also have skills usable by binder.

**Familiar:**
A shaman's fetch acts as a familiar. Provides characteristic POW on the material plane, warns of danger, and assists with spirit world travel.

## Rune Magic

### Cult Membership Levels

**Lay Member:** Worships the cult. No special abilities beyond attending ceremonies.

**Initiate Requirements:**
- Make sacrifice of POW (specific amount varies by cult, typically 1 POW)
- Accept cult geases (restrictions)

**Initiate Benefits:**
- Access to cult special Divine Intervention
- Can learn cult's Rune Magic spells (those with minimum access)
- Special training at cult rates

**Rune Lord Requirements:**
- Be an initiate in good standing for at least 1 year
- Have POW 15 or higher
- Have 5 separate skills at 90%+ (cult-specified skills)

**Rune Lord Benefits:**
- Use of cult magic items freely
- Divine Intervention at normal rate
- 100%+ skill rule: rolls above 100% subtract excess from opponent's parry/dodge skill
- Allied Spirit (after further service)
- Iron weapon available if cult provides
- Magic resistance: at maximum natural POW, immune to outside magic requiring POW vs. POW rolls

**Rune Priest Requirements:**
- Be an initiate in good standing
- POW 18 or higher
- Know how to read and write cult language

**Rune Priest Benefits:**
- Access to all Standard Rune Spells
- Access to all cult-specific Rune Spells
- Regain Rune Magic at 1 point per day of worship
- Rune Priests may not train STR, CON, or DEX (these are now fixed)
- DEX-based skills can exceed normal maximums via DEX×5 rolls

**Rune Priest Restrictions:**
- Must spend minimum 1/4 of time in cult duties
- May not use skills unbecoming the cult

### Rune Magic Casting

- Rune Magic spells cost points of POW sacrificed **permanently** to learn (not spent each use)
- Casting a Rune Spell: costs the stored spell's POW (taken from "Rune Points") — but POW is not lost, merely "used"
- Used Rune Spell points return at rate of 1/day for Rune Priests (via worship)
- Other initiates regain Rune Magic only on cult holy days
- Casting SR = 1 per POW point of spell

### Divine Intervention

Rune Lords and Priests may call on their deity for miraculous aid.

**Divine Intervention Procedure:**
1. Player announces Divine Intervention
2. Roll D100 ≤ current POW
3. If successful: deity intervenes; POW permanently reduced by amount rolled (minimum 1)
4. If failed: nothing happens; POW still reduced by 1 for the attempt

**Divine Intervention Table (what deity can do, by POW spent):**
- 1–5 POW: Minor miracle (escape, hint, small healing)
- 6–10 POW: Major miracle (resurrect, create barrier, slay foe)
- 11–15 POW: World-altering event
- 16+ POW: Deity physically manifests briefly

### Allied Spirit

Rune Lords may gain an Allied Spirit after additional service and sacrifice.

**Allied Spirit Procedure:**
1. The Rune Lord sacrifices POW into a prepared object (weapon, crystal, etc.)
2. The deity grants a spirit of equal POW to inhabit the object
3. Spirit provides its INT, POW, and potentially skills to the Rune Lord

### Stackable Rune Magic

Certain Rune Spells can be cast at higher levels (e.g., Shield 1–4). Each level = 1 additional POW point cost and 1 additional SR.

### Rune Spell Descriptions

**Absorption 1–4 (Passive):** Absorbs incoming spells with POW cost ≤ Absorption level. Converts absorbed spell POW to caster's POW points (not permanent). Excess levels wasted.

**Discorporation (3 POW, Special):** Caster's spirit leaves body and enters the spirit world. Body remains in place, comatose. Caster can interact with the spirit world, contact spirits, etc. Returning requires retracing the path (or Divine Intervention).

**Dismiss Elemental 1/2/3 (Instant):** Instantly banishes a Small/Medium/Large elemental. Must overcome elemental's POW on Resistance Table. Cost matches elemental size.

**Divination (1 POW, Special):** Caster asks deity one question. Deity answers truthfully but may be cryptic. Duration: the answer is instant.

**Extension 1/2/3 (Passive):** Cast simultaneously with another Rune Spell. Multiplies that spell's duration by 10×/100×/1000×. Must stack with a temporal Rune Spell.

**Matrix Creation (1–5 POW, Permanent):** Creates a spell matrix (see Treasure) holding a battle magic spell up to the level paid. Subsequent uses of the matrix cost the embedded spell's normal POW from the user.

**Mind Link 1–3 (Temporal):** Links minds of up to 3+level beings. All share sensory input and can communicate silently. Lasts POW rounds.

**Multispell 1/2/3 (Instant):** In the same round, the caster may also cast 1/2/3 additional spells (Battle or Rune). Additional spells cost normal POW. SR still applies per spell.

**Reflection 1–4 (Passive):** Any spell directed at the caster with POW ≤ Reflection level is automatically reflected back at the original caster. Spells above Reflection level pass through normally.

**Runepower 1–3 (Instant):** Immediately restores 1/2/3 points of used Rune Magic to the caster. Cannot restore more than total Rune Points held.

**Shield 1–4 (Passive):** Adds 1 armor point to all locations per level AND adds 5% per level to Countermagic effect. Stacks with Protection and physical armor.

**Spell Teaching (1 POW, Permanent):** Permanently teaches one battle magic spell (up to 4 POW) to the target. The recipient does not need to seek a teacher or pay training cost.

**Spirit Block 1–4 (Passive):** Reduces incoming Spirit Combat damage by 3×level. A Spirit Block 4 reduces damage by 12 per round.

**Vision (1 POW, Instant):** Caster sees (but does not hear) a specific place the deity has awareness of. Duration: instant glimpse.

**Warding 1–4 (1–4 POW, Passive):** Creates a magical alarm around a specified area (up to POW×2 meters per side). Any creature with fewer than Warding level×3 POW who crosses the boundary: (a) is detected by the caster; (b) takes Warding level D6 damage if hostile; (c) may be barred from entry. The warding remains until the POW is returned (disrupted) or the caster dismisses it.

## Spell Matrices

A Spell Matrix is an object (ring, sword, amulet, etc.) enchanted to hold a Battle Magic spell.

- **Uses:** A matrix may be used any number of times, but each use costs the spell's normal POW from the user
- **Switching Matrix:** If using a matrix for a different spell than last time, +3 SR to cast
- **Storage:** The spell is always available to any owner; does not require INT slots
- **Creating a Matrix:** Only via Matrix Creation Rune Spell (see Rune Magic)

## Magic Crystals

### Types and Rules

**POW Storage Crystals:**
- Can store 2D6+3 POW points (rolled when found; this is the crystal's capacity)
- User must attune crystal before using stored POW (see Attunement)
- Attuned owner can draw stored POW to cast spells (counts as own POW for casting, not for characteristic purposes)
- POW refills at same rate as normal POW recovery (1/4 per 6 hours)

**Powered Crystals:**
- Pre-charged crystals (permanent POW stored)
- Attunement required before use
- Each type of powered crystal has specific effects (see Crystal Table)

### Crystal Attunement

- Spend 1 week meditating with crystal (no other activity)
- Roll POW vs. crystal's POW on Resistance Table
- Success: attuned; crystal responds to owner
- Failure: must wait 1 week before trying again
- Transfer: crystal loses attunement if given away; new owner must re-attune

## Optional Combat Rules (Appendix C)

### Aimed Blows

**Variant 1:** Attacker declares specific location before rolling. Attack at −30%. If hit, damage goes to declared location.

**Variant 2:** Attacker declares specific location. Normal attack roll. If roll succeeds AND result would naturally hit declared location: normal hit. If roll succeeds but wrong location: shot deflects, GM chooses actual location. If roll fails: miss.

### Knockback

When damage from a single blow (after armor) equals or exceeds target's SIZ÷3:
- Roll STR+SIZ (attacker) vs. SIZ+DEX (defender) on Resistance Table
- Defender loses: knocked back 1 meter per point below needed + must roll DEX×5 or fall
- Knockback + fall = +1D6 damage, additional Resistance Roll to avoid leg injury

### Shield Attacks

A shield may be used to actively bash instead of parrying.

| Shield | Attack SR | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Small | +3 to base SR | 1D4+DB |
| Medium | +2 to base SR | 1D6+DB |
| Large | +4 to base SR | 1D8+DB |

Shield Bash: roll Shield Attack skill (starts at Parry skill% −20%); successful hit knocks target back (see Knockback).

### Slashing Weapons

If attack roll ≤ 20% of needed skill (e.g., skill 60% → roll 1–12): automatic max damage × 2 to location. However, weapon lodges in wound (see Impale rules for removal).

### Crushing Weapons

If attack roll ≤ 20% of needed skill: add full Damage Bonus maximum to damage. Target must also roll CON×5 or be stunned for 1 round.

### Encumbrance Variants

**Swenson Variant:** Max ENC = STR. No other factors. Penalty: −5% per ENC over max to all DEX-based skills.

**Sapienza Variant:** Max ENC = (STR + CON) ÷ 2. Penalty: −3% per ENC over max to all skills, −2 movement per 3 ENC over max.

## Natural Hazards (Appendix E)

### Darkness

All skill rolls at −75% in total darkness. Characters who have spent 10+ minutes in dark: −50%. Elves and trolls with darksense: unaffected.

### Dropped Lamp Table (D100)

|---|---|

| 31–60 | Stays lit, falls upright |
| 61–80 | Stays lit, falls sideways (may ignite nearby material) |
| 81–00 | Breaks; oil spills; fire spreads |

### Fire

- Small fire (torch): 1D4 damage/round to area touching
- Large fire (bonfire): 1D8 damage/round to area touching
- Characters on fire: must roll DEX×5 each round to extinguish; otherwise continue taking damage
- Armor provides AP against fire damage as normal

### Drowning

Character can hold breath for CON rounds. After that: roll CON×5 each round or lose 1 HP (all locations) from oxygen deprivation. At 0 HP total: unconscious. After (CON) more rounds unconscious: dead.

### Machine Throwers (Catapults, Onagers, etc.)

Damage = 3D6–8D6 depending on machine size. Range varies. Deviation roll if target is under cover (D6 meters to random direction).

## Diseases (Appendix I)

### Disease Rules

**Contraction Roll:** Each time exposed, roll D100 ≤ current HP×5 or contract the disease.

**Disease Progression:** Chronic → Acute → Terminal (if untreated)

**Recovery:** Requires either magical treatment or rest + successful CON rolls. Xenohealing functions as disease cure if used within 1 round of contraction.

### Disease Table

| Disease | Chronic Effect | Acute Effect | Terminal Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wasting | Lose 1 CON/month | Lose 1 CON/hour | Lose 1 CON/Full Turn; death at CON 0 |
| Brain Fever | −10% INT-based skills/month | Lose 1 INT/hour | Unconscious; death in 1D6 days |
| Soul Waste | Lose 1 POW/month | Lose 1 POW/day | Lose 1 POW/hour; death at POW 0 |
| Creeping Chills | −5% all skills/month | −20% all skills; lose 1 CON/day | Paralysis; death in 1D3 days |
| Shakes | DEX−2/month | DEX−5; STR−2/day | Convulsions; death in 1D6 hours |

**Cure:** Healing 6 (or multiple Healing spells totaling 6 POW) clears any disease at chronic stage. Acute and terminal require Rune Magic (Cure All Disease if cult provides it) or extremely potent alchemical treatment.
