# OSRIC Weapons & Armor

Costs are in gold pieces (gp); encumbrance is in pounds. Damage shown is against man-sized or smaller creatures. In OSRIC melee, a natural 20 always hits and a natural 1 always misses; weapon damage adds the wielder's STR damage bonus (melee and hurled weapons only — not bows or crossbows, though composite bows in the full rules can be rated for STR).

**Class weapon restrictions**: Clerics may use only blunt weapons (club, mace, flail, hammer, staff). Druids may use club, dagger, sling, spear, staff, and scimitar. Magic Users are limited to dagger, staff, and darts; Illusionists likewise. Thieves favor one-handed piercing weapons and may not use two-handed swords, pole arms, or heavy crossbows. Fighters, Paladins, and Rangers may use anything.

## Shields

| Shield | AC Bonus | Weight (lb) | Cost (gp) |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Shield | −1 AC | 10 | 10 |

A shield improves armor class by 1 against attacks from the front and shield-side flank. It cannot be used with a two-handed weapon.

## Armor

OSRIC uses **descending Armor Class**: unarmored is AC 10 and lower is better. DEX adjusts AC (a bonus subtracts further), and a shield subtracts 1. Armor also limits movement rate (base 12").

|-------|----|-------------|----------|-------|
| None | 10 | 0 | 12" | — |
| Leather | 8 | 15 | 12" | Hardened leather; the thief's heaviest option |
| Padded Gambeson | 8 | 10 | 9" | Quilted cloth; cheap, hot, and bulky |
| Studded Leather | 7 | 20 | 9" | Leather reinforced with metal studs |
| Ring Mail | 7 | 35 | 9" | Metal rings sewn to a leather backing |

| Banded Mail | 4 | 35 | 9" | Overlapping metal bands on chain and leather |
| Splint Mail | 4 | 40 | 6" | Vertical metal splints on a leather backing |
| Plate Mail | 3 | 45 | 6" | Plates over chain; the knight's harness |
| Field Plate | 2 | 50 | 9" | Full articulated plate, custom-fitted; a lord's ransom |

**Class armor restrictions**: Magic Users and Illusionists may wear no armor. Thieves are limited to leather (heavier armor penalizes or prevents thief skills). Druids wear only leather and wooden shields. Clerics, Fighters, Paladins, and Rangers may wear any armor.
