# Kal-Arath Spells

Magic in Kal-Arath comes from **Demonic Pacts**. Casting is an **INT check vs. Spell Difficulty**: base difficulty **8, +1 per tier** of the spell. A caster may only attempt a spell of a tier as high as their current unaltered INT score. Natural 12s always succeed and the effect is doubled or "extreme." Critical success doubles the effect of the spell.

**Casting Failure** — the caster takes 1 damage and cannot cast again until a rest.

**Critical Failure** — roll on the Arcane Disaster table (below).

## Dooms

To maintain a pact, the caster takes on a **Doom** — a taboo or observance that must be honored:

| d6 | Doom |
|----|------|
| 1 | Must not use metal weapons or armor. Violation breaks the pact — all spells and powers are lost. |
| 2 | Each new moon, must sacrifice something of great value (monetarily or otherwise, up to and including human sacrifice). Failure may result in a curse or loss of powers. |
| 3 | Must speak the names and prayers of the demon master aloud when casting. Cannot cast if silenced; casting is loud and attracts attention of any in earshot. |
| 4 | At the demon's behest, must travel to a desolate place of powerful significance and spend d6 days and nights there in prayer and meditation. |
| 5 | Spells must be inscribed on scrolls written in blood, costing 1 HP permanently per "active" scroll. Destroying a scroll returns the HP, but that spell cannot be cast until a new scroll is made (1 full night of work). |
| 6 | May not speak for a number of hours equal to the tier of the last spell cast. Breaking silence early may mean losing that spell's use, or some other wrath from the patron. |

## Pact of Blood

### Crimson Palm Scripture
*Tier 1.* Drains d6 HP from anyone touched (no attack roll required); the caster gains an equal amount. HP over the caster's maximum are kept until the next rest or depleted as normal, to a maximum of double normal HP.

### Eternal River of Blessed Strength
*Tier 2.* Sacrifice 4 HP to increase STR and AGI by +2 for 1 hour.

### Mantra of Thirst
*Tier 3.* Exchange 6 HP to roll the next casting check at advantage.

### Ascendance of the Scarlet Flower
*Tier 4.* Summon a powerful blood demon. It will do your bidding for d6 rounds.
**Stats** — HP: 5d6, Armor: 2, Damage: Claws 2d6, Morale: 10.

### Mandala of Calm Serenity
*Tier 5.* Sacrifice 2 HP to deal d6 damage automatically to multiple targets in missile range. This can be stacked (e.g., 8 HP for 4d6 split among multiple targets).

## Pact of Destruction

### Vision of Transience
*Tier 1.* Shatter/disintegrate one non-magical item within missile range no larger than a jug of wine or length of rope, or cause d6 damage to one individual within missile range.

### Way of the Sundering Fist
*Tier 2.* All your unarmed blows roll damage at advantage.

### Temple's Demolishing Breath
*Tier 3.* Force wave of damage around the caster: d6 damage to all in melee range, knocking them back 10'.

### Cleansing By Fire
*Tier 4.* Produces an exploding ball of flame that can be sent up to 50' from the caster. Causes 3d6 damage to all within a 20' radius.

### Enlightenment Through Ruin
*Tier 5.* The caster produces meteor-like storms for d6 rounds plus one per level, capable of destroying a small village or a whole battalion. Everyone within a 100'×100' area takes 2d6 damage per round.

## Pact of Domination

### Diamond Mind Shatters
*Tier 1.* The caster causes uncontrollable confusion and fear in d6 targets for d6 rounds.

### Doctrine of the Subservient Path
*Tier 2.* Target individual obeys the caster within reasonable boundaries of friendship for 1 day.

### Life Is Only A Mirage
*Tier 3.* Create a believable illusion up to the size of a large temple. To maintain it, the caster must keep total concentration and can take no other action beyond a slow walk over open ground.

### Heavenly Edict of Obedience
*Tier 4.* Complete mental domination and control over an individual. The spell must be recast each day (at advantage) to maintain.

### Liberation From All Earthly Choice
*Tier 5.* Vast radius of command: gain temporary control of d6×10 individuals for d6 rounds plus the caster's level.

## Pact of Illumination

### Flame of Insight
*Tier 1.* Success means the caster can force a re-roll of any one roll each time this spell is cast.

### Lantern of the Revealing Path
*Tier 2.* Shows the location of any object known to the caster.

### Ecstatic Meditation on Death
*Tier 3.* For one day, gain advantage on all attack and dodge rolls.

### All-Encompassing Gaze
*Tier 4.* Gain d6 Fate Points this session.

### Golden Final Enlightenment
*Tier 5.* Any roll made in the game is subject to one re-roll at the caster's choice. The effect lasts the entire session.

## Pact of Shadow

### Sworn to the Dark
*Tier 1.* Invisibility except in full daylight for 1 hour per level of the caster. Attacking negates the effect.

### Veil of the Hidden Monastery
*Tier 2.* Creates a mobile globe of shadow and darkness 20' around the caster. He can see out; none can see in. Lasts d6 rounds plus 1 per level.

### Silent Passage Through The Realm of the Master
*Tier 3.* Teleport from one shadow to another that the caster can see, no matter how far.

### Blessed Guardian of Night's Temple
*Tier 4.* Summon a lesser demon of the shadow realm to do your bidding for d6 rounds plus one per level.
**Stats** — HP: 24, Armor: 2, Damage: 2d6, Morale: 10.

### Void of the Black Lotus
*Tier 5.* The caster kills one individual with a mantra. Roll d6: on a 6, gain a level.

## Pact of Corruption

### Decay is the Way of All Things
*Tier 1.* Animate one recently deceased body to serve you for d6 hours, with whatever gear/equipment it had in life. Only one undead servant may be animated at a time. After d6 hours, it returns to death.
**Stats** — as Skeleton Warrior (Bestiary).

### Clouds Descend To The Earth
*Tier 2.* A thick fog 30' around the caster causes d6 damage per round to all inside it except the caster, for d6 rounds.

### Withering Mandala
*Tier 3.* Necrotic rot for d6 damage on d6 targets. If they die from this attack, they animate as ghouls under the caster's control. They last d6 days or until destroyed by other means.

### Soldiers Of the 10,000 Strong Army
*Tier 4.* Summon 3d6 ghouls in a thick fog that follows them. The caster must maintain total concentration or lose control of them.

### Forbidden Temple of the 7th Sigil
*Tier 5.* Causes a major plague outbreak in any populated area, lasting d6 days. Each day, anyone entering the affected area or interacting with the infected has a 1–4 in 6 chance of contracting it. The caster is daily joined by 2d6 plague victims now under his control as Plague Ghouls.

## Arcane Disaster (Critical Casting Failure)

| 2d6 | Result |
|-----|--------|
| 2 | A lesser demon takes hold of the caster's body. The caster's soul is trapped, tormented, and totally enslaved. Your adventure is at an end! |
| 3 | Blood vessels burst, causing severe internal bleeding. Reduced to 0 HP and −2 TOU permanently unless healed by powerful magic. (Roll on Death Table.) |
| 4 | Sigils and mantras overwhelm the mind, causing permanent psychic damage. −2 INT and vivid, traumatic hallucinations. |
| 5 | Part of the caster's soul is torn away. −1 INT and −1 PRE; horror, despair, and overwhelming emptiness. |
| 6 | Cursed by a demon: misfortune and accidents. Lose the ability to use Fate Points until the curse is ended. |
| 7 | The caster's body changes disturbingly, granting a monstrous appearance. −1 PRE, but +1 PRE to intimidation. |
| 8 | Sigils burn into the flesh, leaving permanent painful scars. −1 PRE and chronic pain: STR checks at disadvantage. |
| 9 | The magic lashes back, causing burns and injuries. −1 permanent HP. |
| 10 | A psychic echo of the failed spell causes headaches and nightmares. −1 INT for d6 sessions. |
| 11 | The spell drains the caster's vitality. −1 STR from fatigue for the remainder of the session. |
| 12 | The spell fails, but the caster suffers only a minor psychic backlash. |
