# I. INTRODUCTION

## Background

RuneQuest is set in the world of Glorantha, a fantasy world rich with mythology, magic, and conflict. The Third Age of Glorantha is the primary setting for adventurer activities.

## Key Setting Elements

- **Pelorian Chronology**: The current era is S.T. (Storm Time) in the Pelorian calendar
- **Glorantha Map**: A world of diverse lands, cultures, and magical forces
- **Mythology as Living Reality**: In Glorantha, myths are not just stories—they actively shape the world and its inhabitants

## Role of Cults

Cults represent the organized religious and magical traditions of various societies. Cults provide:
- Magic access (spirit and rune magic)
- Social structure and identity
- Ritual practices and holy days
- Protection and community benefits
