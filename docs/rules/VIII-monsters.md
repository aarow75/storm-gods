# VIII. MONSTERS

## Overview

Glorantha is filled with a diverse array of creatures, from mundane animals to chaos-corrupted abominations. This section covers the creatures adventurers may encounter, including their statistics, special abilities, and combat mechanics.

**Note**: The RuneQuest: Glorantha Bestiary hardcover provides extensive creature details. This section covers core concepts and common examples.

---

## 1. Creatures of Glorantha

### Creature Categories

Creatures in RuneQuest are organized into several categories:

| Category | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Animals** | Natural creatures of Glorantha | Bears, wolves, shadowcats, tuskers |
| **Non-Human Races** | Intelligent races predating or contemporary with humans | Elves, dwarves, trolls, dragonewts, merfolk |
| **Elementals** | Pure elemental spirits given form | Air, Earth, Fire, Water elementals |
| **Spirits** | Non-corporeal entities from the Spirit World | Ghost, shade, ancestral spirits |
| **Chaos Creatures** | Corrupted or perverted life forms | Broos, harpies, scorpion men, gorps |

---

## 2. Non-Human Races

### Overview

Several intelligent non-human races inhabit Glorantha. Each has unique characteristics, combat abilities, and cultural traditions.

### Aldryami (Elves)

**Characteristics**:
- Plant-like humanoids, standing ~170cm tall, weighing ~65kg
- Native to forests; worship the goddess Aldrya
- Multiple color variants (green, yellow, red) based on forest type

**Typical Stats**:
- STR: 2D6 (avg 7)
- CON: 3D6 (avg 10)
- SIZ: 2D6 (avg 7)
- DEX: 3D6+6 (avg 17)
- INT: 3D6+3 (avg 13)
- POW: 3D6 (avg 10)
- CHA: 3D6 (avg 10)

**Combat Abilities**:
- Weapons: Bow (often 60%+), spear
- Natural armor: 1-2 points (bark skin)
- Special: Archers, excellent perception

**Special Rules**:
- **Iron Vulnerability**: Iron weapons deal double damage when penetrating elf armor
- **Rune Affinity**: Plant Form Rune (high affinity)
- **Longevity**: Elves age much slower than humans
- **Forest Bonding**: +20% to skills when in their native forest type

---

### Mostali (Dwarves)

**Characteristics**:
- Underground dwellers claiming descent from Mostal the Maker
- Organized into occupational castes (Rock, Lead, Copper, Iron, Gold)
- Shorter stature (~130cm), stockier than humans

**Typical Stats** (varies by caste):
- STR: 2D6+8 (avg 15)
- CON: 2D6+8 (avg 15)
- SIZ: 1D6+3 (avg 7)
- DEX: 2D6 (avg 7)
- INT: 3D6 (avg 10)
- POW: 3D6 (avg 10)
- CHA: 2D6 (avg 7)

**Combat Abilities**:
- Iron Dwarves: Heavy armor (4-6 points chain or plate), weapons (axe 50%+)
- Other castes: Skilled craftspeople, moderate combat ability
- Hit Points: Typically 12-16

**Special Rules**:
- **Spell Resistance**: 70% magic resistance (spells have 30% base chance to affect dwarves)
- **Dark Vision**: See in complete darkness
- **Enemies**: Eternal enemies of elves and trolls; gain +20% vs. these races
- **Craftsmanship**: Exceptional skill with stone, metal, and craft work

---

### Uz (Trolls)

**Characteristics**:
- Mountain and cave dwellers
- Tall (~185-195cm), muscular, with reptilian features
- Multiple breeds: Dark Trolls (basic), Trollkin (smaller, subservient)

**Typical Stats** (Dark Troll):
- STR: 3D6+9 (avg 19)
- CON: 3D6+6 (avg 16)
- SIZ: 3D6+9 (avg 19)
- DEX: 2D6 (avg 7)
- INT: 2D6 (avg 7)
- POW: 3D6 (avg 10)
- CHA: 2D6 (avg 7)

**Combat Abilities**:
- Weapons: Axes, clubs, swords (50%+)
- Armor: Hide and bone (2-4 points)
- Hit Points: 15-18
- Special attacks: Bite (if unarmed), powerful crushing blow

**Special Rules**:
- **Iron Vulnerability**: Iron weapons deal double damage when penetrating armor
- **Dark Vision**: Superior night vision; no penalty in darkness
- **Rune Affinity**: Darkness Rune (high affinity)
- **Regeneration** (some breeds): 1 HP per day of rest (in darkness)
- **Troll Kin**: Smaller trolls (SIZ 8-12) serve dark trolls; weaker but more common

---

### Other Non-Human Races

**Dragonewts**: Bird-like humanoids with draconic features; unpredictable and magical

**Merfolk**: Aquatic humanoids; highly resistant to magic and water-based attacks

**Broos**: Chaos-corrupted humanoids with bestial features; chaotic and savage

## 3. Common Animals

### Brown Bear

**Statistics**:
- STR: 3D6+15 (avg ~24)
- CON: 2D6+6 (avg ~13)
- SIZ: 3D6+15 (avg ~25)
- DEX: 3D6 (avg ~10)
- POW: 3D6 (avg ~10)

**Combat**:
- Hit Points: 17
- Move: 8
- Strike Rank: 3
- Armor: 3 points (skin and fur)

**Attacks**:
- Bite: 50%, 1D8+2D6 damage
- Claw: 35%, 1D6+2D6 damage
- Hug: 35%, 5D6 damage (crushing)

**Behavior**: Aggressive if provoked or protecting cubs; may attack for food

---

### Shadowcat

**Statistics**:
- STR: 2D6 (avg ~7)
- CON: 2D6+6 (avg ~13)
- SIZ: 1D6 (avg ~3)
- DEX: 3D6+12 (avg ~22)
- POW: 2D6+12 (avg ~19)

**Combat**:
- Hit Points: 12
- Move: 10
- Strike Rank: 3
- Armor: None (supple hide)

**Attacks**:
- Claw: 60%, 1D6 damage
- Bite: 50%, 1D4 damage
- Rip: 40%, 3D6 damage (if claw succeeds first)

**Skills**: Dodge 50%, Hide 75%, Move Quietly 90%, Search 40%

**Special**: Ambush predator; nearly invisible in shadows; magical affinity (can sense magic)

---

### Wolf

**Statistics**:
- STR: 2D6+6 (avg ~13)
- CON: 3D6 (avg ~10)
- SIZ: 2D6+3 (avg ~10)
- DEX: 2D6+10 (avg ~17)
- POW: 3D6 (avg ~10)

**Combat**:
- Hit Points: 11
- Move: 12 (fastest land animal in RQ)
- Strike Rank: 4
- Armor: 2 points (fur)

**Attacks**:
- Bite: 30%, 1D8 damage

**Skills**: Dodge 30%, Search 60%, Track 80%

**Pack Behavior**: Wolves hunt in packs; coordinated attacks give bonuses to hit (+20% with pack support)

- CON: 1D6+12 (avg ~15)

- DEX: 1D6 (avg ~3)
- POW: 3D6 (avg ~10)

- Armor: 4 points (tough hide)

**Attacks**:

**Special**: Often ridden by tusk riders or chaos creatures; aggressive but not particularly intelligent

## 4. Non-Human Hit Location Table

Standard humanoid hit location rules (7 locations with specific HP proportions) apply to creatures with human-like anatomy. Creatures with non-humanoid bodies have specialized hit location tables.

### Non-Humanoid Hit Locations

#### Four-Legged Creatures (Bears, Wolves, Tuskers)

| Location | Roll | HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Right Front Leg** | 01-03 | 1/4 total | Loss = stumbling, reduced movement |
| **Left Front Leg** | 04-06 | 1/4 total | Loss = stumbling, reduced movement |
| **Right Rear Leg** | 07-09 | 1/4 total | Loss = stumbling, reduced movement |
| **Left Rear Leg** | 10-12 | 1/4 total | Loss = stumbling, reduced movement |
| **Abdomen** | 13-15 | 1/6 total | Vulnerable; internal organs |
| **Chest** | 16-18 | 1/3 total | Most vital; lungs, heart |
| **Head** | 19-20 | 1/8 total | Instant death at threshold |

**Special**: Loss of any single leg = –20% to movement-based skills; loss of two legs on same side = immobilized

---

#### Arthropods (Scorpion Men, Giant Insects)

| Location | Roll | HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Right Front Leg** | 01-02 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty (redundant limb) |
| **Right Middle Leg** | 03-04 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty |
| **Right Rear Leg** | 05-06 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty |
| **Left Front Leg** | 07-08 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty |
| **Left Middle Leg** | 09-10 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty |
| **Left Rear Leg** | 11-12 | 1/8 total | Loss = no additional penalty |
| **Abdomen** | 13-16 | 1/4 total | Vulnerable; vital organs |
| **Chest/Head** | 17-20 | 1/4 total | Instant death at threshold |

**Special Rules**:
- Damage exceeding double a leg's HP is **ignored** (legs are naturally expendable)
- Loss of two legs on same side = –20% to Agility skills
- Loss of three legs on same side = immobilized

---

#### Elemental Creatures (Air, Earth, Fire, Water)

| Location | HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Whole Body** | Variable | Elementals have only 1 hit location (their entire form) |

**Special Rules**:
- No armor (pure elemental)
- Vulnerable to opposing element (water harms fire elemental, etc.)
- Immune to normal weapons (magical weapons or spells required)
- Dispersal: When HP reach 0, elemental is dispersed and returns to its element

---

### Special Hit Location Modifiers

**Aimed Blow**: If targeting a specific hit location, halve attack skill, then apply other modifiers (as per standard rules)

**Multiple Limbs**: Creatures with 6+ legs can lose individual legs with minimal effect; creatures with 2-4 legs suffer greater penalties

## 5. Monster Statistics Reference

### Reading Monster Entries

Each monster stat block includes:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **STR, CON, SIZ, DEX, INT, POW, CHA** | Characteristics (rolled as indicated: e.g., 3D6, 2D6+6) |
| **Hit Points** | Total HP (total of all hit locations combined) |
| **Move** | Movement rate in MOV (meters ÷ 3) |
| **Strike Rank** | Base SR modifier |
| **Armor** | Points of natural armor or hide |
| **Skills** | Common skill percentages (Dodge, Hide, Attack, etc.) |
| **Attacks** | List of available attacks with skill % and damage |
| **Special Rules** | Unique abilities, immunities, vulnerabilities |

### Creating Custom Creatures

When designing a custom monster:

1. **Assign characteristics** — Roll or set based on creature type
2. **Calculate derived stats** — Hit points, damage bonus, healing rate
3. **Determine armor** — Natural hide, scales, or bone
4. **Create hit locations** — Use humanoid (7 locations) or specialized table
5. **Assign skills** — Combat, perception, special abilities
6. **List attacks** — Weapons, natural attacks, special abilities
7. **Special rules** — Regeneration, magical resistance, vulnerabilities

## 6. Creatures of Note

### Dragons

Dragons are among the most powerful creatures in Glorantha:

**Characteristics** (varies widely):
- STR: 4D6+20 (avg ~35+)
- CON: 4D6+15 (avg ~30+)
- SIZ: 5D6+20 (avg ~40+)
- DEX: 3D6+6 (avg ~16)
- POW: 4D6+12 (avg ~26+)

**Combat**:
- Hit Points: 50+ (varies by age and type)
- Strike Rank: 0-2 (very fast)
- Natural Armor: 6+ points (scales)
- Attacks: Bite (100%, 4D6+damage bonus), claws (75%, 2D6+damage bonus), tail (60%, 3D6+damage bonus), breath weapon (varies by type)

**Special**: Ancient dragons possess intelligence and magical knowledge; nearly unkillable through normal means

### Spirits

Spirits inhabit the Spirit World and manifest only under specific conditions:

**Types**:
- **Guardian Spirits**: Bound to locations; protect sacred sites
- **Ancestral Spirits**: Departed individuals; may aid or hinder living descendants
- **Elemental Spirits**: Pure manifestations of elements (weaker than elementals)

**Combat**: Spirit Combat skill required; physical weapons ineffective

### Undead

Undead are corrupted versions of living creatures:

**Zombies**: Reanimated corpses; slow, but difficult to kill; may require decapitation

**Skeletons**: Animated bones; fragile but persistent; can be "killed" by dispersing bones

**Ghosts**: Spirit form; can only be harmed by spiritual magic or rune magic

## 7. Encounter Guidelines

### Creature Difficulty

| Challenge Level | Typical Opponents | Recommended Party Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Trivial** | Unarmored bandits, small animals (rats, snakes) | Any |
| **Easy** | Armed bandits, large animals (wolves, boars) | 2-3 adventures |
| **Moderate** | Warriors, organized groups of enemies | 3-4 adventures |
| **Hard** | Skilled warriors, magical opponents, unique creatures | 4+ adventures |
| **Deadly** | Dragons, chaos creatures, god-touched beings | 5+ adventures or high-level party |

### Scaling Encounters

- **Single Strong Opponent**: Add +20-30% to all skills and HP
- **Mob Tactics**: Multiple weaker opponents; use coordinated maneuvers (+20% to group attacks)
- **Environmental Advantage**: Add bonuses if creatures have favorable terrain/positioning

## 8. Monster Creation Examples

### Creating a Troll Warrior

**Characteristics**:
- STR 19, CON 16, SIZ 19, DEX 7, INT 7, POW 10, CHA 7

**Derived Stats**:
- Hit Points: (16+19)/2 = 17 (rounded)
- Damage Bonus: (19+19)/2 = 19 → 1D10+1D4 (over 30)
- Strike Rank: getDexterityModifier(7) + getSizeModifier(19) = 5+1 = 6

**Combat**:
- Weapon: Battle Axe (1D8+2 + damage bonus) — Attack 50%
- Armor: Hide armor (2 points) + shield (4 points, covers left arm + chest)

**Hit Locations**: Standard humanoid (7 locations)
