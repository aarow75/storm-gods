# VII. RUNE MAGIC

## Overview

Rune Magic is the most powerful form of magic in RuneQuest, accessed only through cult membership. Rune Spells are granted by the gods themselves and are twice as potent as Spirit Magic spells of equivalent cost. This section covers Runes, cult membership, Rune Magic mechanics, and the path to becoming a Rune Lord or Rune Priest.

---

## 1. Mastering the Runes

### What Are Runes?

Runes are mystical symbols representing fundamental forces in Glorantha:

- **Elemental Runes** — The building blocks of physical reality
- **Power Runes** — Abstract forces affecting society and magic
- **Form Runes** — Physical manifestations in material form

### The Three Rune Types

#### Elemental Runes (Six)

| Rune | Represents | Opposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Air** | Wind, sky, upper atmosphere, weather | Earth |
| **Earth** | Soil, stone, stability, foundation | Air |
| **Fire** | Heat, passion, destruction, purification | Water |
| **Water** | Liquid, fluidity, change, healing | Fire |
| **Moon** | Night, cycles, magic, illusions | Darkness |
| **Darkness** | Void, secrets, underworld, death | Moon |

#### Power Runes (Eight)

| Rune | Represents | Opposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Death** | Ending, finality, separation, combat | Fertility |
| **Fertility** | Growth, birth, abundance, continuity | Death |
| **Harmony** | Balance, order, law, cooperation | Disorder |
| **Disorder** | Chaos, freedom, passion, individualism | Harmony |
| **Truth** | Honesty, reality, clarity, knowledge | Illusion |
| **Illusion** | Deception, appearance, magic, misdirection | Truth |
| **Stasis** | Preservation, stillness, unchanging | Movement |
| **Movement** | Change, progress, journey, action | Stasis |

#### Form Runes (Three)

| Rune | Represents |
|---|---|
| **Man** | Humanoid form; civilization, culture, society |
| **Beast** | Animal form; instinct, nature, physical power |
| **Plant** | Flora; growth, interconnection, natural order |

### Rune Affinities

Each adventurer has an **affinity percentage** (0–100%) for each rune:

- **0–20%**: Weak affinity; spells are difficult to cast
- **21–50%**: Moderate affinity; normal casting chance
- **51–75%**: Strong affinity; casting is reliable
- **76–100%**: Master affinity; casting is second nature

Rune affinities are determined during character creation based on the chosen characteristic set and cult membership.

### Opposed Runes

Certain runes oppose each other. An adventurer cannot have high affinity in both opposed runes simultaneously:

**Elemental Opposition**:
- Air ↔ Earth
- Fire ↔ Water
- Moon ↔ Darkness

**Power Opposition**:
- Death ↔ Fertility
- Harmony ↔ Disorder
- Truth ↔ Illusion
- Stasis ↔ Movement

If an adventurer tries to raise both opposed runes above a combined total of 100%, they are forced to choose: the one with lower skill is reduced proportionally.

## 2. Rune Cults

### What Is a Cult?

A cult is an organized religious following centered on a deity (god). All RuneQuest adventurers must select a cult during character creation. Cults provide:

1. **Rune affinity bonuses** — Predetermined rune percentages
2. **Rune spells** — Access to cult-specific magic
3. **Skills** — Occupation and cult-specific skill bonuses
4. **Passions** — Emotional drivers tied to cult values
5. **Community** — Social identity and support network

### Sample Major Cults

#### Orlanth (God of Storm and Wind)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Air: 60% (primary)
- Movement: 50% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Wind Words, Lightning, Cloudform, etc.

**Cult Values**: Leadership, heroic deeds, protection of family, warrior code

**Skill Bonuses**: +15% Rune Magic, +10% Dodge, +10% Ride

**Passions**: Loyalty (Clan), Loyalty (Temple), Hate (Lunar Empire)

#### Ernalda (Goddess of Earth and Fertility)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Earth: 60% (primary)
- Fertility: 50% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Bless Crops, Earth Touch, Heal Body, etc.

**Cult Values**: Nurturing, community, agriculture, motherhood

**Skill Bonuses**: +15% Rune Magic, +15% Farm, +10% Lore (Plant)

**Passions**: Love (Family), Loyalty (Tribe), Devotion (Ernalda)

#### Humakt (God of War and Death)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Death: 70% (primary)
- Movement: 40% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Sword, Protect, Sever Spirit, etc.

**Cult Values**: Martial prowess, honor, death in battle, truth

**Skill Bonuses**: +20% Sword & Shield, +15% Rune Magic

**Passions**: Loyalty (Humakt), Honor, Hate (Chaos)

**Special**: Members accept a geas (binding oath) from Humakt—often "Never Lie" or similar constraint.

#### Chalana Arroy (Healing Goddess)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Fertility: 50% (primary)
- Harmony: 50% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Heal, Resurrection, Disease Cure, etc.

**Cult Values**: Compassion, healing, peace, acceptance

**Skill Bonuses**: +20% Heal, +15% Rune Magic

**Passions**: Compassion, Devotion (Chalana Arroy)

**Special**: Healing cult members gain +20% to healing-related spells.

#### Issaries (God of Trade)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Movement: 60% (primary)
- Harmony: 40% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Roads, Blessing, Chameleon, etc.

**Cult Values**: Commerce, travel, neutrality, peace-making

**Skill Bonuses**: +20% Bargain, +15% Speak (Other), +10% Navigation

**Passions**: Loyalty (Merchant), Hospitality

#### Kyger Litor (Troll Dark Mother)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Darkness: 70% (primary)
- Earth: 50% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Shadow, Darkness, Terror, etc.

**Cult Values**: Survival, cunning, tribal loyalty, skepticism of outsiders

**Skill Bonuses**: +15% Rune Magic, +15% Hide, +10% Stealth

**Passions**: Loyalty (Tribe), Caution (Sunlight)

**Note**: Kyger Litor cult members are typically trolls, though humanoids can join.

#### Seven Mothers (Lunar Magic)

**Affinity Runes**:
- Moon: 60% (primary)
- Illusion: 50% (secondary)

**Associated Rune Spells**: Illusion, Reflection, Healing (Lunar variant), etc.

**Cult Values**: Change, magic, civilization under Lunar rule, transformation

**Skill Bonuses**: +15% Rune Magic, +20% Read/Write, +10% Sorcery

**Passions**: Devotion (Lunar Empire), Loyalty (Sartar or Tarsh)

**Note**: Most common in Lunar-controlled territories.

### Other Sample Cults

**Storm Bull** (War and Chaos Destroyer)
- Primary: Movement (60%), Death (50%)
- Bonus: +20% Unarmed, +15% Rune Magic
- Passion: Hate (Chaos)

**Babeester Gor** (Warrior Maiden, Ernalda's Daughter)
- Primary: Death (60%), Movement (50%)
- Bonus: +20% Sword & Shield, +15% Rune Magic
- Requirement: Female-only initiate

**Lhankor Mhy** (God of Knowledge)
- Primary: Truth (70%), Harmony (40%)
- Bonus: +20% Lore (any), +15% Rune Magic, +10% Read/Write
- Values: Knowledge, truth, learning

**Waha** (Desert God)
- Primary: Movement (60%), Stasis (30%)
- Bonus: +20% Ride, +15% Lore (Animal), +10% Survival
- Values: Desert traditions, nomadic life

**Daka Fal** (Ancestral Cult)
- Primary: Stasis (50%), Death (50%)
- Bonus: +15% Rune Magic, +15% Spirit Combat, +10% Lore (World)
- Values: Ancestors, tradition, death rites

## 3. Becoming a Rune Lord

### What Is a Rune Lord?

A Rune Lord is a warrior-priest who serves a god through martial prowess and magic. Rune Lords:
- Are elite warriors with combat training
- Have strong combat and weapon skills
- Wield Rune Magic in battle
- Are expected to defend the cult and its interests
- Often become leaders of adventuring groups

### Requirements to Become a Rune Lord

1. **Cult Membership** — Must be an initiated cult member
2. **Combat Skill** — Minimum 50% in a weapon skill (any)
3. **Rune Affinity** — At least 50% in one of the cult's primary runes
4. **Quest or Trial** — Perform a significant deed for the cult (GM determines)
5. **POW Sacrifice** — Permanently reduce POW by 2–4 points to bind to the god
6. **Holy Ritual** — Undergo a sacred ritual granting Rune Lord status

### Rune Lord Powers

Once a Rune Lord:

| Power | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Rune Point Allocation** | Gain 4–6 Rune Points (instead of 3) |
| **Rune Spell Access** | Access all cult Rune spells (not just starting spells) |
| **Divine Favor** | In emergencies, can request god's intervention (rare; GM discretion) |
| **Cult Leadership** | Eligible to lead cult temple or adventuring party |
| **Increased Magic** | Rune spells cast at +20% bonus to success chance |

### Rune Lord Geases

Rune Lords often accept additional geases from their god:

**Example Geases**:
- "Always protect clan members" — Must aid clan in danger
- "Never retreat from battle" — Cannot flee; must stand and fight
- "Never lie" — Bound to absolute honesty
- "Defend the sacred sites" — Must protect cult temples and holy places
- "Tithe to the temple" — Must give 10% of wealth to the cult

Breaking a geas results in loss of magical access and potential divine punishment.

## 4. Becoming a Rune Priest

### What Is a Rune Priest?

A Rune Priest is a full-time religious leader who serves their god as intermediary between the cult and its members. Rune Priests:
- Lead temple ceremonies and rituals
- Have extensive magical knowledge
- Are spiritual advisors to the cult
- Are typically non-combatant (though trained warriors can be both Lord and Priest)
- Hold authority over lower-ranking cult members

### Requirements to Become a Rune Priest

1. **Cult Leadership** — Usually starts as Rune Lord or extended initiate
2. **Divine Calling** — The god must accept the individual (communicated through signs)
3. **Knowledge** — Study cult doctrines (typically 1–2 years)
4. **Ritual Mastery** — Master sacred rituals and ceremonies
5. **POW Sacrifice** — Permanently reduce POW by 4–6 points (more than Rune Lord)
6. **Sacred Oath** — Accept absolute devotion to the god

### Rune Priest Powers

Once a Rune Priest:

| Power | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Rune Point Allocation** | Gain 6–10 Rune Points (highest allocation) |
| **Full Rune Spell Access** | Access all cult spells including secret/forbidden spells |
| **Spell Creation** | Can create new Rune spells (with GM approval) |
| **Divine Authority** | Speak with god's voice during rituals; gain cult authority |
| **Ritual Power** | Conduct ceremonies affecting multiple people (healing, blessing, etc.) |
| **Holy Days** | Lead worship ceremonies to restore cult members' Rune Points |

### Rune Priest vs. Rune Lord

| Aspect | Rune Lord | Rune Priest |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Role** | Warrior | Religious leader |
| **Combat Ability** | High | Variable |
| **Rune Points** | 4–6 | 6–10 |
| **Spell Access** | Cult spells | All cult spells + secrets |
| **Authority** | Cult military commander | Cult spiritual leader |
| **POW Cost** | 2–4 permanent | 4–6 permanent |

## 5. Rune Magic Mechanics

### How Rune Magic Works

#### Base Chance

**Rune Spell Base Chance** = Relevant Rune affinity (0–100%)

Example: Air Rune affinity 60% → 60% base chance to cast Air rune spells

#### Rune Points

Rune Points (RP) are magical resources specific to Rune Magic:

- **Starting allocation**: 3 RP (or more if Rune Lord/Priest)
- **Cost to cast**: 1–3+ RP per spell (varies by spell)
- **Replenishment**: Only on Holy Days (cult worship ceremonies)
- **Sacrifice**: Permanent POW reduction to gain RP (rare; conversion not recommended)

#### Casting a Rune Spell

1. **Announce intent** to cast the rune spell
2. **Spend Rune Points** required by the spell
3. **Roll D100 vs. Rune affinity**
4. **Success** (roll ≤ affinity): Spell takes effect immediately
5. **Failure** (roll > affinity): Spell doesn't work; Rune Points still spent

**Important**: Rune Points are spent regardless of success or failure.

#### Rune Spell Duration

Most Rune Spells are:
- **Instant** — Effect happens immediately and is permanent (e.g., healing)
- **Sustained** — Effect lasts as long as caster concentrates (uses no additional RP but prevents other actions)
- **Ritual** — Requires ceremony lasting hours/days; typically grants permanent or long-lasting effects

#### Strike Rank

Casting a Rune Spell always costs **SR 1** in combat (very fast).

### Rune Spell Characteristics

Unlike Spirit Magic, Rune Spells:
- Are **twice as potent** (if spirit magic equivalent exists, rune spell is 2× stronger)
- Cannot be cast while other Rune/Spirit magic is active (with rare exceptions: Extension, Illusion runes)
- Are **permanently bound** to the caster's god (cannot use rune spells from multiple cults without massive penalty)
- Cannot be taught by one cult to members of another

## 6. Rune Magic Spell Descriptions

### Spell Organization

Rune Spells are organized by **associated rune**. Each spell is tied to one or more runes, and the caster's affinity in that rune determines the base chance to cast.

### Sample Rune Spells

#### Air Rune Spells

| Spell | Cost | Duration | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wind Words** | 1 RP | Sustained | Communicate across distance through wind; target can hear your voice |
| **Lightning** | 2 RP | Instant | Strike with lightning bolt; 2D6 damage (ignores normal armor) |
| **Cloudform** | 2 RP | 2 hours | Caster transforms into cloud; can move freely, invisible |
| **Thunderbolt** | 3 RP | Instant | Call down lightning; 3D6 damage in area; resistible |

#### Earth Rune Spells

| Spell | Cost | Duration | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bless Crops** | 1 RP | 1 season | Double fertility/yield of crops in blessed field |
| **Stone Skin** | 2 RP | 1 hour | Caster's skin becomes stone-hard; +4 armor |
| **Earth Touch** | 1 RP | Sustained | Read information encoded in earth/stone |
| **Earthquake** | 3 RP | Instant | Shake ground; damage structures and creatures |

#### Death Rune Spells

| Spell | Cost | Duration | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sword** | 1 RP | 2 hours | Magical weapon appears; caster wields it expertly |
| **Sever Spirit** | 3 RP | Instant | Break magical bonds; sever spirit from body (kills spirit) |
| **Deathblow** | 2 RP | 1 round | Weapon strike deals fatal wound (must succeed in combat) |
| **Holy Sacrifice** | Variable RP | Instant | Sacrifice creature's life for powerful magical effect |

#### Other Rune Spells

## 7. Elementals

### What Are Elementals?

Elementals are spirits of pure elemental energy. They can be:
- **Bound into form** (summoned and controlled)
- **Encountered in the wild** (in their native environments)
- **Destroyed** (dispersed back to their element)

### Summoning Elementals

Some Rune Spells allow summoning elementals:

| Elemental | Summoning Requirement | Cost | Duration | Ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Air Elemental** | Air Rune 50%+ | 2–4 RP | 1 hour or sustained | Fly, move swiftly, weather control |
| **Earth Elemental** | Earth Rune 50%+ | 2–4 RP | 1 hour or sustained | Move through earth, tremor attack, strength |
| **Fire Elemental** | Fire Rune 50%+ | 2–4 RP | 1 hour or sustained | Burn, move through flames, heat control |
| **Water Elemental** | Water Rune 50%+ | 2–4 RP | 1 hour or sustained | Swim, drown attack, water control |

### Elemental Control

Once summoned, the elemental:
- Obeys the caster's commands (telepathically)
- Acts independently if given broad orders
- Can be dismissed early (no RP refund)
- Becomes uncontrolled if caster is incapacitated

## Summary

**Key Takeaways**:
- Rune Magic is twice as potent as Spirit Magic
- Rune Spells cost Rune Points (replenished on Holy Days)
- Base casting chance = Rune affinity percentage
- Opposed runes cannot both be high (air/earth, fire/water, etc.)
- Rune Lords are warrior-priests; Rune Priests are spiritual leaders
- POW sacrifices are permanent and reduce the character's Power stat
- Rune Spells always cost SR 1 in combat (very fast)
