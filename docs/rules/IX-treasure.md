# IX. TREASURE HOARDS

## Overview

Treasure in RuneQuest encompasses both mundane equipment and magical items. This section covers currency, equipment costs, treasure distribution, and special items of power.

---

## 1. Currency and Economy

### Gloranthan Currency

The primary unit of currency in Glorantha is the **Lunar** (L), a silver coin.

**Currency Denominations**:

| Denomination | Value | Common Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Copper** (C) | 1/100 Lunar | Daily wages, minor purchases |
| **Silver** (S) | 1/10 Lunar | Tools, weapons, moderate expenses |
| **Lunar** (L) | 1 Lunar | Standard currency for significant purchases |
| **Gold** (AU) | 10 Lunars | Rare; used for very valuable items or major transactions |

**Exchange Rates**:
- 1 Lunar = 10 Silver = 100 Copper
- 1 Gold = 10 Lunars = 100 Silver = 1,000 Copper

### Cost of Living

Adventurers must pay for lodging, food, and maintenance depending on their lifestyle:

| Standard | Cost/Season | Cost/Week | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Destitute** | Free | Free | Sleep in streets/wilderness, no repairs |
| **Poor** | 3 Lunars | 0.4 L | Minimal accommodations, poor food |
| **Free** | 12 Lunars | 1.5 L | Clean, secure home, adequate food |
| **Noble** | 40 Lunars | 5 L | Estates, servants, fine accommodations |

**Note**: A "season" is 8 weeks in game time. Costs can be divided weekly for shorter stays.

## 3. Treasure Distribution

### Typical Treasure by Encounter

**Defeated Bandits/Raiders**:
- Coins: 1D6 × 10 Lunars
- Weapons: 1–2 (average quality)
- Armor: Light or medium pieces
- Personal items: 0–1 (minimal value)

**Defeated Warriors**:
- Coins: 1D6 × 20 Lunars
- Weapons: 1–2 (good quality)
- Armor: Matching set
- Valuable items: 1–3 (rings, belts, talismans)

**Defeated Nobles/Priests**:
- Coins: 1D6 × 50 Lunars
- Weapons: 1–2 (fine or magical)
- Armor: Excellent quality or magically enhanced
- Jewelry: 2–5 (significant value)
- Documents: Letters, maps, contracts

**Treasure Hoard (Dragons, Ancient Sites)**:
- Coins: 1D10 × 100+ Lunars
- Weapons: 1D4 (often magical)
- Armor: 1D4 (often magical)
- Jewelry: 1D10 × 10+ Lunars value
- Magical items: 1D6 (varies in power)
- Artifacts: 0–1 (unique, powerful)

## 4. Special Items

### Magical Equipment

#### Spell Matrices

A **spell matrix** is an enchanted object containing a prepared spell:

**Types**:
- **Spirit Magic Matrix**: Contains a spirit magic spell
- **Rune Magic Matrix**: Contains a rune magic spell

**Cost**: Variable based on spell (typically 50–500 Lunars for common spells)

**Function**: 
- Non-casters can use matrices at POW×5 base chance
- Casters can access spells without needing training
- Matrices recharge through use or ritual (GM discretion)

**Limitations**:
- Limited number of uses before recharge needed
- Cannot be taught; only used as provided

#### Enchanted Weapons

Weapons can be magically enhanced:

**Enhancement Types**:
- **+1 to hit**: Weapon grants +1 to attack skill (cost: 100–200 L)
- **+1D4 damage**: Weapon deals extra damage (cost: 150–300 L)
- **Magical property**: Weapon affects spirits, ignores certain armor (cost: 200+ L)
- **Artifact quality**: Legendary weapons with multiple properties (cost: 500+ L or quest-reward only)

#### Enchanted Armor

Armor can be magically enhanced:

**Enhancement Types**:
- **+1 armor**: Provides extra protection point (cost: 100–200 L)
- **Magic resistance**: Provides resistance to spells (cost: 200+ L)
- **Light as feather**: Reduces encumbrance (cost: 300+ L)
- **Artifact quality**: Legendary armor with multiple properties (cost: 500+ L or quest-reward only)

#### Protective Amulets

Amulets and talismans grant minor protection:

**Types**:
- **Protection amulet**: Grants +1 armor or +10% to dodge (cost: 50–100 L)
- **Warding**: Protects against specific threat (disease, curse, magic) (cost: 100–300 L)
- **Charm**: Grants +10% to a specific skill (cost: 75–150 L)

#### Healing Items

**Healing Salves**:
- **Minor salve**: Heals 1D3 HP (cost: 10 L)
- **Greater salve**: Heals 1D6 HP (cost: 25 L)
- **Master salve**: Heals 2D6 HP (cost: 75 L)

**Potions**:
- **Antidote**: Cures poison (cost: 15 L)
- **Cure disease**: Removes disease (cost: 30 L)
- **Restoration**: Restores characteristic lost to curse/poison (cost: 100+ L)

## 6. Artifact Examples

### Legendary Items

**Holy Artifacts**:
- **Holy Sword of [Cult]**: Weapon +2 to hit, +1D6 damage, spirits cannot touch
- **Sacred Shield**: +2 armor, grants spiritual protection
- **Artifact Armor**: +3 armor, magical enhancement, legendary craftsmanship

**Magical Treasures**:
- **Spell Matrix (Powerful Rune)**: Single-use spell (e.g., Resurrection, Thunderbolt)
- **Crystalline Focus**: Enhances all magic casting by +20%; stores magic points
- **Immortal's Ring**: Grants +2 to one characteristic or ability

**Unique Items**:
- **Dragonbone weapons**: Extremely durable, enchanted by dragon fire
- **Elven craft**: Impossibly fine workmanship; +1 armor or +10% skill
- **Dwarven craft**: Masterwork quality; affects value and function

## Summary

**Key Takeaways**:
- Currency: Lunars (L), Silvers (S), Copper (C)
- Equipment costs: 1–300 L for common items
- Magical items add significant value
- Cost of living varies by lifestyle: 0–40 L per season
- Artifacts are rare, powerful, and often tied to quests
- Trade goods can replace coin in some regions
