# V. BASIC MAGIC

## Overview

RuneQuest features two primary forms of magic available to common adventurers: **Spirit Magic** (also called Battle Magic) and **Spirit Combat** (astral combat). This section covers both, as well as shamans who specialize in spirit magic.

---

## 1. Battle Magic (Spirit Magic)

### What Is Spirit Magic?

Spirit Magic is the casting of spells powered by **Magic Points**, representing personal magical energy. These spells are:
- Temporary in effect (typically lasting 2 minutes unless instant)
- Provided through cults or learned through study
- Fueled by the caster's POW stat
- Available to any adventurer, regardless of cult membership (some cults provide free access)

### Casting Spirit Magic

#### Base Chance

**Spirit Magic Base Chance** = POW × 5

Example: POW 12 → Base 60% chance to cast

#### Learning Spirit Magic Spells

Spells can be learned from:
1. **Cult membership** — Specific cults grant knowledge of cult-approved spells
2. **Shamans** — Can teach any spirit magic spell
3. **Other practitioners** — Adventurers with 50%+ skill in a spell can teach

A new learner starts at **base chance (POW×5)** when learning a spell and improves through practice.

#### Casting a Spell

1. **Announce intention** to cast the spell
2. **Spend magic points** required by the spell (before rolling)
3. **Roll D100 vs. spell skill rating**
4. **Success** (roll ≤ spell rating): Spell takes effect
5. **Failure** (roll > spell rating): Magic points still spent; spell doesn't work

**Important**: Magic points are spent whether the spell succeeds or fails.

#### Magic Point Recovery

Magic points recover at:
- **During rest**: 1 point per 6 hours of sleep
- **During meditation**: Up to +25% bonus to spell casting if 50 melee rounds (10 minutes) spent concentrating
- **Through ritual**: Can recover up to +50% casting bonus if 1 full day spent in ritual preparation

Example: Caster with 15 MP recovers ~2-3 per 6-hour rest period.

### Spirit Magic Spells

#### Cost and Duration

| Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | Magic points spent to cast (before rolling) |
| **Duration** | How long the effect persists (instant = immediate effect; 2 minutes = standard duration) |
| **Range** | Touch (must be adjacent) or 50m line of sight |
| **Resistance** | Some spells allow targets to resist (oppose caster's POW×5 vs. target's POW×5) |

#### Common Spirit Magic Spells

| Spell | Cost | Duration | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bladesharp** | 1–3 | 2 minutes | Buff | Increases weapon skill by +5% per point |
| **Countermagic** | 2 | 2 minutes | Defense | Blocks incoming magical effects |
| **Detect (substance)** | 1–2 | 2 minutes | Detection | Sense nearby presence of named substance |
| **Disruption** | 3 | Instant | Offense | Damages target's spirit; 1D6 damage per point |
| **Extinguish** | 1–3 | Instant | Control | Puts out fires based on points spent |
| **Fanaticism** | 1–2 | 2 minutes | Buff | +10% to weapon skills; +2 damage per point |
| **Firearrow** | 1–3 | Instant | Offense | Projectile spell; 1D6 damage per point |

| **Glamour** | 1–2 | 2 minutes | Illusion | Makes caster appear different; –20% to target perception |
| **Heal** | 2–4 | Instant | Healing | Restores 1D3 hit points per point (capped at max per location) |
| **Ignite** | 1–2 | Instant | Control | Starts fires; damage based on points spent |

#### Incompatible Spells

Some spells cannot be active simultaneously:
- **Bladesharp** and **Fireblade** (both modify weapon damage)
- **Shimmer** and **Visibility** (both modify visibility)

If a caster attempts to cast an incompatible spell while another is active, the older spell immediately ends.

### Resistance to Spirit Magic

When a spell targets an unwilling opponent:

1. **Caster rolls** POW×5 vs. spell skill
2. **Target rolls** POW×5 vs. their resistance
3. **Compare results**:
   - Caster critical success → Always affects target
   - Caster special success → Beats target's normal success
   - Both normal success → Caster wins (spell takes effect)
   - Caster failure → Spell doesn't affect target

## 2. Spirit Combat

### What Is Spirit Combat?

Spirit Combat occurs when spirits or astral-projecting adventurers fight in the **Spirit World** (also called the Astral Plane). Spirit Combat is not physical—it occurs in a non-physical realm.

### How to Enter Spirit Combat

1. **Use a spirit magic spell** that allows astral projection
2. **Engage with a spirit** that attacks your spirit
3. **Shamans** are trained in spirit combat from the beginning

### Spirit Combat Mechanics

#### Initiating Combat

1. Caster's spirit meets opponent's spirit
2. Both roll **Spirit Combat skill**
3. Opposition determined (no hit locations)
4. Combat is resolved **per round** (12 SR slots as in physical combat)

#### Spirit Combat Damage

**Damage in Spirit Combat** = POW value (not rolled, direct value)

Example: POW 14 means each hit in spirit combat deals 14 damage (not 1D6 or equivalent—the actual POW stat).

#### The Astral Plane

In spirit combat:
- **No armor** — Physical protection doesn't apply
- **No weapons** — Combat is direct spiritual conflict
- **Distance irrelevant** — One can fight across great distances in the spirit world
- **Time slower** — 1 spirit combat "round" = multiple physical rounds (GM discretion)

#### Ending Spirit Combat

Spirit combat ends when:
1. One participant is reduced to 0 HP (spirit is defeated/banished)
2. One participant chooses to disengage and escape
3. The spell maintaining the astral link is broken

**Defeat in spirit combat** does not cause physical death; the defeated spirit is returned to their body (or banished, if a non-player spirit).

## 3. Shamans

### What Is a Shaman?

A Shaman is an adventurer specialized in spirit magic and spirit combat. Shamans:
- Have higher base Spirit Magic skill (typically POW×5 + 20% bonus)
- Are trained in Spirit Combat from beginning (not requiring special spells)
- Can communicate with spirits
- Are often affiliated with nature-based cults or independent spiritual traditions

### Shaman Abilities

| Ability | Requirement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirit Combat Initiation** | Shaman class or training | Can enter spirit combat without needing a spell |
| **Spirit Communication** | Shaman training | Can ask spirits questions; negotiate with spirits |
| **Spirit Binding** | Advanced spell (cost varies) | Can bind spirits into objects or people temporarily |
| **Shamanic Journey** | Training + magic points | Project astral form into the spirit world to gather information |

### Learning Shamanism

Shamanism is learned through:
1. **Cultural training** — Growing up in a culture that practices shamanism
2. **Apprenticeship** — Spending years under a trained shaman mentor
3. **Spiritual calling** — Some adventurers are chosen by spirits to become shamans

Once trained, shamans gain:
- +20% to all spirit magic casting
- Ability to enter spirit combat without spell support
- Increased magic point recovery (through meditation/ritual)

## 4. Magic System Mechanics

### Opposed Casting

When two casters' spells oppose each other:

1. **Both cast their spells** (roll D100 vs. respective spell skills)
2. **Compare success levels**:
   - Critical beats Special beats Normal beats Failure beats Fumble
   - If same success level, highest roll wins
   - Tied rolls = Simultaneous effect (both spells may work partially)

Example: Caster A casts "Protection" (succeeds normally), Caster B casts "Dispel Magic" (special success). Dispel Magic wins and removes the Protection.

### Spell Augmentation

A spell can be "augmented" by spending extra magic points for increased effect:

| Augmentation | Effect |
|---|---|
| +1 point | 20% increased effect (e.g., +1 more to Bladesharp bonus) |
| +2 points | 40% increased effect |
| +3 points | 60% increased effect |

Augmentation is declared before rolling to cast.

### Spell Matrices

A **spell matrix** is a magical container (trinket, tattoo, amulet) that stores a spell. Spell matrices allow:
- Non-casters to cast stored spells at POW×5 base chance
- Casting without needing the original skill
- Multiple uses before matrix "exhausts" (GM discretion on recharging)

---

## Summary

**Key Takeaways**:
- Spirit Magic costs magic points (spent regardless of success)
- Base casting chance = POW × 5
- Spirit magic typically lasts 2 minutes unless instant
- Spirit Combat uses POW value directly for damage
- Shamans specialize in spirit interaction and combat
- Some spells are incompatible and cancel each other
- Magical resistance is opposed POW×5 vs. caster's spell skill
